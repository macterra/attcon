# Independent reference grounding: development 1

2026-10-10. Primary-literature basis for candidate report distinctions, reviewed
before new integrated-task reports. This is not a transcript corpus, a completed
reference validation, human reviewer endorsement or a confirmation endpoint.
The parent selected these sources with knowledge of prior project development;
the publications themselves were produced independently of this project. Future
reference-feature review must disclose that selection and exposure. AI reviewers
can perform that review without recruiting humans.

## What external evidence supports

[Tagliabue et al. (2016)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full)
collected visual discrimination and a separate graded rating of perceptual clarity.
The procedure distinguishes judgments about appearance from answer accuracy and
asks participants about their scale criteria afterward. This supports separating
content, clarity and decision confidence in the rubric. It does not validate a
conversion of simulated recovery probability into felt clarity. The published
scale categories are constrained report options, not unrestricted prose transcripts.

[Liu, Abrams and Carrasco (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19254239/)
manipulated voluntary spatial attention with a separate task and measured perceived
contrast at attended/unattended locations. They reported increased perceived
contrast and considered response bias. This motivates a content-specific
attention/appearance relation as a correspondence candidate. Our allocation and
recovery fields are not measurements of perceived contrast, so an accurate recovery
forecast cannot automatically receive appearance credit. Nor does this behavioral
finding identify an attention-control model as the source of qualia.

[Anton-Erxleben, Abrams and Carrasco (2010)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/3156576/)
compared comparative and equality judgments and discussed their sensitivity and
biases in studying apparent contrast. A subsequent
[reanalysis (2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22072727/)
argued that the observations were compatible with attention changing decision
criteria rather than appearance. That disagreement motivates retaining ordinary
decision/description alternatives and distinguishing what the text says from what
its cause establishes. This project does not resolve that psychophysical dispute.

## Prospective mapping and limits

| Reference distinction | Candidate text feature | Current model support and scoring limit |
|---|---|---|
| Identified perceptual content | Object-linked attributes with modality/scope | Bound category distributions support model-content claims, not observed world truth by default. |
| Graded quality of appearance | Content-specific presentation manner | Recovery forecasts alone support availability, not felt clarity; score an actual presentation claim separately and audit unsupported inferences. |
| Attention linked to appearance | Selection versus prominence, and a stated content/access relation | Current command/recovery relations support functional forecasts. Similarity to appearance reports is a hypothesis, not a synonymous field name. |
| Appearance versus decision judgment | Separate appearance, attribute certainty, answer confidence and emitted response | A cutoff/abstention or high recovery number cannot supply all four. |

This mapping is a project hypothesis informed by external studies. It leaves
temporal availability and causal attribution as structural/source measures unless
further reference evidence justifies their role in a consciousness-report pattern.
Do not award a consciousness primary for their mere presence or name a recovery
band a phenomenal scale.

The next reference review should independently assess these mappings, specify
positive/negative text examples with provenance, and measure overlap with
information-matched apparatus descriptions. AI-authored examples must be labelled
synthetic and kept out of Reporter prompts/training. Existing scale labels cannot
be treated as a large sample of independently produced first-person reports.
Fix the candidate pattern and reliability/acceptance rules before fresh target
evaluation. Grounding, operational correspondence and optional superiority over
ordinary-description controls remain separate outcomes. No numerical success
threshold or power calculation is certified here.
