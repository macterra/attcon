## Additional architectural and theoretical limits

R-C1 suggests a native reporter and cites Attention Schema Theory as grounding.
The Reporter remains separate diagnostic instrumentation; a native reporter is
an optional comparison, not a new requirement or the experiencing subsystem.
Controller use of the attention model is demonstrated by the executed task assay,
but neither that use nor architectural location alone establishes qualia.

The project objective remains the attention-control-model source-of-qualia theory.
Do not replace it automatically with Graziano's theory or the full MSTC comparator
architecture. The user-referenced [MSTC paper, v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01073v5)
locates its proposed phenomenal subject in the Modeler-schema and distinguishes
Controller/Targeter roles. That related formulation does not derive the current
own/external contrast. All current controls contain attention models; an ordinary
causal-description account remains viable when a reporter infers their wiring.

Faithful description is judged against the supplied internal state. Retain physical
prediction accuracy separately for causal interpretation; a physically wrong model
can be reported accurately. Do not discard either measurement or substitute world
truth for reporter-visible model state.
