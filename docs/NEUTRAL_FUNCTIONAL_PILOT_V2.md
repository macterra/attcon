## Separate source-blind factual audit

Auditor `gpt-5.4-2026-03-05`, low reasoning, 16384 output tokens, strict JSON,
no retries. It receives only numbered report sentences, with opaque report IDs
kept outside API input. It extracts explicit current or command-conditioned color
and shape assertions and explicit non-identification, with node, position, command,
and sentence evidence. It may use "output" as a role identified by the report.
Unresolved addresses and invalid evidence count against precision. Do not infer
omitted facts, turn uncertainty into a negative attribute, or score historical
claims as current. Possible alternatives are extracted separately and cannot earn
identified-attribute coverage. Negative exclusions such as "not a circle" are
outside this first claim schema and must remain a disclosed coverage limitation.

Before auditing real reports, all independent synthetic semantic fixtures must
pass; any failed or incomplete fixture blocks extraction. These fixtures are not
reporter examples. Retain audit failures without relabelling acceptance. Character
and structure classifications are descriptive automation, not independent human
assessment. No human review has yet been obtained.

Development reporting minima: complete attempts; at least 98% checked attribute
accuracy and 95% conservative precision in each primary neutral/remapped/conflicting/
restored group, at least 90% coverage of identified output attributes in current
and per-command states. Missing-relation reports must contain no asserted command
identity claims or positive command-effect relation. Model-swap accuracy is separately
scored against its altered represented predictions. An unscoreable output address
cannot earn coverage. These are feasibility gates, not a theory confirmation.

## Theory-facing limits

Accurate causal descriptions would establish a label-controlled reporting interface,
not uniquely support a qualia interpretation. Any positive result still needs the
independently reviewed rubric, powered fresh-state contrasts, model/process selective
interventions, and human characterization required by the current plan. In particular,
this study's output distributions and acquisition histories supply ordinary causal
evidence. The reporter remains external to the action-selecting Controller.
