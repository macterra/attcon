# Operational termination of the first capped rater

Decision dated 2026-10-19, before inspecting the first annotation labels or text.
This is a single operational termination of the original 8,192-token blind-rater
batch, prompted by provider token-cap metadata. It is not scientific futility
stopping or successful completion of the original full-attempt schedule.

The latest published checkpoint records 202 `max_output_tokens` incompletes among
282 rater outcomes. The same checkpoint records 272 completed and 57 incomplete
auditor responses. A separately registered and reviewed full-inventory resource
amendment already exists; its fictional, unscored acceptance fixture completed
with 8,688 output tokens and passed the frozen contract. No reference feature
labels, quote judgments, source effects or favorable outcomes determine this stop.

A separate-context methodological review recommends stopping the failing rater
while allowing the original auditor to continue. The review identifies this as
a departure from the protocol's intended first pass over all 1,104 positions:
no metadata-based early-stopping rule was registered at launch. It also identifies
the protocol's existing interrupted-batch disposition: no resume, no replacement
within this version, untouched positions remain missing.

This addendum explicitly supersedes the earlier operational intention that both
original batches would continue unchanged. The frozen protocol, resource-v2
registration, original runtime manifests, instructions, schemas, reference,
endpoints and scientific criteria remain preserved without alteration. This
addendum must be committed, pushed and verified before the stop signal.

## Single stop and preservation

Identify and verify the original rater child process from its exact command,
manifest, output root and process identity. Send one interrupt to that child
only, leaving the original auditor running. Observe the actual original tool
handle to establish termination; a state file or observation timeout alone is
insufficient. Preserve the original execution log and receipt, all raw first
outcomes and all reservations. Do not fabricate a normal batch-completion record.

The runner has no stop-and-drain mechanism. Up to six in-flight calls can have
reservations without returned outcomes when interrupted. Their provider results
may therefore be unknown and must not be described as retained. Record a complete
ordered census distinguishing returned completed responses, returned capped
responses, other returned outcomes, claimed-without-outcome positions and
never-reserved positions. Retain all 1,104 scheduled positions. Missing or unknown
positions remain unestablished, never negative feature judgments. Preserve any
partial or ambiguous storage without repairing it.

The original rater cannot be restarted, resumed or selectively completed. Use
the existing frozen interrupted collector with exact request/manifest/lineage
bindings, after terminal evidence and raw files are preserved. This changes no
annotation or validator. The original primary summary must identify interruption
and keep all missingness and original denominators. Token exhaustion can correlate
with text complexity; the observed prefix is not an unbiased complete sample.

## Subsequent full measurement

The already reviewed resource-v2 requests, settings, roots and criteria remain
unchanged. Its full 537-audit and 1,104-rating inventories are not selected from
the original completed subset. Its summary remains supplementary, on the same
12 source cases: no pooling as independent cases, itemwise best-response selection,
failure replacement or promotion to primary. The original auditor still runs to
its first terminal outcome. Both originals must be authoritatively terminal and
their first evidence preserved before either resource-v2 batch starts.

This operational decision is intended to avoid hundreds of additional capped
measurements and reach the complete supplementary assessment sooner. It supplies
no consciousness-related result and does not change the full theory goal.
