# Sensory task adapter v1: pre-output development protocol

This is a new ancillary task-development study following the [failed isolated readout](ISOLATED_ORIENTATION_TASK_V1_RESULTS.md), whose artifacts and verdict remain immutable. It trains decisions across the intended sensory operating conditions and qualifies correctness probabilities conditionally. It is not a consciousness confirmation, native participant-stimulus validation or an appearance target. The full source-of-qualia and report-correspondence goal remains unchanged.

## Data and sensory sources

Use frozen visual checkpoints 1301/1311/1321. For each model generate 1,024 training bases, 256 calibration bases and 512 evaluation bases. Base nuisance seeds are `930000000 + visual_seed + 10000*split`, split 0/1/2; balanced shuffled orientation labels use an independent generator with nuisance seed plus 1,000,000. All seeds are distinct from prior diagnostic splits. Whole bases contain one orientation and six contrast variants; no counterfactual partner is used in primary prediction. Phase/aperture classes may recur across independently drawn bases; this is not a held-out sensory domain.

The unchanged isolated renderer produces four-cycle binary gratings in 16×16 RGB with random phase, circle/square mask, radius 3.7–5.2, center jitter 7.5±1 and independent Gaussian RGB noise SD .015 at each contrast. The six luminance pairs/background/divisor and synthetic/native mismatch remain as in the [isolated protocol](ISOLATED_ORIENTATION_TASK_V1_PROTOCOL.md). Labels do not determine geometry, phase or noise. No human ratings, attention variables, explicit contrast covariate or participant rows enter fitting or inference.

Extract the same five sources: visual hidden64, logits8, category probabilities8, category confidence4, physical directional edge1. All sources use identical training/calibration/evaluation bases, training steps, hidden width and bootstrap indices. Width/data/budget equality does not equal parameter count or equal information; archive each readout's count. The physical source is a sanity comparator, not a candidate experiencing subsystem.

## Task adapter and correctness calibration

Flatten all six training conditions, equally weighting every image. Compute training-only column mean and centered RMS deviation in float32; scales ≤1e-6 become 1. Fit `Linear(input_dim,32) → ReLU → Linear(32,1)` with binary cross-entropy against horizontal0/vertical1. Use standard PyTorch Linear initialization in an isolated RNG scope with seed `940000000 + visual_seed` for each source. Fixed full-batch Adam, 1,500 steps, learning rate .003, betas(.9,.999), epsilon1e-8, coupled weight decay .001 on every parameter, amsgrad false, foreach false. No early stopping, source selection, hyperparameter search or extension for poor convergence. Record initial/every500-step BCE and final BCE; nonfinite final BCE fails the reserved attempt. Frozen encoders are not trained. Predict vertical if final logit >0, horizontal otherwise.

Fit the previous two-parameter correctness logistic map on the separate calibration split only: absolute task logit standardized by calibration mean/SD (zero SD becomes 1), 100 fixed Newton steps, L2 .001 on both coefficients, maximum final gradient ≤1e-8. Archive coefficients/objective/gradient. Probability below .5 is permitted. The quantity is probability of this fixed classifier's answer being correct, separate from orientation posterior, acquired-image event, modeled recovery, participant confidence and clarity. Neither this adapter nor correctness calibrator becomes the Controller or attention model.

Apply the original frozen isolated-task readouts/calibrators to the new evaluation features as a descriptive legacy comparator, without refitting. Freeze their archive hashes. Their smaller highest-contrast fitting budget and linear architecture differ, so improvement cannot be attributed solely to sensory-condition coverage. Both old and new results remain visible; none is a theory result.

## Assessment and fixed gates

Use ten equal probability bins on [0,1]; internal boundaries enter the upper bin. Report pooled and each-contrast accuracy, mean predicted correctness, signed residual (prediction minus correctness), ECE, Brier and bin counts/means. Also report each contrast's true-orientation and predicted-orientation strata; empty predicted strata have count0. These strata are diagnostics, not predictors or post-hoc selection criteria.

Primary pooled constant comparator is correctness mean from the calibration split. Per-contrast calibration correctness means supply additional Brier comparators diagnostically; neither constant is fitted on evaluation. Bootstrap 256 whole-base samples of 512 bases, seed `950000000 + visual_seed`, retaining all six dependent conditions. Report conditional intervals for accuracy, signed residual, ECE and Brier differences, plus pooled Brier difference. These condition on fits/checkpoints and exclude training/calibration uncertainty. Bootstrap ECE is descriptive and positively biased as a finite-sample error estimate; binning/intervals are not population calibration proof.

Supplemental null inference duplicates evaluation zero-contrast images under both labels. Require exact duplicate-feature, score and probability equality and 50% accuracy for all sources, distinct from the primary independent-image null sample.

Engineering gates per model: hidden and physical highest-contrast point accuracy ≥.90; their primary zero-contrast point accuracy within .075 of .50; every supplemental duplicate null exactly .50; hidden ECE ≤.10 at every contrast; absolute hidden signed residual ≤.075 at every contrast; hidden pooled Brier ≤ calibration-derived global constant Brier. All three models must pass. Conditional Brier and orientation-stratified metrics stay visible even when primary gates pass; no claim of conditional reliability beyond the tested mixture/conditions. Equal-count artificial conditions and known finite renderer do not establish future calibration under memory, delivery or attention interventions.

## Freeze, review and decision

Five tests guard all-condition fitting, isolated inference, deterministic initialization/RNG isolation, zero scales/ties, null probability identity, ten-bin cancellation, whole-base bootstrap and label/nuisance separation. Subagent design review occurs before checkpoint inference. Freeze source snapshots/checkpoint/legacy archive hashes at preparation, commit before one reserved run, and archive all sources/inputs/coefficients/optimizer-loss summaries/probabilities/assessments. No retry, selected seed, rescore or replacement of failed predecessor. Record exceptions as retained failed/incomplete attempts. Exact replay retrains the same fixed budget and checks every archived tensor/result. A separate subagent audits numerical/interpretation evidence; shared-model/commissioning limits remain disclosed, humans optional.

Pass permits designing an acquisition model learned from executed synthetic outcomes, subject to separate calibration under its histories/actions and source controls. It does not itself qualify that model or identify appearance. Failure preserves the new attempt and leaves acquisition grounding incomplete. Qualified attention-manipulated appearance reference targets, selective attention-model interventions/restoration, matched sensory/Controller/Reporter alternatives, accurate diagnostic reports, powered theory-facing confirmation and broader replication all remain necessary. Never interpret a usable correctness estimate as evidence of qualia by definition.
