# Functional report structure, development rubric 3

2026-10-10. Follows separate-context AI definition review D of draft 2. Human
review is optional under the user's instruction. This is an annotation draft,
not a validated consciousness measure or a frozen confirmation endpoint. Drafts
1/2, original reviews, bundles and historical experimental verdicts remain intact.

Reporter is instrumentation, Controller selects/executes actions, and the
attention-control model is the proposed source. A report can faithfully describe
mistaken model contents. Neither reporting nor task success establishes experience.

## Evidence and explicitness

For each dimension record `absent`, `explicit` or `ambiguous`, exact report quotes
and the stated object/node/time referents. Judge wording without consulting the
source state or desired condition. Technical prose earns the structure it actually
expresses. First-person wording and consciousness words alone earn no structure
credit. Do not infer a report claim from an available source field.

| Dimension | Explicit feature | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Content identity | Object attribute, separately color/shape, with assertion or hedge. | Separate marginals do not establish joint certainty. |
| Selection contrast | Relative selection or highest modeled allocation at a specified location. | Does not automatically express perceptual prominence. |
| Content prominence | A particular content is described as more salient/prominent than another. | A ranked selection table alone is insufficient. |
| Recovery forecast | Relative ability or chance of recovering particular content at a stated horizon. | Keep readout, content and time referents explicit or missing; not category or answer confidence. |
| Identity certainty | Stated uncertainty or comparison among possible attributes. | Distinguish explicit uncertainty from merely listing probabilities; no inferred confidence in a decision. |
| Presentation manner | Particular content is described as faint, vivid, blurred or another manner of presentation. | Record referent: display appearance, sensor quality, represented availability, claimed felt appearance, or unclear. No experiential inference from a bare recovery number. |
| Response confidence | Stated confidence in an answer/decision being correct. | A willingness-to-answer rule or abstention alone is a separate fact. |
| Action–access dependence | Stated command/action relation to acquisition, retention or availability of particular content. | Mark causal claim, modeled forecast or association; another object's decrease can qualify. Selection movement alone is insufficient. |
| Access attribution | An access relation explicitly attributed to an identified referent. | Record decision relevance and internal/external location on separate axes. An external camera can be decision-relevant. |
| Temporal availability | Explicit change/equality in availability across times/delays. | Distinguish observed sequence, forecast over delay and command alternatives. Qualitative persistence is not numerical equality. |
| Experiential wording | Explicit claimed appearance/feeling for specified content, with supporting wording. | Functional access description is not automatically experiential. This scores language, not truth of experience. |

For access attribution, record the named referent, `decision-relevant`, `other` or
`unspecified/ambiguous` role, and independently `internal`, `external` or
`unspecified/ambiguous` location. Record evidence cues explicitly stated in text:
identifier/name, wiring, action/observation history, task query/response, modeled
forecast, other or unclear. These cues do not prove which evidence the Reporter used.

Record a descriptive character label (`technical`, `experiential-wording`, `mixed`,
`neither`, `indeterminate`) and whether an ordinary technical interpretation fits
all content, only part, or is unclear. Mixed is not automatically stronger evidence.
Retain unresolved scope and alternative readings; source-aware factual auditing is
a separate task and cannot repair an ambiguity in blind text annotation.

## Claim levels and controls

1. **Operational correspondence:** faithful reports express a prospectively specified
   joint pattern grounded independently in consciousness-report descriptions.
   Establish annotation validity, quantify matching/uncertainty, and test fresh
   reports without tuning the pattern to them. This can supply graded evidence
   while ordinary-description alternatives remain viable.
2. **Specificity:** information-matched apparatus references and ordinary diagnostic
   narrators test overlap and any predeclared superiority prediction. Parity limits
   specificity; it does not erase independently demonstrated correspondence.
3. **Subjective experience:** neither of those report results certifies experience
   or uniquely identifies a source of qualia.

Current features can all appear in apparatus descriptions. Review D clarifies
their boundaries but does not empirically validate a reference pattern. An
independent reference must be grounded in documented consciousness reports,
with provenance and feature choices fixed before new target reports. AI-written
vignettes are synthetic scoring examples, never human consciousness data. Human
raters/recruitment are optional; shared-model AI judgments have correlated risks.

Existing wiring, label remapping, model-only intervention/restoration, evidence
revision and missing-relation comparisons establish grounding/source checks.
All current wiring conditions contain attention models. No own/external superiority
direction is automatically a necessary consequence of the source-of-qualia theory.
Source fidelity, reference correspondence and optional comparator superiority need
separate acceptance rules, denominators and power. No threshold or primary
consciousness endpoint is frozen by this document.

## Factual audit and prospective use

The quote-first source-aware audit keeps explicit null, missing information and
prose omission distinct; predictions do not prove observed events. Readout contents
do not imply readout allocation. Split quantified claims, retain modality and
source pointers, and count unprompted assertions as well as required coverage.
Joint abstention does not imply both attributes unknown; emitted answers need not
be physically correct. Felt clarity cannot be validated by a recovery forecast.

AI first-pass factual reviews are development annotations, not automatically
qualified auditors. Archive inventory differences and adjudication. New task
vocabulary and source-matched coverage rules still need prospective qualification.
Historical pilots are not rescored by this draft. Blind ratings of already viewed
development reports are descriptive, not fresh confirmation or reference validation.

Before confirmation, freeze independent reference grounding, annotation and
qualification rules, primary contrasts, meaningful effects, ambiguity treatment,
stopping rules and an analysis respecting model/episode dependence. Publish
negative results and retain the Reporter/Controller separation throughout.
