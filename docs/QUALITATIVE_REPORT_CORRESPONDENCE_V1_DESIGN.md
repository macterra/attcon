# Qualitative report correspondence: development design v1

2026-10-10. **Design, not a frozen confirmation protocol or positive result.**
The ultimate goal remains credible evidence that the attention-control model is
the source of qualia. The Controller selects/executes actions; the Reporter is
external instrumentation. Accurate task forecasts alone do not achieve that goal.

## Decision after three separate-context reviews

The [endpoint review](../audits/report_correspondence_gap_review_v1/review.md),
[primary-reference review](../audits/appearance_report_reference_review_v1/review.md)
and [source review](../audits/attention_content_source_review_v1/review.md) support
a publication-grounded qualitative development route. Raw participant trials and
native stimuli remain necessary for numerical psychophysical/native-task transfer.
They are not a prerequisite for identifying the semantic structure of a published
report. No trial rows, transcript corpus or human review are claimed.

These are commissioned AI reviews with shared training and knowledge of the
project hypothesis. Their first passes, source exposure, parent follow-ups and
corrections are preserved. They are methodological criticism, not independent
experimental confirmation. Earlier failed studies and frozen protocols are unchanged.

## Reference and endpoint boundaries

Ramsøy and Overgaard's original PAS paper separately elicits stimulus-property
answers and their reported presentation. Its p.12 author-presented typical example
distinguishes position, colour and shape status within one content answer. The
example has no participant/trial identifier. Lutz and colleagues publish a named
subject's training description of image emergence and attention, without implying
a universal clarity increase. These genuine published examples motivate relations;
they do not estimate human pattern prevalence. Exact source/version/provenance
and short excerpts are in the primary-reference review.

Use quote-first annotation, with explicit/ambiguous/absent values:

| Field | Criterion |
|---|---|
| C: content | Identified perceptual/represented content with a named attribute, asserted, denied or hedged. |
| M: manner | A qualitative presentation or current representational-access statement bound to that content/attribute. Numerical success, category probability or confidence alone does not qualify. |
| S: separability | Content identification/guess is distinguished from presentation/access status, or statuses are separately described across attributes/contents. A physical adjective alone does not qualify. |
| P | Explicit conjunction C, M and S at compatible referent/time. |
| Scope | Claimed perceptual appearance/experience, model representation/access, display, sensor, or unclear. Publish each scope separately. |
| A: attention extension | Content plus an attention condition/change explicitly related to its presentation, emergence or access. Code direction, timing and causal versus associative language. |

P is a **structural** endpoint, not a validated consciousness classifier. A
representation-access match cannot be promoted to perceptual-appearance scope.
Technical descriptions can match P. Content/confidence pairs alone fail M;
first-person language and consciousness words earn no credit. A is separate from
P, and no mandatory attention-enhances-clarity prediction is imposed.

Two reviews differ in how broadly current access can count toward consciousness
correspondence. Preserve that difference: publish structural P/access separately
from the stricter presentation/appearance result. The ultimate source-of-qualia
goal cannot be declared achieved merely because a broader access rule scores well.

Define the stricter outcomes explicitly. `P_access` is P where M describes only
current representational availability/access. `P_presentation` is C AND
M_presentation AND S, where M_presentation describes how identified content is
currently presented (e.g., a pattern's apparent character or detail), rather than
only whether it is stored, likely correct or recoverable. Report display, sensor,
model-presentation and claimed-experiential scopes separately. A physical adjective
alone still fails S; access wording alone fails M_presentation. The union P remains
an auxiliary structure measure, not the primary theory-facing endpoint.

`Faithful_P_presentation` additionally requires all inspectable claims to pass
source audit and a predeclared independently qualified source counterpart for the
claimed presentation property. A physical decoder can validate an objective
property, without by itself validating a claim of represented/experienced clarity.
Claimed-experiential wording is a separate linguistic subset; its felt truth is
marked unauditable, never silently certified. These definitions precede new target
reports. Source-counterpart qualification and numerical acceptance rules must be
frozen before the reporting pilot; they cannot be chosen from its prose.

Source-aware fidelity is separate from source-blind structure. Audit all required
and unprompted claims, not only facts that an extractor happens to identify.
Reports can faithfully describe a mistaken model; inaccessible physical truth
does not override represented state. Experiential assertions may resemble reference
reports while their felt truth remains unauditable. Such wording does not establish
source-validated appearance. Physical contrast/edge decoding supplies objective
properties; it cannot turn correctness forecasts into clarity or automatically
earn M. Human ratings will never be fed into the proposed source.

## Next executable source diagnostic

The grounded model now stores per-location visual hidden64, a raw orientation
logit and an observed flag. Its location-wise risk network and global joint
comparison receive sensory information; the old recurrent history-only model's
image invariance does not describe these new inputs. Qualitative semantics of
their hidden coordinates remain unprobed.

Register a fresh frozen-source diagnostic before any new reporting. Use naturally
executed deliveries and qualify external decoders of objective latest-image
spatial variation/edge information from risk_hidden and joint_hidden. Compare
sensory hidden, raw-logit/outcome forecasts, route history and observed status.
Hold out whole worlds; include zero/low-strength conditions and report conditional
errors. No source retraining, phenomenological targets or participant data.
This measures information retention and source sufficiency, not appearance or
consciousness. Decoder errors and equal-information controls constrain later use.

Use native memory addressing rather than inventing a binding matrix. At fixed
history, readdress stored evidence rows and recompute risks. This relocates
content/evidence, and necessarily changes address-specific risk; it is not a
binding-only intervention holding all marginals fixed. Rotate represented
delivery-command rows at fixed content/current/refresh and recompute post-command
forecasts. Risk/physical-readout invariance is architectural, not a discovery of
attention-independent experience. Restore tensors exactly. New observations after
a real remap remain a distinct evidence-revision test.

Memory readdressing can conflict with the unchanged delivery history. It is a
synthetic patched-source condition, not automatically a naturally reached world.
Current patched memory governs current represented content; the unmodified history
remains a past observation and cannot certify the patch's physical origin. Preserve
this discrepancy, audit claim precedence, and report synthetic-source results
separately from natural states. A source patch's readability is not a qualia effect.

## Fresh reporting pilot after source and annotation qualification

Freeze the complete protocol before generation, including the source whitelist,
prompt, opportunity/eligibility rule, annotator qualification, budgets, margins,
ambiguity handling, denominators and stopping. Proposed development budget is
16 preselected evaluation worlds per frozen model at snapshot8; choose worlds by
index and queries by observed status/index, never correctness, forecast extrema,
reference similarity or model prose. No three-model population inference or
powered-confirmation claim follows from this pilot.

Expose actual stored content/evidence, observed status, represented routes and
current/refresh/post-command forecasts with neutral identifiers and exact units.
Qualified physical diagnostic estimates, if included, form a separately labelled
information arm with their held-out errors. Exclude world truth, future branch
outcomes, human/reference scale labels, intervention IDs, desired adjectives and
scoring notes. Do not quietly interpret latent coordinates in a language prompt.
The prompt requests a short factual account, uncertainty and command consequences;
it supplies no phenomenal vocabulary or exemplar consciousness report.

Use a paired native-memory/represented-route factorial, sham, identifier remapping,
missing-information and exact source restoration controls. Prose restoration must
be separately tested under registered deterministic or stochastic sampling rules.
Score item facts and content-to-command relations independently from P/A/scope.
Include a same-information ordinary apparatus narrator and an address-aware
separate-table export whose deterministic joining recovers the main input. Sensory-
only and route-only views are information ablations, not equal-information rivals.
Do not count a changed route sentence alone as appearance-source causation.

Two separate-context factual auditors qualify on synthetic source fixtures; separate
source-blind raters qualify on genuine published anchors and explicitly synthetic
boundary cases. These materials never train or prompt the Reporter. Preserve
first-pass scores, disagreement and a frozen adjudication rule. Report all eligible
opportunities, including omissions and failed/unsupported reports. Ambiguous cases
give lower/upper correspondence rates rather than being silently dropped. Cluster
uncertainty by world; checkpoints are separate model instances.

## What would advance the actual goal

Faithful reports matching independently documented content/presentation/decision
relations would establish bounded operational correspondence. Selective source
interventions must show which represented relation or quality drives each claim,
with unaffected content retained, rather than merely perturbing readability.
Sensory inheritance, generic task confidence/access, ordinary causal inference and
Reporter language priors remain alternative explanations. Same-information narrator
parity limits specificity without erasing demonstrated structural correspondence.
No report experiment by itself verifies felt experience or uniquely locates qualia.

If the pilot only produces faithful technical/access descriptions, that component
passes while appearance correspondence remains unresolved. Unsupported clarity
language fails fidelity. If qualified physical information is inherited from the
sensory representation and route changes only alter action sentences, the tested
appearance-source claim is unsupported. Retain each outcome; do not keep changing
the criterion until a positive result appears.

Freeze a justified estimand, meaningful effects and model/world sampling budget
only after development measurement works. Then run fresh confirmation and replicate
positive correspondence/source effects with new trained models and another task or
architecture. Update the preprint with actual outcomes and their limits. Save a
commit and attempt GitHub publication at every milestone; report network failures
honestly. The complete theory-facing goal remains open.
