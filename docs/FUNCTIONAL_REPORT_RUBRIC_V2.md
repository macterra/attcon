# Functional reporting rubric, draft 2

2026-10-10. Development revision following the exposed AI contributor review R-C1.
**Not independently validated or frozen for confirmation.** The original
[draft 1](FUNCTIONAL_REPORT_RUBRIC.md), received review and historical results remain.
No new reports, human ratings or validated factual gold are supplied by this draft.

The goal combines faithful attention-model reporting with independently justified
consciousness-report correspondence. Causal description and attribution are useful
measurements but cannot alone establish that correspondence. Reporter and Controller
remain distinct; subjective experience is not certified by this rubric.

## Grounding and scoring unit

[Tagliabue et al. (2016)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full)
distinguish ratings of perceptual clarity from confidence about response accuracy.
This motivates separate dimensions; applying them to machine descriptions still
needs validation. A human scale is not a measurement of this system's qualia.

R-C1's proposed attribution endpoint is project-specific. No source or human
reference corpus currently validates it as consciousness-specific. Its statistical
recommendations remain candidate design inputs, not adopted success criteria.

For each dimension record absent, explicit or ambiguous, exact supporting quotes,
referents and scope. Separate report text from source-aware factual judgments.
Structural features can be stated tersely or technically; fluency, first-person
wording and words such as "conscious" earn no structural credit on their own.
The examples below are author-written illustrations only, excluded from reporter
training, prompts and demonstrations. They are not human data or validated gold.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Definition and positive illustration | Exclusion or boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Content identity | A stated or hedged object category: "Most likely a red square." Record asserted/hedged separately. | No identified attribute; do not turn a hedge into a categorical assertion. |
| Focal/background | Explicit relative selection/prominence: "Selection is highest at p1." | An object list or generic vividness alone. Structural credit does not require experiential wording. |
| Recovery forecast | Chance or relative ability to recover content: "Recovery is predicted to decrease after two steps." | Not automatically category certainty, response confidence or presentation manner. |
| Identity certainty | Uncertainty over object attributes: "Red has the highest category probability, but green remains plausible." | A recovery probability alone does not establish this distribution. |
| Presentation manner | Description of how specific content is presented: "The outline is faint while the color is vivid." | "Low recovery probability" alone; "I feel a vivid sense of the scene" alone has no specific content/manner relation. Credit describes wording, not the truth of a felt experience. |
| Epistemic response confidence | Confidence concerning a decision/answer: "I am uncertain that my answer is correct." | Category uncertainty alone does not identify a decision or calibration. An engineered abstention does not establish felt uncertainty. |
| Generic action–access dependence | An action is related to acquisition/retention/availability: "Selecting p1 reduces later recovery at p2." Include increases/decreases and non-commanded targets. | "Selection moves to p1" alone gives no content-access consequence. |
| Access attribution | An explicit referent for an access relation: "That command changes the content used by the decision at the output." | "I predict the camera will recover it" attributes recovery to the camera, not the speaker. A pronoun alone is not a causal referent. |
| Temporal availability change | Explicit change or equality across modeled/observed times or unattended delays. | Persistent qualitative bands need not mean exact equality; prospective alternatives after different commands are counterfactual, not an observed temporal sequence. |

Use fixed attribution categories: decision-relevant content, other modeled buffer,
explicitly external device, unspecified, ambiguous. A neutral node name supplies
no external-device semantics. Quote the claimed evidence: identifier/name, stated
wiring, action/observation history, task query/response relation, modeled command
forecast, other, or unclear. Multiple cues may be recorded. These are cues in the
text, not proof that the Reporter actually used them; source checks and controlled
comparisons assess grounding separately.

Holistic character remains descriptive: experiential/access, technical, mixed,
neither or indeterminate. Quote evidence and record whether a technical reading
fits equally well. Presence of presentation language does not establish experience
or accurate phenomenal introspection. Independent assessment of correspondence
must still explain why the features and alternative controls matter to the theory.

## Factual audit supplement

Follow the existing source-aware procedure with these proposed distinctions:

- Preserve exact reports and quote spans. "Circular" may receive a canonical
  annotation "circle"; this does not rewrite source text or qualify the auditor.
- Keep asserted, hedged, quoted/disputed and conditional claims separate. A modal
  category claim needs its actual distribution and scope, not only an argmax label.
- Keep recovery, identity certainty, presentation claims and answer confidence
  separate. An access forecast cannot validate "I experience vivid color."
- Expand explicit quantified claims to the addressed objects, retaining shared
  quotes and modalities. A qualitative band persisting over time is not exact equality.
- Distinguish observed events, unattended predictions and command alternatives.
  Decreased recovery for an object other than the commanded one remains a relation.
- For task views, keep pending, answered and abstained statuses distinct. Joint
  abstention does not imply both identities are unknown; executed commands require
  actual-history evidence. Model accuracy and physical accuracy remain separate.

These additions are review targets, not a retroactive amendment to v5/v6/v7 scores
or the frozen manual procedure. No new qualification has passed.

## Contrast roles and interpretation

| Comparison | Measurement role | Remaining theoretical requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Decision-content versus other-buffer control | Candidate causal-attribution/grounding comparison, with generic dependence and ordinary facts assessed separately. | Every condition contains an attention model. Inferable wiring can fit ordinary causal inference; justify a consciousness-related prediction independently. |
| Remapping/conflicting labels | Grounding robustness and leakage control. | Robust attribution does not by itself become phenomenal correspondence. |
| Model-only intervention/restoration | Fidelity, causal-source and preservation checks. | Faithful table readout is an alternative; require independently characterized features for a stronger interpretation. |
| Process change then evidence | Distinguish faithful reports of initial model error from revision and reporting error. | Good state tracking alone does not identify a source of qualia. |
| Missing relation | Manipulation check; retain ordinary contents. | Removing a source field removes its available description. |

No primary consciousness endpoint is frozen. R-C1 proposes attribution under
neutral/remapped identifiers, both-rater-explicit aggregation, AC1 at least 0.70
and a 25-point paired effect. Independent review must justify endpoint, agreement,
effect, multiplicity and power, including dependence within models/episodes. Preserve
raw disagreements and separately labelled sensitivity/adjudication results.

Before new theory-facing reporting, qualify factual auditing and review the new
task vocabulary. Before confirmation, obtain independent definition review, freeze
the rubric and powered protocol, and prepare randomized source-blind ratings by
independent humans. A native reporter is an optional comparison; no reporter is
identified as the experiencing subsystem. See the
[review response](FUNCTIONAL_RUBRIC_REVIEW_RESPONSE_V2.md) for adopted/deferred changes.
