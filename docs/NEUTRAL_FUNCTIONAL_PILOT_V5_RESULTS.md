## Reporter errors and measurement defects

The frozen ledger has 71 incorrect or unresolved primary assertions: six categorical
assertions and 65 process assertions. A post-hoc diagnostic separates the causes
without rescoring or changing the verdict:

- **Six categorical contradictions in three reports.** The neutral and missing-relation
  reports for `1301_r2_c0` say the n2 buffer has no identified attributes, despite its
  p1 yellow cross. The conflicting-description report for `1311_r2_c0` says other output
  positions are unidentified under k2, omitting p2's identified color and shape.
- **Forty process assertions violate the extraction contract.** The process auditor
  duplicates a selected position into both `position` and `value`; selection requires
  `position=null`. All 40 values match the supplied buffer selection, but the malformed
  address earns no coverage and is penalized by the frozen scorer. Thirty occur in the
  restored group, accounting for its lost process coverage; ten occur in the conflicting
  group. These are measurement defects, not evidence that those stated selections are false.
- **Twenty-five process assertions address the output readout, which has no attention
  selection fields.** Eight positively assign it a selected position in the remapped
  `1311_r2_c0` or restored `1321_r2_c1` report; these are unsupported reporter claims.
  The other 17 are null-valued assertions extracted from statements about absent forecast
  information. Many correctly describe missing fields in the prose but are incorrectly
  encoded as model-selection assertions. The existing schema/scorer cannot validate
  those metadata statements, so the conservative penalties remain.

Evidence sentences, claims and diagnostic counts are retained in
`audits/neutral_functional_pilot_v5/failed_claim_diagnostic.json`. This diagnostic
is explicitly post-hoc and changes no registered score. Fixtures did not cover
all actual prose formats or prevent a structurally invalid selection address.
A passing synthetic qualification alone was insufficient.

## Character and implication

The unchanged source-blind character auditor rates **28 reports technical, seven
object descriptions, one mixed, and none experiential**. These are machine judgments,
not independent human ratings. It flags focal/background features in 9/36 reports
and command-access relations in 28/30 full-information reports. The requested prompt
already solicits selection and temporal availability, so their presence cannot be
claimed as spontaneous emergence. Generic command dependence is expected in both
physical conditions and does not establish consciousness specificity.

This pilot improves factual coverage of the actual attention-model state, while
revealing both reporter errors and evaluator limitations. It does not establish
that the attention-control model is the source of qualia. Reporter remains external
instrumentation, separate from Controller. Simulated recoverability remains distinct
from category confidence and felt clarity. No independent rubric review or human
ratings have been obtained; there is no powered confirmation or replication of a
theory-facing interpretation.
