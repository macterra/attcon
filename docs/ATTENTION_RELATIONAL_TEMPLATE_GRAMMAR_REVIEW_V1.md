2026-10-19. A critical code/reference review finds no qualifying O/D/A witness
in the frozen literal renderer's grammar. This is a pre-label assessment of method
capacity, not a rating of actual target reports, a prediction of V3 outputs, or
a negative consciousness finding. No pending V3 judgment was read.

The [frozen renderer](../scripts/attention_relational_report_v1.py) emits a fixed
coordinate/conditional header, addresses, origin/time JSON and complete role peak
sets. The [unchanged reference](ATTENTION_RELATIONAL_REFERENCE_V1.md) explicitly
excludes coordinates/order, uncertainty, generic labels and command changes alone
from its stronger organization/access/attention relations.

| Feature | Grammar-level assessment |
| --- | --- |
| C: identified content | Role-bound represented locations can identify content and an attribute. |
| O: organization/presentation/access | Enumerated positions and ties do not themselves describe the additional bound organization or access required by the reference. |
| D: content/status distinction | Origin kinds and times distinguish provenance, not a separately expressed qualifying organization/presentation/access status. |
| A: content-processing relation | Command-indexed coordinates do not themselves state a relation to qualifying organization, emergence, presentation or access. |

could itself supply a positive correspondence route. Its useful role is technical
control: exact construction, source provenance, property tracking and detection of
rubric overextension. A positive raw rater label must still have actual semantic
witnesses; none is granted because its token came from A. A complete peak list or
an origin record cannot become a presentation report merely by being renamed.
