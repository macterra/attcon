# One bounded same-address model-state reporting pilot

2026-10-20. Informed development design after the
[distribution/processing-score result](ATTENTION_MODEL_DISTRIBUTION_GEOMETRY_V1_RESULTS.md).
No new source inference or reports have been run for this pilot. This document
fixes the scientific question and finite budget; source/runtime and measurement
implementations must be bound and published before their respective first runs.
It does not release another auditor version or reinterpret old failed gates.

## Question and consequence

Can neutral reports faithfully describe the relationship between **current
attention-model content and possible processing effects on that same item**,
with independently supported organization/access structure? Do those attributed
claims follow selective model-state changes while distinguishing changes to W?
An accurate technical probability summary is a useful measurement result, but
does not by itself establish the additional correspondence.

The previous narrator already had full localization distributions. This pilot
changes the source/query relationship and reporting workload: one same-address
current estimate, three conditional estimates, explicit native score semantics,
and short reports. It does not claim to give the narrator full fields for the
first time. The Reporter remains external instrumentation; operation-score
selection and the native Controller's later targeting remain distinct.

## Fixed bounded sample

Use **encoder1311, worlds0–3, decision12**, from the same **heldout native
archives already exposed in reporting development**. These are not fresh heldout
evidence, new systems or confirmation. One fixed encoder bounds feasibility cost;
1311 is the middle registered seed, not selected for a favorable new output.
Existing numerical outcomes are known. Do not run other seeds to rescue a negative
pilot. Confirmation would require fresh complete systems under a separate design.

Set candidate address to the previous command's address, n3. Both the current
learned estimate and all conditional alternatives must concern n3, rather than
silently combining a learned current constituent at one node with copied W or a
forecast at another. Use each case's actual queried role: red/blue/red/blue.
Keep current represented time12, feedback through11, and conditional represented
time13 separate. Future alternatives are not executed outcomes.

Compare A, independently initialized same-architecture ordinary, analytic, and
native W. Ordinary and analytic also estimate attention-command effects. W is a
supplied completed/conditional reference, not another learned predictor.

For each of four cases and each of three predictors, prepare five joined views:

1. Predictor baseline plus baseline W.
2. Addressed predictor donor state plus unchanged W.
3. Exact saved predictor restoration plus unchanged W.
4. Unchanged predictor plus counterfactual changed current W.
5. Unchanged predictor plus exact saved W restoration.

This fixes **60 joined opportunities**, plus **12 W-only opportunities** covering
baseline, changed and restored W in each case: **72 reports total**. Baseline and
restored public inputs may be identical; retain them as repeated measurements,
not independent evidence or a demand for identical sampled wording. No favorable
case selection, source substitution, extra views or replacement first calls.

## Neutral short reports

Each public view includes only the selected item/role's current and three
conditional 4×4 localization fields, origins/times, and the published processing
score rule. Joined views have two separately identified panels. W reference
values and source predictions remain distinguishable; no experiential/status
labels are added to the source. Declare fields as model/readout values, not
physical truth or calibrated confidence.

Freeze neutral identifiers, payload/schema, exact prompt, actor configuration,
word budget, ordering, transport and failure policy before reports. Use **at most
120 prose words**. Ask for the represented current content and differences under
the possible processing operations, including unchanged content. Request no
theory, consciousness reference, phenomenological adjectives or preferred style.
Supply no human report examples, semantic reference, desired result or peer text.
Do not require an exhaustive list of all cell maxima/propositions; that workload
was separate from prose and is unnecessary for this pilot's question.

Preserve every first prompt/response, incomplete output and failure. A new output
is not a replacement for an old report. No report can pass fidelity because a
self-selected proposition list is correct while its prose is unchecked.

## Finite independent assessment

Before first reports, publish the complete assessment instructions, output schema,
assignment inventory and join/disposition rules. Use two separate first streams
for source-aware whole-prose factual review and two for source-blind semantic
review under the unchanged relational reference. Keep firsts separate until all
their assigned contexts end; do not replace failures or fill missing reviews.
AI reviewers are authorized; shared training/commissioning limits independence.

Factual reviewers enumerate **all claims in the actual short prose**, retain exact
quotes, source paths/operands, referent and current/conditional time, and assess
each claim's support. Unsupported or unauditable assertions cannot establish whole
fidelity. Semantic reviewers receive only blinded text and the existing reference,
not tensors, source/condition, functional outcomes, audit results or desired matches.
Report COD and CA separately, preserving organization, access and appearance scopes.
Coordinates, probabilities, preference scores or their changes alone do not earn
the stronger structure. Genuine permitted witnesses must also remain available.

Source grounding must bind each proposed composite to compatible evidence for the
same content/address/attribute/time. A-dependent correspondence requires a necessary
actual-A operand or explicit relation involving it. Retain mixed A/W meanings;
neither p0 naming nor provenance alone establishes it. No scope upgrade, repaired
binding, new hidden-status requirement or metadata-wide disqualification.

Publish unconditional availability, whole-fidelity yields, both first streams and
all unknowns over72, with A/ordinary/analytic20 each and W12 separately. Any
valid-only rate is supplementary. Report source-current and future-conditioned
bindings separately. Pair asserted properties across baseline/donor/restoration
and fixed-A/W-change controls, preserving true nulls and unchanged assertions.
The same COD/CA structure may describe changed content: no category switch required.
Record reviewer disagreement; exposed root discussion cannot erase firsts or
manufacture a missing measurement. Direct factual review is a development method,
not a retroactive pass for an old auditor or proof of general reliability.

## Stop-or-advance decision

Complete this pilot once. Advance toward a fresh confirmation registration only
if it establishes source-faithful, independently warranted organization/access
correspondence involving actual A, with interpretable non-null source tracking
and restoration and the matched alternatives retained. A positive development
case is feasibility evidence, not the full theory result or an adequate powered
sample. Meaningful effect/precision, measurement error/availability, clustered
sampling, N, inference and stopping must be fixed before fresh confirmation;
replication must change task or architecture.

If the pilot yields only accurate technical summaries, unresolved source/semantic
meaning or assessment failure, **close this reporting route for this source and
interface**. Do not try another seed, prompt, rater or auditor until it passes.
Retain the result without inferring absence of consciousness or refutation of
the theory. The next theory-directed option is a distinct comparison/refinement
architecture study, measuring whether a content-bound temporal discrepancy
representation changes subsequent World-Model construction under matched
informative controls, selective interventions and restoration. That is an optional
MSTC extension, not a prerequisite retroactively imposed on this pilot.

The surviving rival in either route is ordinary attention-conditioned estimation
and external summarization, or generic predictive correction. An engineered
functional candidate and fluent language do not uniquely establish a quale-generating
locus. The ultimate goal remains credible evidence for that connection, not a
passing instrument or task-performance necessity. Commit, push and verify each
completed source, report, assessment and decision milestone.
