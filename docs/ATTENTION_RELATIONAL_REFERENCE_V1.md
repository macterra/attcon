Frozen before any new narrator reports, rater calls or report-software outputs were inspected. **The published evidence warrants a limited qualitative content/status and content–attention reference, not a consciousness classifier or a coordinate-only endpoint.** The [machine-readable pack](../audits/attention_relational_report_v1/reference/reference.json) and [rater instructions](../audits/attention_relational_report_v1/reference/rater_instructions.txt) define the exact annotation contract. It does not require a form variable disjoint from every fine-content/uncertainty/access description, private-experience proof, task necessity, unique ordinary superiority or mandatory Modeler refinement. ## Published basis and limits

[Ramsøy and Overgaard (2004)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/216733480_Introspection_and_Subliminal_Perception), original pp.11–12, elicit separate property answers and presentation judgments. Their typical-report illustration distinguishes position, colour and shape statuses. It is author-presented, without a participant/trial identifier; the scale includes certainty language. It grounds differentiation, not a clean machine confidence-to-experience mapping.

[Lutz et al. (2002)](https://portablelaboratory.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/lutzetal02.pdf), p.1587, publish a named subject's training description relating image emergence and attention over time. Four trained participants and prompted introspection limit generalization; it supplies neither universal clarity improvement nor an internal model measurement.

[Tagliabue et al. (2016)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full), Methods, separately collect discrimination and perception-quality responses. [Chuyin et al. (2024), version3](https://f1000research.com/articles/11-69/v3) collect content words with response confidence. These constrained formats motivate distinguishing answer/confidence from presentation; feature-negative content/confidence text is not a negative consciousness case.

[Liu et al. (2009)](https://npal.psy.msu.edu/wp-content/uploads/Publications/Liu_2009_PsychSci.pdf) motivate attribute-specific attention/appearance comparisons. [Schneider and Malik (2021)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34038507/) retain a competing criterion/uncertainty interpretation. Neither validates a general attention-enhances-clarity rule. [Webb and Graziano (2015)](https://grazianolab.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf3411/files/graziano/files/webb_graziano_2015_reprint.pdf), pp.1–3, supply theory motivation for self/content/attention relations and model/process dissociation; they are not an empirical report anchor.

The two short authentic excerpts appear only in reference.json, with exact status and provenance. No positive machine vignette was invented from source estimates. Generic synthetic negatives are explicitly labelled and are not human reports. Original publications predate this project, but selection and abstraction are hypothesis-framed. This is not a systematic narrative corpus, estimated human prevalence, calibrated sensitivity/specificity or trial-level psychophysical transfer. Primary-text access/version limits are preserved in the pack and metadata.

## Frozen endpoint contract

| Feature | Required text distinction | Explicit negative |
|---|---|---|
| C | Identified perceptual/represented content and an attribute, including denied or mistaken content | Unbound scalar or generic consciousness claim |
| O | Bound organization/presentation/emergence/current-access description, with scope/time | Coordinates/order, physical adjectives, confidence/probabilities or task success alone |
| D | Content identification versus status/organization, or separately attributed statuses across contents/attributes | Merely different positions, identities or confidence values |
| A | Content-specific relation to an attention/allocation/content-processing condition/change | Attention word, command choice or changed coordinate alone |

Freeze two qualitative composites: **COD = C AND O AND D**, and separately **CA = C AND A**. Every conjunction needs compatible referent/attribute/time evidence; unrelated sentences cannot be pooled. Neither demands a first-person claim or a mandatory clarity increase. Annotate explicit/ambiguous/absent using exact quoted report substrings and offsets. Explicit yields a lower match; explicit or ambiguous yields a possible upper match. Preserve each rater's first judgment and disagreement; do not revise the rubric to improve target matches.

Scope remains separate: model organization, model access, claimed perceptual appearance, display/sensor property or unclear. A report of an explicitly organized model-location pattern can be scored as a prospective **model-organization structural analogue**, provided it goes beyond listing coordinates or confidence. That abstraction is a project hypothesis; the publications do not empirically validate location-field concentration as human clarity. A model/access match never becomes a claimed-appearance match, v1 manner qualification or evidence of felt truth. Forecasts never become current perception. Basic spatial relations, uncertainty, answer confidence, success/recovery and model/process distinction remain auxiliary features.

This answers the reference crux directly: **red/blue pairwise spatial order by itself is insufficient** for a consciousness-like organization endpoint. A separately described bound organization/status relation can be tested as limited publication-grounded structure. If the current source only affords coordinates and confidence, faithful reporting may pass while COD/CA remain negative or ambiguous. That is an honest useful result, not a license to induce unsupported clarity. If appearance scope is absent, report that absence explicitly; do not call all technical organization phenomenal.

## Separation from source fidelity and interpretation

Source-blind raters receive only neutral unit identifiers, report text and this reference pack. They never see source tensors/identity, condition, intervention, Controller outcomes or factual-audit scores. Narrators never see this pack, reference examples, theory, rubric or desired language. Source-aware auditors separately check every inspectable claim against the actual supplied estimate, preserving wrong estimates, timestamps and attribution. Felt truth is unauditable; unsupported experiential assertions stay visible. Only the frozen reporting protocol may join fidelity and reference labels.

Direct-W, ordinary, analytic, generic-sensor and literal-template comparisons delimit the external language pipeline's contribution. They may match the same structure; parity limits specificity rather than canceling genuine correspondence or requiring uniquely necessary A. A positive could establish the restricted computational content/report bridge with causal A/W attribution, not that A generates experience. A source-independent template match or lack of faithful independent structure is a concrete negative crux.

The reporting protocol must freeze its complete opportunity inventory, information/prompt budgets, rater allocation, first-run retention, schema/evidence validation, aggregation and A/W/restoration controls before execution. This reference fixes semantic labels and boundaries without inventing a numerical theory-success threshold or authorizing a new report/fit cycle. It is ready for protocol and software binding; actual rater qualification and reports have not occurred. Same-family AI task separation with inherited context is disclosed and does not claim independent-family or human validation. Controller remains distinct from Reporter.
