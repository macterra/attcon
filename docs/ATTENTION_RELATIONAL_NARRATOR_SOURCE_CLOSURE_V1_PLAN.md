addresses, operator, content referent, scope and time. Compare that property on
the complete registered baseline/donor, fixed-source W-change and restoration
pairs. Retain unchanged properties as true nulls, and missing support as unknown
rather than invariance. Check unaffected facts. Native source-level shams remain
native controls; there is no extra scheduled sham-narration variant.

Tracking concerns the attributed assertion and changed property, not word changes
or a demand that the COD/CA class switch. Mixed A–W relations are allowed with
all operands explicit. Ordinary and analytic sources also model attention effects;
their parity does not supply an attention-model-absence test or disqualify the
general theory. Do not require current A to be the Controller's targeting input
or consciousness to be necessary for task performance.
