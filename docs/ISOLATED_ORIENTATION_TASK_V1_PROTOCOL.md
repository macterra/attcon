# Isolated synthetic orientation task v1

Status: frozen before checkpoint inference or evaluation outputs. This ancillary development study replaces the paired-input advantage of the [previous diagnostic](ORIENTATION_TRANSFER_DIAGNOSTIC_V1_PROTOCOL.md) with isolated-image decisions. It does not test the attention-model origin of qualia or supply participant appearance targets. Its result determines whether synthetic sensory decisions and their correctness probabilities are usable prerequisites for grounding a subsequent acquisition model.

## Inputs and splits

Use frozen visual checkpoints 1301, 1311, 1321. For each checkpoint draw 256 classifier-development bases, 256 calibration bases, and 512 evaluation bases using nuisance seeds `910000000 + checkpoint_seed + 10000*split`, split 0/1/2. Draw balanced horizontal/vertical labels with a separate generator using nuisance seed plus 1000000. Labels never alter geometry, phase or noise generation. Split base identities are distinct; independently sampled phase classes and aperture masks may recur. No participant rows are present.

Each base has one orientation and six contrast variants, all retained. Renderer: 16×16 RGB, horizontal or vertical binary grating with four cycles across the image, random phase, random circle/square aperture, center jitter 7.5±1 and radius 3.7–5.2. Noise is independent Gaussian RGB noise with SD .015 for each base and contrast. The physical luminance pairs `(38.2,38.2), (39,37.3), (39.5,36.5), (40,36.2), (40.2,36), (41.1,35.8)` are divided by 50, background 35.5/50, then clamped after noise. These six synthetic levels follow the previous diagnostic; native human stimulus transfer, spatial frequency, temporal presentation and gamma remain unvalidated. Zero contrast retains an aperture/background difference but no orientation texture.

## Instruments

Fixed sources: visual hidden state (64), visual logits (8), color/shape category probabilities (8), category maximum probabilities and entropies (4), physical directional edge energy (1). No visual encoder retraining. Fit one ridge readout per source and checkpoint using only highest-contrast classifier-development images. Target is −1 horizontal/+1 vertical. Normalize using development column mean and RMS centered deviation; zero scale becomes 1. Fixed ridge .01, intercept development target mean. Predict vertical for score >0, horizontal otherwise. No evaluation batch centering, paired differences, contrast centering or access to another image during prediction.

Fit a separate two-parameter logistic correctness instrument to the 256×6 calibration scores. Input is absolute classifier score standardized with calibration mean/SD; SD=0 becomes 1. Target is actual synthetic correctness. Fit intercept and slope by 100 deterministic Newton iterations on mean BCE plus `.001/2 * squared coefficient norm`, penalizing both coefficients. No tuning, clipping to .5, fallback, human target or explicit contrast covariate. Nonfinite fit or final maximum gradient exceeding 1e-8 terminates the reserved attempt as a failure. Archive final gradient and penalized objective. Magnitude may indirectly encode contrast. Probability means that this fixed classifier's answer is correct; it is neither participant confidence, phenomenal clarity, recovery probability nor attention-model state.

## Assessment and gates

Archive predictions, inputs, features, coefficients, probabilities and generator nuisances. Report pooled and each-contrast accuracy, Brier, ECE and reliability bins. ECE bins are [0,.2), [.2,.4), [.4,.6), [.6,.8), [.8,1]; internal boundaries enter the upper bin. Compare Brier to the constant calibration correctness mean applied to evaluation. All splits have balanced orientation and an equal six-contrast mixture; outcomes can be unbalanced. Bootstrap whole evaluation bases, retaining all six contrasts: 256 draws of 512 bases, seed `920000000 + checkpoint_seed`. Report conditional accuracy intervals by contrast and pooled Brier-difference intervals. They condition on fixed fits/checkpoints; three models are not a population replication sample.

Supplemental null control duplicates each evaluation zero-contrast image with both labels and requires identical scores and exactly 50% correctness. This is outside the primary independent-image stream, not another isolated-task sample. Independent primary zero-contrast accuracy may fluctuate around chance.

All gates apply separately to all three models: hidden and physical-control highest-contrast accuracy ≥.90; their primary zero-contrast accuracy within .075 of .50; supplemental duplicated-null accuracy exactly .50 for every source; hidden pooled ECE ≤.10; hidden pooled Brier ≤ calibration-mean baseline Brier. Other source assessments and conditional calibration remain visible regardless of gate outcome. These are engineering gates for this artificial mixture, not a theory endpoint; pooled success does not certify calibration at every contrast. Retain failures without retries, flipped labels or selecting a model/source after evaluation.

## Review, integrity and decisions

Independent subagent review checks design before outputs. Tests check batch invariance, label/nuisance separation, fit/calibration split isolation, known scoring and grouped resampling. Freeze source snapshots/checkpoint hashes and commit before one reserved run. Exact replay verifies every tensor and result. A separate subagent audits outcomes and interpretation, with AI commissioning/shared-model limitations disclosed; no human qualification is required.

Passing permits designing an acquisition model grounded in synthetic outcomes, while preserving Controller/Reporter separation. It does not promote this correctness instrument into the attention model or establish appearance correspondence. Failing retains the diagnostic and its failure. The broader goal still requires independently grounded consciousness-related endpoints, causal model-state interventions/restoration and sensory/reporting alternatives; missing human archives and the rejection of direct existing-model transfer remain recorded.
