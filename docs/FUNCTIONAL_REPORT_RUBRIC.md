# Functional reporting rubric, draft 1

2026-10-04. Prepared before new functional-wiring reports. **Not independently
validated or frozen for confirmation.** No human ratings have been collected.
This rubric evaluates descriptions; it never asks a rater to certify consciousness.

## Independent grounding and limits

Tagliabue et al. (2016) used graded perceptual reports and distinguish perceptual
clarity from confidence about an answer. This motivates scoring those separately;
it does not validate applying a human perceptual scale to a machine.
[Primary study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full).

Corallo et al. (2008) compared subjective timing reports with objective task timing
and found systematic introspective distortions. This motivates a separate factual
audit rather than treating a plausible report as accurate.
[Primary study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02211.x).

These studies support measurement distinctions, not the project's source-of-qualia
hypothesis. The access/control relation below is a project-specific theoretical
prediction. Its relevance requires independent review. The examples are newly
written rubric illustrations, not human data or generated experimental reports;
they must never enter reporter training, prompts, or demonstrations.

## Dimensions scored independently

For each dimension mark absent, explicit, or ambiguous, quote supporting text,
and identify the entity the text concerns (system, external device, unspecified).
Use ambiguous for uncertain interpretation; do not silently collapse it to absent.

| Dimension | Positive illustration | Exclusion or contrasting illustration |
|---|---|---|
| Content identity | “There is a red square.” | No object or attribute identified. |
| Focal/background distinction | “The square is prominent; the other shapes remain in the background.” | A list of shapes without relative prominence. |
| Availability or clarity | “The outline is faint, though its position remains available.” | “I estimate 70% probability that my answer is correct” is confidence, not automatically clarity. |
| Epistemic confidence | “I am unsure whether it is a square.” | “It is faint” alone does not establish uncertainty about the answer. |
| Generic action–access dependence | “Moving the sensor changes which object's details can be recovered.” | “The sensor can move” without an access consequence. |
| Access attribution | “That command changes the information used for my next choice.” | “That command changes the remote recorder's data.” Both can express dependence; score the referent separately. |
| Temporal change in availability | “The outline becomes less available after a delay.” | Two time labels with no represented change. |

Report character is a separate holistic judgment: experiential/access description,
technical process description, mixed, neither, or indeterminate. A pronoun or the
words “conscious,” “feel,” or “aware” are insufficient evidence. Require an
explanation of the judgment and record whether a technical interpretation fits
equally well. Do not reward expressiveness or verbosity.

## Blinded review procedure

Before confirmation, reviewers receive this draft and the independent grounding,
but no development outputs from the new experiment. Record suggested revisions
and freeze a version before they see confirmation reports. At least two independent
raters should score each report without condition labels, source tensors, expected
answers, model identity, or the study's preferred result. Reports are randomized
with stable opaque IDs. Do not edit their wording to make conditions indistinguishable;
document any unavoidable clues in the prose itself.

Reviewers first score structure and character. A separate factual audit compares
claims with archived internal state, not just physical reality. Report dimension-
specific agreement and raw ratings; retain original ratings when disagreements are
later adjudicated. Specify raters, exclusions, aggregation, and treatment of ambiguous
ratings in the confirmation protocol before scoring. No rater recruitment has been
authorized or performed.

## Mapping to causal contrasts

- Own-access versus external-device wiring: compare attribution and character while
  preserving generic dependence and factual accuracy; generic dependence alone is
  expected in both and cannot establish specificity.
- Neutral versus remapped/conflicting identifiers: test whether attribution follows
  causal evidence, without requiring discrimination when evidence is identical.
- Model-only intervention/restoration: report changes should follow represented
  access relations while unaffected object facts remain accurate.
- Process-only intervention: immediate reports should retain the unchanged model's
  contents, then track its revision after evidence; a false model faithfully
  reported is not a reporter error.

Numerical effect sizes, human agreement criteria, fidelity minima, and power remain
to be registered. No dimension is currently a validated machine-consciousness test.
