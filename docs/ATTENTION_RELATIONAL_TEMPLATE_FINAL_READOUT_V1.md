## Decision

Close this fixed analysis with its unknowns. Do not repeat raters, repair failed
firsts, tune the reference or launch another full-corpus prose audit to seek a pass.
The useful next development question is whether reports can describe actual native
content changes across allocation operations when provenance labels are removed,
with current estimates, conditional predictions and completed state kept distinct.
That directly tests the model-content route without asking origin labels to carry
the consciousness-related interpretation. Authoring such a relation into a template
would only check instrumentation; neutral reporting still needs independent factual
and semantic evaluation.

Fresh trained systems, a frozen meaningful effect/precision and sampling plan,
then confirmation and a different-task/architecture replication remain future work
under the [confirmation requirements](ATTENTION_RELATIONAL_CONFIRMATION_DESIGN.md).
Reporter and native Controller remain separate. Ordinary and analytic are also
attention-effect estimators; their parity is a valid result. Neither private
experience proof nor task necessity is a prerequisite, and the ultimate theory
goal remains active.
