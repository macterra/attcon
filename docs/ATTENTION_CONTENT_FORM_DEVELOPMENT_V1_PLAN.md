# Attention-dependent content form: development plan v1

2026-10-10. Prospective architecture/mechanism development, not a registered
evaluation, qualified presentation counterpart or positive qualia result.
The [source assessment](../audits/attention_source_bridge_design_v1/review.md)
finds no qualified native counterpart in the current source. The
[critical review](../audits/attention_source_bridge_design_v1/critical_review/review.md)
identifies a useful next mechanism test and the controls needed before execution.
Both originals remain preserved. This plan adopts their bounded scope and
critical amendments; it does not replace their negative strict-counterpart verdict.

The ultimate goal remains credible evidence that the attention-control model is
the source of qualia. This study addresses a missing bridge: native, content-specific
representational form that can change through attention operations while upstream
sensory evidence remains fixed. It is separate from the auxiliary factual pilot.
Prediction of a filter output alone cannot complete that goal.

## Define the states before testing them

Use fresh worlds with the existing frozen sensory encoders as engineering
components. Retain delivered spatial features V and separate identity evidence,
raw orientation coordinate and observed status. New spatial features are an
explicit source extension, not an unreported reinterpretation of the previous
hidden64 interface.

An executed internal allocation operation T constructs addressed current pattern
W from saved V. Preserve nonselected items. The initial mechanical family is
block averaging at bandwidths 1, 2 and 4, returning the original spatial shape.
Apply it to saved V, not cumulatively to a previously degraded W. Specify shape,
edge handling, channel units and all rounding before source evaluation. Lossy
averaging has no inverse; restoration uses exact saved state.

A new learned content-bound process model A has separately defined current-state
estimate `A_current(t)` and prospective estimate `A_next(t, command)`. Actual W(t)
and completed W(t+1) remain independently recorded. A future estimate cannot be
described as current presentation. Declare what observations update the current
estimate and prevent copying its scored target as a purported prediction. A
mistaken estimate remains an inspectable source state, with its disagreement from
actual W retained. Neither estimate is physical-world truth.

The Controller chooses and executes operations using predictions. The Reporter
is external diagnostic instrumentation. It does not choose attention operations
or generate W. Attention-model state A is the candidate report source; current
W and upstream V are competing information paths, not interchangeable names for it.

## Milestone 1: mechanical feasibility

Implement and check only the state/operation primitives using toy maps. Verify
address isolation, immutable copied inputs, original logit/evidence preservation,
distinct current/completed/prospective time semantics and saved-state restoration.
Include a non-attentional exogenous driver producing exactly the same W from V
under the same numerical operation. Its provenance stays audit-only.

Toy cases can establish that coarse spatial relations survive while a fine
arrangement changes; they do not qualify feature semantics in a trained encoder.
Reject malformed, nonfinite, unseen/unknown-address and invalid-bandwidth inputs.
Label a known-operator analytic baseline as such; it is not a learned model of
an unknown command mapping. No production inference, new fit, report or semantic
qualification belongs to this milestone.

## Milestone 2: source and semantic qualification

Before reserved evaluation or prose, specify one literal, addressed content relation
and one form property of that relation in the actual native W representation.
Validate their meaning on a declared development split using an independent native
content consumer or held-out relation readout, separate from A's prediction loss.
Use content changes and address remapping to distinguish item form from pooled
nuisance. A consumer introduced solely to restate a desired adjective is insufficient.

Separate form from stored-sample status, scalar correctness/confidence, original
pixels and generic activation energy. The preserved original logit controls only
that particular pathway: assess W-derived identity/confidence/access alternatives
as well. If the only defensible property is an activation statistic, record that
technical scope and retain **no qualified presentation counterpart**. Do not train
phenomenal adjectives, invented clarity bins, human ratings or report text into A.

An independently qualified model-representation counterpart need not prove felt
truth. Its claim is about how identified content is represented, and must support
the predeclared content/manner/separability rules. It does not authorize claims
about experienced blur, felt vividness or a physical stimulus's appearance.

## Milestone 3: register and execute a bounded mechanism study

Freeze extraction, T, timestamp/update contracts, unknown command mappings,
learning dimensions/optimizer/stopping, all inputs, targets, controls, split seeds,
world-level opportunity denominators, numerical tolerances and failure policy.
The proposal's 512 training, 256 development and 512 evaluation worlds per frozen
encoder is a budget candidate, not yet a registered count or power argument.
Freeze final numbers and any engineering margins before held-out outputs.

Build same-V randomized operations into the design instead of selecting convenient
examples after observing changes. Preserve every sham, uninformative transition,
wrong prediction and incompletely identified mapping. Compare current-estimation
and prospective-prediction losses separately, with whole-world paired uncertainty.

The central controls are:

- An ordinary process predictor with exactly the same V/current-state permissions,
  addresses, commands, completed transition history, feedback, targets and training
  budget. Publish residual capacity mismatch.
- A mapping estimator plus analytic T application with the same declared operator
  knowledge. This tests lookup/filter explanations without pretending an oracle
  mapping was learned.
- Sensory-only and confidence-only ablations, explicitly labelled as information
  omissions rather than equal-information source-specificity controls.
- W-derived task/confidence/access readouts and content/address crossings.
- A matched non-attentional internal transformation producing the same W, plus
  a separately assessed upstream transformation. Do not claim exact upstream
  matching if the encoder makes it infeasible.
- Sham and saved restoration, and secondary reachable A/W mismatch interventions
  with unchanged observations and explicit donor/address/time provenance.

Attention-conditioned held-out form prediction beyond upstream/scalar explanations
would support a restricted source-mechanism bridge. Ordinary/analytic parity is
a legitimate explanation and limits specificity. A direct source patch changing
exported predictions is an instrumentation effect, not proof of changed experience.

## Milestone 4: only then test neutral report correspondence

If source semantics qualify, freeze a new reporting version and a fixed, output-
independent world/query schedule. Keep current estimates, future predictions and
actual completed events separately attributed. Cross A and W, include direct-W
and exactly joinable exports, and give ordinary apparatus narrators the same
information and instructions. Preserve reports even if they are technical-only,
unsupported, refused, incomplete or fail to resemble the reference structure.

Use the qualified source-aware factual audit and separate source-blind structure
ratings. Report fidelity, P_access, P_presentation, model-presentation scope,
attention relation and unauditable experiential wording separately. Do not supply
the theory, examples, rubric, desired differences or phenomenal vocabulary to the
Reporter. A successful factual gate cannot replace semantic qualification.

Faithful independently recognized form descriptions following selective source
changes would support the restricted claim that attention-model state supplies
reportable content form. They would not uniquely exclude ordinary state estimation
or establish that A produces qualia. A rival allowed the identical computation
under a different name supplies no differing observable prediction.

## Stronger role, confirmation and the full goal

The [MSTC role audit](MSTC_REPORTING_ROLE_AUDIT_V1.md) distinguishes action correction
from Modeler refinement. A separately registered extension could compare predicted
and newly constructed content in common, fixed representation coordinates, then
use that discrepancy to update the underlying Modeler. Measure improvement on
later independent observations. Blocked, reversed and world-yoked feedback, a fixed
sensory schedule, Controller-only correction and equally informed ordinary
predictive updates are essential alternatives. This tests a comparison-medium
role; it does not certify that the medium is phenomenal.

After a positive qualified source/correspondence result, preregister a powered
theory-facing confirmation with fresh trained systems and meaningful independent
sampling, then replicate in another task or architecture. Retain negative source
semantics, ordinary parity and absent report correspondence as terminating study
outcomes. No success threshold can convert engineered compatibility into proof
of qualia. The full project goal remains unachieved until its evidence requirements
are actually met.

Commit and attempt authenticated `gh` publication at each milestone. Preserve
first passes before repairs/comparisons and retain publication failures explicitly.
