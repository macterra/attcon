## Outcome

All three frozen controllers and 15 fitted reporters were evaluated. No seed meets
the full reporting criteria. The model affects control and reports often follow
its mistaken beliefs, but those findings do not establish consciousness-report
correspondence beyond the supplied fields and wording. The theory remains
underdetermined. See [results](ATTENTION_MODEL_RESULTS.md).

All metrics and case files replay exactly, archive restoration passed, and the
full repository suite passed 160 tests. Initial numerical evaluations are retained;
the exact-95% correction did not alter the overall outcome.
