# Fresh confirmation and replication: design requirements

2026-10-19. Informed development plan, not a frozen confirmation registration or
runtime release. Its author knows the original aggregate reference table and
instrument failures. No pending V3 result or new V4 target response informed this
plan. No sample size, power claim or numeric theory-success gate is established.

The ultimate goal is **credible evidence for the theory that the attention-control
model is the source of qualia**. The proposed evidence is accurate reports of that
model's contents, independently characterized as resembling consciousness reports,
with selective causal attribution and relevant alternatives tested. Completing
engineering checks or this document does not achieve that goal. Reporter remains
external instrumentation, distinct from the native Controller.

## Evidence to confirm

Freeze the claim and estimand together before new confirmation outputs. For each
planned system/world/condition opportunity, assess separately:

1. Source fidelity: the complete report is supported by the supplied model state,
   including estimates, times, origins and conditional claims. A faithfully
   described mistaken estimate can qualify; agreement with external world truth
   is a different measurement. Correct peak lists cannot certify free prose.
2. Independently specified correspondence: content/status differentiation (COD)
   and content–attention relation (CA), with compatible referent, attribute and
   time. Preserve separate scopes for model organization, model access, claimed
   perceptual appearance, sensor property and uncertainty.
3. Source attribution: the evidence earning correspondence concerns actual
   attention-model estimates, or an explicit relation involving them, rather than
   copied prior W, comparison W, supplied labels or the narrator's unsupported
   invention. Preserve mixed and unresolved origins.

A candidate primary estimand is the system-averaged proportion of planned
opportunities with all three established, for a predeclared scope and endpoint.
COD and CA remain separately reported; registration must identify primary versus
secondary endpoints or a justified co-primary rule and multiplicity treatment.
Do not collapse model-access, organization and appearance into one stronger label.
Missing/invalid assessments mean failure to establish the conjunction, not absent
consciousness-like structure. Publish availability and each component alongside
that unconditional measurement-yield estimand; a valid-only rate is supplementary.
If estimating underlying feature prevalence, report its uncertainty from missing
measurements explicitly rather than treating missingness as known absence.

The corresponding causal estimand concerns source-specific tracking under matched
interventions. A baseline/donor pair should faithfully describe the changed
source property while retaining independently grounded correspondence and correct
unaffected facts; fixed-source W changes should preserve source assertions while
allowing W assertions to change. This does not require the COD/CA category itself
to switch when content changes: the same report structure can describe different
contents. Define tracking on attributed claims/properties, not wording changes.
Keep null source-property changes in their original denominator and distinguish
unavailable measurement from a true invariance result.

## Independent units and fresh data

The present twelve cases, repeated views, templates and reratings are development
data. They supply no new independent systems and cannot be relabelled confirmation.
The main generalization unit is a freshly trained system. Worlds nest within
systems; time points, source views, interventions, extracted facts, repeated model
calls and rater judgments nest within worlds. Pair controls within each system.

Specify what is freshly trained: encoder/native readout and attention predictor,
training-world namespace, initialization and fixed fit procedure. If the encoder
or readout is reused, state that the estimand is conditional on that shared
component and account for its clustering. Several predictor fits on one encoder
are not automatically independent complete systems. Use fresh training/evaluation
world namespaces and an output-independent sampling schedule; disclose recurring
layouts/operator families and the resulting domain limit. Retain failed fits and
failed measurements under registered dispositions; do not keep drawing systems
until the retained sample looks favorable.

## Power and uncertainty before choosing N

First choose the smallest effect or precision that would materially strengthen
the theory-facing claim, its comparator, admissible uncertainty, and inferential
scope. No defensible powered sample size exists before those choices. Do not
estimate the meaningful effect by selecting the largest observed development
contrast. Development can estimate feasibility, missingness, annotator error and
within-system dependence, with substantial uncertainty retained.

Then evaluate candidate numbers of independent systems and nested worlds using
an explicitly specified clustered model/simulation or appropriate system-level
paired test. Include plausible between-system variation, within-world pairing,
rater dependence, ceiling/null cases and instrument failure. Compare sensitivity
across a range of effect/dependence assumptions; disclose if the budget cannot
resolve a meaningful effect. More correlated views cannot replace more systems.
Freeze the chosen estimand, inference method, confidence/credible intervals,
multiplicity, target effect/precision, N, world budget and stopping rules together
before confirmation. Do not adapt them to confirmation labels. Report model-level
results and uncertainty, not only pooled assertion totals.

## Frozen measurement and controls

Before confirmation, demonstrate that the chosen factual instrument detects
planted wrong facts, time/origin substitutions, unsupported experience/clarity
and omitted compound assertions. Independently check the reference's semantic
application on held-out examples, not merely its quote/schema mechanics. Preserve
first-pass disagreements and uncertain cases. Separate-context AI reviewers are
authorized; shared training and project commissioning limit independence. Human
participation is optional. Reference examples and desired vocabulary remain
withheld from the neutral Reporter; source-aware auditors and source-blind raters
remain separate. Freeze a whole measurement-version policy, never a better
response or auditor selected separately for each item.

Retain source-only and joined views, reachable model-state interventions at fixed
world state, W changes at fixed exported source, exact source restoration, shams,
null effects, copied-record provenance, unaffected-content checks, neutral-ID
remapping and leakage checks. Establish the source's actual command/control role
without requiring that the current estimate be the native Controller's direct
input or that consciousness be necessary for task performance. If two visible
inputs are identical, expected semantic invariance is a control, not a demand
that a Reporter infer hidden wiring. Preserve all first requests/responses,
refusals, capped outputs, unknown in-flight attempts and registered denominators.

Compare alternative attention-effect models fairly. The current ordinary model
uses the same architecture as A, independently initialized; analytic models the
same allocation-command effects through a different inference algorithm. Neither
is an attention-model-absent control. Their parity can limit dependence on the
particular fit or learned realization without logically disqualifying genuine
correspondence to attention-model contents. It cannot by itself identify or
exclude an attention-model source of experience. Declare actual information and
capacity differences: richer W and reduced sensor controls are not equally
informed estimators. Literal templates and generic apparatus descriptions test
shared content/language explanations. Template resemblance alone does not locate
qualia; equally informed alternative attention models need not be outperformed.

## Replication and warranted interpretation

Register a fresh replication after the confirmation outcome is retained. Use
fresh trained systems and at least one substantively different task or model
architecture; doing both provides a stronger scope test. Preserve the theoretical
endpoint and source-tracking prediction while prospectively specifying any
necessary source-semantic/interface translation. Qualify the new task's content
semantics without tuning it to produce experiential phrasing. Prespecify the
replication estimand, meaningful effect/precision and independent-unit budget;
apply the same uncertainty discipline. Another narrator pass over the old corpus,
a checkpoint rename or a new fit of the identical measurement is not this
cross-task/architecture replication. Publish negative and mixed outcomes too.

Credible support would combine reproducible, source-faithful correspondence,
selective content tracking, interpretable control comparisons and uncertainty
small enough for the registered claim. A model-organization analogue is evidence
at that limited level, not automatically claimed appearance. Appearance wording
alone is not represented appearance; unsupported felt assertions cannot supply
fidelity. A stronger appearance claim needs an independently warranted native
counterpart. Private experience remains unverified, and general language learning,
ordinary causal description, shared-model evaluation and other implementations
remain possible explanations. Their surviving scope must accompany any positive
claim; no operational pattern uniquely proves a quale-generating locus.

Do not introduce private-experience proof, task necessity, mandatory humans or
Modeler refinement as blanket prerequisites. The stronger MSTC comparison/refinement
hypothesis is a separate extension. Nor declare the full goal achieved from
report accuracy or a broad analogue alone: explicitly evaluate whether the
confirmed and replicated evidence supports the proposed qualia-source connection
at the stated level and what rival interpretation remains sufficient.

The original measurement stays primary for its original study. V3's quote-grounded
reference assessment and its preselected whole resource-v2 factual join remain
separate, including unestablished fidelity. Any V4 factual/reference join is a
new supplementary development version declared before its outputs. Neither
replaces the original, pools cases as independent evidence, repairs old judgments
or completes confirmation/replication. The next concrete deliverable is one
bounded confirmation registration after measurement feasibility is actually
known, followed by its retained outcome and the separately registered replication.
