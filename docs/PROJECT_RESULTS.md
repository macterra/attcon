# Current project results

**The registered object-content reporting goal is achieved in the engineered test.**
The system reports objects and their relations within the bound attention-control
model. Reports follow selective internal changes while the physical scene stays
fixed. The source-of-qualia theory remains an interpretation to assess, not a
conclusion that subjective experience has been demonstrated.

Fresh confirmation v5 uses three model pairs, 24 underlying episodes, and 240
reports across ten conditions. Every registered criterion passes:

- Checked color/shape accuracy: 99.11–100%; identified-object coverage: 99.06–100%.
- Focality: 98.24–100%; most-recoverable attribution and temporal direction: 100%.
- Control attribution: 95.39–99.75%; explicit command destinations: 100%.
- Conservative precision, including unresolved evidence: 99.18–99.96%.
- Paired object relations: 461/480 (96.04%); restoration: 93/96 (96.88%).
- Missing-component controls: 48/48 without flagged inventions or unresolved evidence.
- All four registered report-structure features: 48/48 ordinary/binding reports.
- Character: nine subjective-access, 36 mixed, three technical-process reports.

The broad character label alone is nonspecific: 17/24 visual-only controls also
receive a mixed label. Those controls have no graded/temporal-access or agency
relation evidence, so the full conjunction of structure and fidelity matters.
These are separate-model automated judgments, not human ratings. Conditions
within an episode are correlated, and the audit does not check every implication
of unrestricted prose.
