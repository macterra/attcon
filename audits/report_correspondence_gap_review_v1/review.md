# Report-correspondence gap: first-pass methods review

2026-10-10. Reviewer `/root/theory_endpoint_gap_review`. Unblinded AI methods review; a proposal for development, not a frozen protocol, report rating, author endorsement or completed experiment.

## Verdict

**A qualitative operational report-correspondence assay can proceed without participant trial-level transfer.** The current plan has made a defensible prerequisite for a particular psychophysical claim into an unnecessary prerequisite for the user's broader report-correspondence goal. That correction does not make existing factual reports successful consciousness-related reports. Existing zero-appearance findings, failed calibrators and all other historical verdicts remain unchanged. The actual goal remains unachieved.

The unnecessary dependency is explicit in the interpretation plan's statements that actual archive qualification/transfer precedes the full reporting goal, in the requirements audit's final next step, and in the grounded-outcomes interpretation review's requirement to acquire immutable external trial/stimulus bytes first. These should distinguish two endpoints:

| Proposed claim | Necessary external evidence |
|---|---|
| Fresh faithful reports express an independently documented qualitative consciousness-report pattern | Primary publications documenting report procedures, category meanings or actual report descriptions; a provenance-qualified reference specification and semantic measurement. Trial rows and native stimuli are not inherently necessary. |
| Machine responses reproduce participant contrast-dependent clarity/confidence distributions, apparent-contrast shifts, psychometric parameters or attention effects under transferred stimuli | Qualified trial/stimulus/task files, participant units, exclusions, corrections, original-analysis reproduction and valid sensory conversion, or another explicitly justified numerical reference design. The presently proposed trial-level transfer cannot be claimed without those files. |
| An attention-control representation causally contributes the relevant represented presentation/content relation | Selective source interventions with sensory, memory, Controller and Reporter controls. Neither reference type establishes this automatically. |

Published summary estimates can motivate a hypothesis; they do not become trial rows. No claim that all psychophysical research universally requires archived rows is intended. The missing archive matters to the project's particular transfer/individual-trial analysis, rather than to every qualitative endpoint.

## Independent reference already available

The strongest additional primary source I verified is **Ramsøy and Overgaard (2004), Introspection and subliminal perception**, DOI `10.1023/B:PHEN.0000041900.30172.e8`. Their procedure asks for shape, color and location guesses and separately each property's experienced clarity. Table 2 summarizes post-experiment category descriptions. Page 12 gives an author-described typical report in which location is clear, color is glimpsed and shape is not experienced. This supports feature-bound presentation descriptions with graded/partial content, distinguished from an answer. It is one typical report description and generalized interview categories, not an acquired transcript corpus or a reference frequency distribution. The [author-hosted PDF](https://tzramsoy.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/01/ramsc3b8y-overgaard-2004-introspection-and-subliminal-perception.pdf) is marked **uncorrected proof**; the matching methods/table/example were also checked in [author-uploaded typeset text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/216733480_Introspection_and_Subliminal_Perception). Before registration, preserve the exact used version and verify final-version wording. The publication's scale development was instructed; this is not evidence of unprompted report emergence.

[Tagliabue et al. (2016), Methods, EEG Experiment](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full) documents a brightness choice followed by a separate graded perception-quality response, plus an end-of-session category-criteria questionnaire. It supports channel separation; it does not supply unrestricted participant narratives or machine clarity values. Its commentary distinguishes clarity from confidence, but that experiment did not collect an additional confidence scale. Do not claim independent empirical dissociation of all three from it.

[Liu, Abrams and Carrasco (2009), Procedure and control condition](https://npal.psy.msu.edu/wp-content/uploads/Publications/Liu_2009_PsychSci.pdf) documents voluntary attention directed through RSVP and a contrast-contingent orientation response, including reversed higher/lower contrast instructions. It motivates an optional attention-linked apparent-attribute comparison and a response-rule control. It does not establish that access probability means perceived contrast, or derive an own-versus-external model prediction. The earlier reference review retains the 2010/2011 criterion dispute; I did not newly verify the 2011 full reanalysis, and a direct PubMed open returned a browser challenge.

These publications are independent of this project. Their selection and the proposed joint endpoint are project decisions. A literature-grounded operational endpoint is possible now; a validated human free-prose distribution is still unavailable. Those are different validity levels.

## Strongest next executable development assay

Register a **qualitative represented-presentation correspondence and source-dissociation pilot**. Start with the frozen grounded-outcome archive and clearly separated upstream sensory instrumentation. A diagnostic addressed joint serialization can expose its existing memory, risk and route relations; it must not be described as a newly learned bound appearance model. The older frozen categorical bound-content system is a separate source family, suitable for a separately analyzed extension; do not fuse its checkpoints/worlds with the new archive or imply a comparison is matched across those families. Do not train a new appearance head or add human appearance targets merely to produce the desired result. No production inference or Reporter generation was performed for this review.

The experiment should have a source-validity stage and a fresh-report stage under one pre-output design. An honest failure at the first stage is a completed negative development result, not permission to rename forecasts and continue until prose sounds conscious.

### Exact independent endpoint

For each prospectively selected state/query, define a blind-text core match `K = C AND P AND D`:

- `C`: a specified represented content or addressed visual feature, including explicit absence/partial specification when appropriate.
- `P`: a content-bound description of its represented manner of presentation or apparent perceptual attribute, with modality, referent and scope resolved. A contrast in presentation between features/locations earns the same criterion when stated explicitly.
- `D`: a separately scoped identification/decision claim or explicit no-answer status. Presentation must not simply be asserted as another name for answer correctness or identity certainty.

This is my operational synthesis of documented report structure, not a composite those papers validated. First-person grammar, consciousness words, good prose, uncertainty alone, selection ranking, recovery, correctness forecasts and mere willingness to answer cannot supply `P`. A literal pixel-property report earns a physical-description annotation, not automatically `P`. Record claimed felt experience separately; its truth is not auditable from this interface.

Independently source-aware auditors assign `F`: every content/presentation/decision assertion and asserted relation is supported by the declared source at that time and modality. The primary descriptive outcome is `Q = count(K AND F)/N`, where `N` is **all preselected report opportunities**, including omissions, unsupported outputs and opportunities with no qualified presentation source. Also publish `count(K)/N`, ambiguity bounds, unsupported-claim rates, and the number failing source validity before generation. Conditional summaries for source-qualified cases are secondary; they must not hide absence of a presentation representation. No success percentage is invented by this review; a development pilot estimates feasibility, and confirmation requires a justified meaningful-effect rule before fresh confirmation data.

Keep an optional attention extension separate: an explicit relative-attention/access relation and a presentation comparison for the same addressed content. Source attribution and generic action dependence are auxiliaries; their presence cannot substitute for the core.

### Source-validity stage: what could support `P`

Freeze a source inventory and identify what each candidate actually represents. Current grounded-outcome heads represent delivered location and decision success. Their names and calibration cannot qualify presentation. The earlier bound state contains category distributions and access relations; posterior uncertainty alone does not resolve perceptual ambiguity versus uncertainty about a crisp stimulus. An unsupported interpretation must remain unsupported.

An appearance head is not logically required: a distributed source could retain relevant represented sensory quality. Test that possibility with existing retained tensors and a prospectively bounded factual decoder/semantic audit, without participant appearance labels. Validate decoded properties and their source addresses on held-out physical variants and donor-state interventions; separately test whether the interpretation is only stimulus reconstruction or decision uncertainty. Physical contrast/edge recovery establishes physical signal, not a phenomenological scale. A candidate represented-presentation claim needs a defensible account of what the system's retained perceptual representation depicts and why the report describes that depiction rather than world truth, risk or a decoder's newly invented display.

The physical-source results make the upstream visual hidden representation a useful positive **physical-information** control and the history-only recurrent attention source an important invariance control. Hidden features are not presently part of the production categorical binding. Exposing them to instrumentation is a declared diagnostic source extension, never a silent enlargement of the attention model or a positive attention-origin result. If no existing source qualifies `P`, record source sufficiency failed for this frozen system. That is a precise remaining gap, not a reason to demand unavailable participant files.

**Concrete bridge worth implementing first:** qualify objective sensory-property information in the frozen grounded model's `risk_hidden` and equal-information `joint_hidden` on fresh generated worlds. Preserve the grounded model and sensory weights; fit bounded diagnostic probes only on new source-development training/calibration splits. Targets are contrast of the latest actually delivered image, independently computed image edge measures, and its orientation, kept in the evaluator archive. Future latent contrast is not a valid target for an unseen query. Use a held-out whole-world evaluation split, record seen status and actual delivery time, and compare the same probe budget on sensory hidden, raw orientation logits, calibrated risk outputs, history/status, and address-aware joint inputs. Report contrast-conditional errors and whether quality decoding persists within predeclared orientation/logit/risk bins; dimensional/basis differences require explicit controls. Do not import failed sensory calibrators or select worlds by a favorable probe outcome.

This bridge is executable without human trials. Its result is **physical/source sufficiency**, not `P` or qualia correspondence: the risk network was trained for correctness and consumes sensory/logit features, so quality information may be inherited sensory evidence associated with decision risk. Decoding contrast from its hidden units does not show that the model represents how contrast appears, or that the control model generates it. A neutral Reporter may faithfully state a diagnostic estimate of represented image properties, separately from answer/access; that is valuable but cannot automatically earn the qualitative primary. Presentation prose earns `P` only after its scope and source interpretation pass the separate semantic validity assessment. If that assessment cannot distinguish physical estimation from represented perceptual presentation, publish the physical result and mark correspondence unestablished.

Holding the two risk-head values fixed while intervening on hidden units may be algebraically possible, but it is not automatically a valid selective source intervention. Check natural donor support, other decoded content/property changes, calibration/context consistency and exact restoration. A probe chosen to map the altered latent directly into presentation words would build the claimed causal effect into instrumentation. Freeze its factual mapping independently, keep the Reporter fixed, and require natural-state evidence as well as intervention effects. A successful hidden intervention would locate the report channel's information dependence; additional evidence must still distinguish a control-model presentation role from a sensory projection or an external diagnostic visualization.

### Roles, sampling and causal controls

The proposed source remains the attention-control model, with its explicitly specified bound-content variant distinguished from the narrow route/history estimator. Sensory encoding and memory supply candidate contents. The Controller selects/executes actions; retain its independent emitted decision and forecast provenance. The Reporter only describes supplied internal state. It does not become the purported experiencing system or infer unobserved world truth. The evaluator alone receives labels, branch correctness and physical parameters.

An executable pilot budget is the first 16 evaluation worlds per grounded model at snapshot 8: 48 whole worlds conditional on the three checkpoints. Query the lowest-index observed location and, where present, its next observed comparison location, selected solely by observed status/index. Keep an absent comparison explicit rather than replacing the world. Freeze this selection before extracting new presentation judgments. These are naturally reached **seen-content** states. Use every chosen state, including zero contrast, ties and uncertain content. Retain world/model dependence; 48 worlds is a development budget, not a certified power calculation. The earlier unseen-query gain reduces to routing with constant risk inputs and is unsuitable as a presentation primary. New report inputs may come from archived grounded states; the new model has no prior Reporter outputs. Historical full reports must not be relabeled as fresh evaluation.

For each state, compare source views with the same factual task/answer context and fixed readout budget: sensory/memory only; attention/route/risk only; declared addressed bound source; and equal-information ordinary diagnostic narration. Source masks are information ablations, not architectural superiority tests. More expressive bases or added hidden channels must be disclosed and matched where a comparison claims equal information.

Freeze neutral reporting instructions and glossary from factual source meanings. Keep the literature, target categories, synthetic positive examples, evaluators' appearance judgments and desired adjectives out of Reporter training and prompts. Asking directly for the target PAS pattern can be an explicitly secondary elicitation experiment; it cannot demonstrate spontaneous source-origin emergence. Auditors should qualify on held-out supportedness and feature-boundary cases before seeing fresh target outputs. Use separately contextualized source-aware fidelity auditors and randomized source-blind character raters, retaining first passes and disagreements.

Cross the following interventions, saving exact originals and restorations:

1. Change sensory evidence with route/history fixed: does a candidate presentation claim track upstream sensory representation? This may locate a sensory source and limit the attention-origin claim.
2. Change represented attention/access at fixed delivered pixels and memory; record any change in actual risk/decision context. Require the proposed presentation outcome itself to change for a selective presentation-causality claim. Changed command forecasts, attribution sentences or report length alone do not suffice. If risk cannot be held fixed, use crossed/matched risk controls and explicitly retain the unresolved mediator.
3. Change binding/addressing with sensory and attention marginals fixed: do content and presentation referents relocate coherently? This tests integration/addressing, not generation of a new qualitative property.
4. Change Controller decision rule/answer while source evidence is fixed; the answer clause should change without a spurious corresponding presentation change. Test a conflicting label and identifier remapping with fixed source precedence.
5. With byte-identical Reporter-visible inputs, change hidden evaluator condition; reports should be invariant. Also compare remapped functional evidence versus supplied names. A Reporter cannot identify inaccessible wiring.

Freeze predictions for present versus prospective presentation separately. Rotating a future route forecast need not change present sensory depiction; demanding that it do so would invent a theory prediction. Acute donor/intervention states may be inconsistent or out of distribution; retain natural-state results separately and do not interpret incoherence as absence of qualia.

For causality, analyze paired changes in the specific faithful `P` assertion/value/referent and core match, not just whether the whole paragraph changes. Restoration must recover source tensors and derived factual inputs exactly; deterministic instrumentation should restore its output, while stochastic prose needs a fixed sampling design or distributional comparison. Neither success nor necessity for task performance is a primary requirement.

## What the outcomes would mean

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Accurate technical reports; no source-supported `P` | Fidelity achieved, qualitative correspondence absent for this interface/system. Preserve the negative result. |
| Target-like presentation words unsupported by source, or induced only by target wording | Wording/interpretation leakage; no faithful correspondence. |
| Faithful core matches, also found in ordinary apparatus narration | Operational correspondence demonstrated at the declared level; specificity limited. Do not erase correspondence or claim qualia. |
| Core matches arise only from upstream sensory instrumentation | Correspondence located in the sensory/readout path; no selective attention-source support. |
| Binding changes only which content is named | Address integration evidence, not evidence that attention generates presentation quality. |
| Presentation changes follow answer criterion/risk rather than the controlled attention relation | Decision/uncertainty alternative remains viable. |
| Faithful presentation/core changes track selective attention-source changes, survive label controls and restore, with matched alternatives | Incremental causal evidence for the declared operational report mechanism; still not unique identification of experience or a proof of qualia. |
| No interpretable source distinction or poor rater qualification | Uninterpretable assay, not a substantive consciousness verdict. |

The immediate missing items are therefore a version-qualified qualitative reference record, an accepted precise semantic endpoint, a source-supported interpretation of represented presentation distinct from risk/physical reconstruction, a qualified annotation procedure, and identifiable source contrasts. Participant rows and native stimulus conversion remain missing for the separate psychophysical endpoint. They do not block registering and executing this qualitative development assay. A powered confirmation and broader replication come after feasibility; no positive outcome should be forced.
