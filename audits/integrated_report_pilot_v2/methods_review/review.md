# Methods review: fidelity and appearance correspondence

Date: 2026-10-10. Fresh AI methods review; no report generation, factual adjudication, rescoring, or protocol/model changes.

## Finding

The conjunction is **logically possible but not yet measurement-ready for the current appearance candidate**. Strict factual auditing does not forbid appearance language. It forbids unsupported assertions about what that language describes. However, if success requires every proposition to be source-supported **and** an assertion of actual felt appearance, the current information boundary makes that particular conjunction unattainable: subjective experience is not measured. This is an evidential constraint, not proof that the model lacks experience.

The broader operational target can remain coherent: independently grounded report structure, faithful description of a demonstrably represented presentation property, and causal attribution to the attention-control model. That target does not require certifying experience. The reviewed documents correctly separate these claims, but they have not demonstrated an admissible, source-supported presentation-manner proposition for the current export. A joint text-feature count alone cannot resolve that gap.

This review makes no judgment about any target report or earlier outcome. The permitted interpretation plan contains embedded historical summaries; those were encountered as context, not independently checked.

## Four claims that must stay separate

| Claim | What would support it | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A source-state proposition is true | Identified tensor/export entry, defined semantics, correct entity/time/modality | Category distributions, modeled allocation/access/effects, and supplied histories can support appropriately scoped claims, including mistaken model predictions. |
| A text states a manner of presentation | Exact wording with content and referent, assessed blind | Rubric 3 can score such language whether or not its proposition is true. The score is not factual support. |
| The described property originates in the attention-control model | Selective model intervention, corresponding state/readout change, restoration, and upstream/Reporter controls | Tracking an exported access forecast locates an access-description source; it does not by itself locate the source of appearance. |
| The model has subjective experience | Evidence beyond the measured functional/report properties, with explicit inferential limits | Neither factual auditing nor linguistic resemblance verifies this claim. Reporter first-person wording does not transfer experience to the model. |

The Controller selects and executes actions. The Reporter is external instrumentation. Neither task performance nor fluent reporting must require consciousness. Ordinary readout success is therefore compatible with the intended experiment; it remains an alternative explanation of the report evidence.

A phrase scoped to represented availability can be a faithful qualitative description if its meaning is fixed and warranted by the source. It cannot receive felt-clarity support merely by using an appearance adjective. Conversely, display/sensor appearance could be a truthful presentation claim if measured, but its source would initially be the display/sensor, not automatically the attention-control model. Reporting that a phrase was uttered is also not support for its object-level assertion.

## What the code permits and what it does not establish

In `bound_content.py`, the renderer varies image intensity and geometry and adds noise. The visual encoder is trained/output-configured to produce color and shape distributions. Binding links those distributions to locations; remembered status gates unknown contents. Its earlier hidden features might retain other image information, but this review did not inspect trained weights or test that possibility.

In `functional_model.py`, acquisition quality scales a glimpse, recovery persists/decays, and `buffer_contents` mixes categorical distributions with a uniform distribution according to recovery. That operation changes categorical uncertainty. It is not a calibrated model of apparent contrast, brightness, blur, or felt clarity. The neutral record exports categorical distributions and command/acquisition histories; it does not supply a separate appearance judgment. The bound-content full record likewise exports category, selection, recovery, and effects.

Therefore:

- Absence of a dedicated appearance head is **not an architectural impossibility**. A distributed representation could carry a presentation variable without a named head. A newly named head alone would not establish qualia.
- For explicit brightness/blur claims, the reviewed interfaces have a **source-information/semantic limitation**: no independently established appearance measurement is exposed.
- Whether relevant information exists in learned internal states is **untested here**. Raw image intensity variation establishes physical stimulus variation, not perceived brightness or an attention-model representation of it.
- Even a successful hidden-state probe could locate upstream sensory information rather than the proposed source. The attention-control model must be tested separately from the visual encoder and from Reporter-generated interpretation.

Identical reporter-visible inputs cannot support different condition-specific claims. They are an invariance control. All three wiring conditions contain attention-control models, so own/external/decoupled contrasts do not remove the hypothesized substrate; a superiority prediction needs an additional theoretical argument.

## Evidential risks in the present correspondence proposal

The reference-grounding document provides reasons to distinguish identified content, appearance, and answer judgment. It is not a validated prose reference distribution. Constrained perceptual-scale categories and contrast judgments do not establish the frequency or specificity of a free-prose pattern. The documented appearance-versus-decision-criterion dispute further limits importing an attention/appearance causal interpretation.

Published studies are independent of this project; selection of those studies and the feature mapping is not independent of project development. Fresh-context AI review reduces local exposure, but shared training, commissioning, and common interpretive habits remain correlated. No linked publication was read in this review, so its characterization is assessed only as the grounding document presents it.

Ordinary apparatus descriptions can include content, uncertainty, faint imagery, temporal access, and command effects. Reference matching can be real despite that overlap, but then its likelihood under an ordinary-description explanation is also high. It supplies limited evidence about the source-of-qualia hypothesis. Genre cues are another route: neutral JSON plus a technical reporting request encourages technical prose, while a perceptual-rating task encourages appearance language. Comparing those genres without matching task/instruction constraints confounds representation with elicitation.

Model interventions show that report information depends on a model state. General language pretraining and an engineered uncertainty bridge can still explain how that state becomes prose. Neither paraphrasing nor adding phenomenological labels generates independent evidence.

## Next concrete falsifiable measurement

Recommend a **new, separately registered frozen-model source-sufficiency assay**, before further appearance-seeking prose. This is a proposal, not a change to pilot 2 or permission to reinterpret its data.

1. **Qualify the external target first.** Select a held-out, independently produced participant-level appearance dataset, with stimuli, appearance judgments, task answers, and available uncertainty/confidence measures. Use archived data; new human recruitment is not required. Independent reviewers must determine whether it actually supports an identified-content/appearance/answer dissociation, and measure overlap with matched apparatus descriptions. Freeze feature extraction, provenance, exclusions, reliability criteria, and acceptance rules before testing new model data. If suitable source data are unavailable, record that limitation; published scale labels are not a substitute for an empirical prose corpus.
2. **Test representation without language targets.** On a fixed development/evaluation split, vary physical contrast/intensity or edge degradation independently of modeled selection/recovery. Retain content identity and match category certainty/task response where feasible; explicitly measure residual differences rather than assume matching. Use stimuli from the independent dataset where a documented, fixed input conversion is valid. Objective image metrics are calibration targets; participant appearance judgments are the separate reference target. Never equate those two.
3. **Locate the signal.** Fit a capacity-limited diagnostic readout on development data only, with no appearance adjectives, rubric examples, or consciousness targets in Reporter prompts. Evaluate held-out appearance judgments from attention-model states, visual-encoder states, and information-matched category/access/history baselines. Test selective attention-model interventions and exact restoration while holding upstream visual content fixed; separately perturb upstream content. Where feasible match instantaneous category/access distributions so any claimed additional signal is not just uncertainty. Predeclare estimand, independence unit, meaningful effect, uncertainty, and a fixed stopping budget.
4. **Interpret both outcomes.** Failure to predict a held-out appearance dissociation beyond physical metrics/identity/decision baselines means this proposed target is not supported by this frozen representation/readout; it does not prove absence of experience. Signal only upstream supports a sensory-source account. A selective attention-model signal surviving held-out tests and restoration establishes a candidate represented property with a measurable source. It still permits ordinary functional explanation, but it makes a subsequent faithful report-correspondence experiment answerable.

If the physical and modeled factors cannot be independently varied, or the reference variables cannot be separated, the assay fails its identifiability gate before prose collection. If objective appearance-related information is decoded but independent appearance judgments are not, report only physical-feature representation.

Only after that measurement should a new report protocol expose a validated neutral measurement/readout alongside content and task state, without desired wording. A prospective source audit could then check a represented-property claim, while a separate blind evaluator measures independent-reference correspondence and ordinary-description overlap. The audit would still leave subjective experience unverified. Existing reports, frozen pilot rules, and historical verdicts remain untouched.
