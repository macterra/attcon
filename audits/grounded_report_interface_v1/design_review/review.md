## First pass: correction required before source-interface approval

The factual source layout is broadly faithful to the registered physical study, but this draft is **not yet approved** at the first-pass hashes below. Four substantive boundary requirements and a legend-neutrality concern remain. These are validator/helper gaps reproduced on generated toy sources; they are not evidence that immutable production archives contain incorrect data. No production target view was constructed or serialized.

1. **Readdressed external readout association is unchecked.** Changing one `memory_readdressed` physical estimate by `+10` passes `validate_bundle` and `record`. Memory and risk/current/refresh permutations are checked, while that variant's physical tensor is only required to be finite and correctly shaped. Verify its association to the risk decoder, or verify every output discrepancy against the frozen elementwise feature-roundoff propagation envelope plus `1e-12`. The real physical source allows measured float roundoff; requiring bitwise physical permutation would reintroduce the corrected pre-output mistake. Hash-verified loading currently restricts archives, but the direct public bundle/record validator does not enforce its advertised source fidelity.
2. **Table joining does not enforce its whitelist.** A duplicate risk-table address is silently collapsed by a dictionary. An extra `physical_targets` field is carried into the joined location node. Require exactly four unique canonical addresses and exact allowed fields at each table boundary; reject unexpected fields rather than forwarding them. This matters if the draft's same-information helper becomes part of a future exporter. Records produced directly by `record` do not currently contain this injected field.
3. **The full opportunity rule is not enforced by `record`.** `selection_indices` rejects fewer than sixteen worlds, but `record(fixture(n=15), 0)` succeeds. Require the complete fixed first-sixteen opportunity set before constructing any record.
4. **Use a neutral task/image source legend.** Reporter-visible wording includes “felt confidence” and “native presentation or confidence fields.” Definitions of represented orientation decisions, estimated task success and external image statistics suffice. Move theory-facing caveats into audit provenance/inventory. This concern was first raised by the parent before my edge fixture; that influence is disclosed. It is a prospective prompt-contamination concern, not an observed Reporter effect.

The inventory also needs a wording correction: the direct evaluator `command_mapping` field is excluded, but native observed command-delivery rows, and even more explicitly the audit chronology, permit parts of the mapping to be inferred. Claim exclusion of the direct truth field, rather than absence of mapping information. Observed histories are legitimate source information here.
