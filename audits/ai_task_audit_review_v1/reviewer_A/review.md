## Needed prospective rules

Freeze separate domains for query addresses, selected commands, execution markers, observed chronology, response status, emitted attribute values, modeled identification and physical correctness. Specify whether address components are scored separately or as a bound relation; preserve both in inventories regardless. Define the meanings of pending, answered and joint abstained, and keep source null, field absence, withholding and report omission distinct.

Define causal/explanatory assertions separately from their factual consequents and name the evidential source each may use. Define decoding, numerical confidence and correctness endpoints explicitly. Decide how to match inventories containing extra explanatory rows, how to retain ambiguity and how to handle repeated propositions before comparison or scoring. Inspect missing and extra claims, not only matched convenient rows.
