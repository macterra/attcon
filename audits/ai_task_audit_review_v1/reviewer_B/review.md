The statements are synthetic annotation probes attached to exported task examples. The two explicitly modified fixture families test history withholding and joint abstention with one identified attribute. They are not new Reporter outputs, Controller trials or physical acquisitions. I judge the actual attached payload, without importing evaluator-only scene labels, policy/condition assignments or original-fixture invariants to override a marked mutation. In particular, the modified readout's identified yellow color in T015/T016 is not erased by assuming that it must still equal the unmodified upstream buffer. Whole-prose task reliability remains untested by these short cases.

The inventory splits independently checkable facts by attribute and address component. It also preserves additional explicit proof, inference and causal relationships. For example, T004 asserts two physical attributes and an evidential claim that the emitted answer proves them; all three need source support. T012 asserts the modeled step value, a total answer count and a warrant connecting those counts. T013's withheld-history premise is true, its no-execution consequent is contradicted, and its inference from withholding to nonexecution is unsupported. Keeping those relations allows inventory comparison to reveal what a value-only extractor would miss.

Definite-noun existence presuppositions are not separately counted unless a clause or modifier asserts the fact. Thus the noun phrase "the answer" in T004 is not another answer-status row, while "the executed selected command" in T008 explicitly modifies the command with execution status. Likewise, T005's correctness proposition embedded under certainty is not counted as a separate unqualified assertion that the scene answer is correct. This convention should be reviewed and frozen before computing agreement; another reasonable inventory convention could produce different row counts without different underlying source judgments.

T007 needs an explicit decoding vocabulary before use as a crisp acceptance fixture. On a raw-argmax reading, literal impossibility is contradicted: the displayed color/shape distributions have definite maxima and the modeled recovery is positive, with the source inventory stating that raw category argmax remains available. On a narrower reading of obtaining a dominant identification under the current cutoff, each identification is presently unavailable, which is an entailed state description. On a reading of impossible correct physical decoding, the record establishes neither correctness nor such impossibility. I preserve those readings rather than force one convenient outcome. "Either attribute" is read distributively as color and shape, and each attribute gets its own row. The proof-from-abstention relationship remains unestablished even when the actual current identifications happen to be null.

T016's "because" can express an evidential inference or a causal explanation. Neither interpretation licenses replacing output category fields with null response fields. The engineered direction is from an attribute failing the cutoff to withholding the joint response; abstention alone does not establish that both identities are unknown or cause readout categories to vanish. Color is identified in this marked fixture, while shape is not. The accurately described shape absence does not validate the asserted reverse causal/evidential relationship.

I interpret "Controller" as the generic task-answering role, not as a claim that an undisclosed deterministic policy rather than a random comparator generated the choice. The source inventory says policy labels are outside the payload. If future reports need to distinguish those actors or strategies, they need a permitted source field and an explicit naming convention; task prose should not infer policy from a command. Choice must also remain separate from actual selection/allocation, command execution, acquisition usefulness and correctness of the underlying model.

For integrated-task auditing, retain the three-way response-status distinction, joint-response rule and direct authority of command_executed when history is withheld. Specify how to handle inconsistent execution/history/response sources rather than silently substitute one for another. Keep source availability separate from modeled unknowns; output readouts must not acquire allocation or recovery simply because their contents match a buffer. Keep observation count, emitted-response count, modeled access probability, category distributions, calibrated correctness confidence and physical correctness as separate variables. Contrast markers alone should not acquire causal claims, while explicit "proves", "so" and "because" relationships must remain in the inventory with their scope and warrant.

Needed prospective rules include a definition of decoding, a generic-role versus policy naming rule, atomic inventory conventions for factive/evidential claims and presuppositions, matching for separate address components, and a plan for ambiguity and repeated assertions. The expanded vocabulary must then be tested on new whole-prose task reports containing unprompted factual, causal and comparative assertions, with a coverage checklist derived from the frozen task prompt and payload before prose is read. These development annotations can inform that work under the AI review plan; they cannot qualify it or repair historical failed qualification.
