## Why representational manner is still unqualified

The strongest warranted candidate is literal: separately indexed role evidence
becomes repeated over each 2×2 block while centroid order usually survives.
That describes a content-bearing representation's numerical format. It is more
informative than a generic activation-energy change. It does not yet establish
the required presentation manner beyond fine-location content and task access.

Holding “red centroid left of blue centroid” fixed leaves substantial content
free to change: individual constituent location, expected position, probability
assigned to alternative cells and useful targeting information. The native task
was trained precisely to read those locations. No native consumer independently
uses a manner property while these finer content/access alternatives are held
apart. Calling the coarse relation the complete content would decide the disputed
interpretation by definition.

T2 itself erases within-block contrasts before the shared head. It guarantees
repeated evidence, four maximum ties and a zero top-two-site margin; the fixed
row-major policy can then target only block top-left cells. Its .25 balanced
accuracy ceiling follows from that policy. The observed .2266–.2422 accuracies
are consistent with ordinary block-information loss plus some wrong block
choices. T4 similarly destroys spatial variation, forces uniform evidence and
erases coarse order. Entropy increases and reduced maximum evidence accompany
these changes. None of those imposed effects is an independent measurement of
experienced indistinctness or a separable presentation property.

Scalar-preserving site permutations demonstrate that coordinates matter beyond
entropy, maximum, margin and channel energy. But moving the coordinates also
moves the represented location content and the action target. These synthetic
controls cannot adjudicate manner versus additional spatial content merely by
preserving scalars. The reviewed evidence also does not supply a naturally
reachable comparison that separates those interpretations. Unchanged retained
original logits control their saved path only; disputed W-derived category and
orientation deltas supply no stronger exclusion of access/confidence alternatives.

The design explicitly preserves a negative endpoint when only objective position
decoding or fine-task access is defensible. This review applies that existing
rule despite strong utility and abundant coarse-preserving transitions.

## Ordinary/filter explanation and the user's theory-facing goal

An equally informed ordinary apparatus can retain V, apply the same declared
averaging T and pass W through the same frozen H. It yields exactly these native
fields and targeting decisions without a learned attention-model A. That is the
executed architecture here, not an information-deprived imagined rival. Matched
exogenous drivers also reproduce the native outcomes. A mapping estimator plus
analytic T would be a relevant equally informed comparator when unknown command
mapping and prediction are introduced; no such learned-model comparison occurred
in this stage. The raw-grid head is a successful sensory alternative with
different inputs and fewer parameters, not an equal-information specificity test.

These alternatives limit attribution of the current effect to attention-model
contents. They do not show that future faithful A reports could never provide
restricted evidence relevant to the theory. The user's goal can be advanced by
accurately attributed A-content reports that independently exhibit the relevant
consciousness-like structure, without proving private felt experience or requiring
A to be necessary for targeting. This run supplies neither learned A states nor
reports, so no such empirical correspondence or critic-facing crux was tested.
The Controller executes targeting actions; the absent Reporter would be external
diagnostic instrumentation, not the attention Controller or the generator of W.

## Smallest actionable next native advance

First resolve the numerical reconstruction against the preserved archives without
rerunning or refitting this development. The successful native head provides a
concrete spatial-content interface, so neither another decoder-quality sweep nor
another presentation-label rubric addresses the remaining architectural gap.

A useful next source version would add one content-bound A with distinct
`A_current(t)` and `A_next(t, command)` fields in the same fixed role/site
coordinates as the native content consumer. A's inputs and completed-feedback
updates must be timestamped, with current scored W excluded as a copying shortcut.
Commands should vary within saved-V worlds under a predeclared mapping. The
Controller can use A's prospective field to choose an operation or another
observation; actual completed W remains separately recorded.

For a concrete native comparison-medium role, compare predicted and newly
constructed content fields and route their discrepancy into a small content
Modeler update. Measure whether that update improves later independent content
observations. Retain role/address-local mismatches and test blocked, reversed and
world-yoked feedback, Controller-only/no-A behavior and equally informed ordinary
and mapping-plus-filter predictive updates with matched permissions and budget.
Cross reachable donor A states with unchanged W/observations while preserving
wrong estimates and explicit address/time provenance. That asks what the model's
represented content actually does, rather than assigning a new adjective to
filter statistics. It is a proposed mechanism development, not a registered run
or a promised semantic pass; beneficial refinement is a stronger empirical role,
not a newly imposed necessity criterion for the user's report-based goal.

If this establishes independently defensible source properties, a later frozen
neutral export/report study can cross A and W, distinguish current estimates
from future predictions, include direct-W and equally informed ordinary narrators,
and assess factual fidelity separately from source-blind report structure. Theory,
rubric, phenomenal vocabulary and desired examples must remain absent from the
Reporter input. Successful faithful structure would support a bounded source/report
claim even if ordinary predictors achieve parity; the parity must be reported.
No report prose can retroactively qualify this negative native semantic endpoint.
