## Training, inputs and the native consumer

The runner fixes seeds 1301/1311/1321, 128 fit and 64 diagnostic worlds per seed,
four patches per world, both role queries and all T1/T2/T4 alternatives. Generation
passes the exact seed/split namespace and world index to the reviewed renderer.
Layouts overlap across splits, as the corrected protocol states; only nuisance
realizations are fresh. There is no withheld-layout or confirmation claim.

`fit_head` fits channel statistics on fit maps only, uses the declared native/raw
initialization seeds, default shared-head initialization, exactly 500 full-batch
float32 CPU Adam updates with the stated hyperparameters, then eval mode and
disabled gradients. It logs every completed update loss and retains initial/final
weights, standardizer, final fit logits and losses. Diagnostic outputs are never
used for stopping, initialization selection or a layer search. Engineering failure
does not stop inspection of later encoders or trigger a fallback.

The raw-grid reshape decomposes each 16×16 axis into grid-index and within-cell
dimensions. Averaging axes -3 and -1 averages the within-cell row/column axes,
leaving an addressed 3×4×4 grid. This is the intended upstream feasibility
alternative, with fewer head parameters and different source information.

Every primary targeting/coarse action is produced by `NativeController` from a
completed `AddressedState`; batched H calculations are comparison checks, not
action sources. Each addressed operation begins with the same saved initial
V=W state and separately issues/completes at times zero/one. Original pixels,
category/orientation pathways, raw orientation logit and nonselected items are
preserved. The renderer truth enters occupancy training and evaluator scoring,
not the native consumer API. Isolation checks replace retained identity, raw

they retain exact isolated logits/fields. Actions subsequently depend on those
fields alone. The frozen source copy is archived before/after and checked equal.
