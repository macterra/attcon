## What was independently checked

The audit imports NumPy and Torch, with **no production-module imports**. For all three checkpoint seeds and all three splits it reconstructs actual delivery locations from command mapping, queries the last delivered observation at each location, and reconstructs latest sensory memory, raw-logit sign target, command history, last-step index and last pixels. All these associations match bitwise. Unseen rows remain zero placeholders and are excluded by observed status. Split world-seed sets are disjoint; command, noise and bootstrap RNG tensors match their registered namespaces. Hashes match for all 11 source files, 12 frozen inputs, six design-review files, all 75 raw archives, the registered source-text snapshots, manifest and result completion receipts. Archive sizes match and are under 100 MB. These checks attest recorded integrity, rather than proving historical absence of every unrecorded action.

Pixel targets are independently recomputed from the archived last delivered images using NumPy population SD over all 768 RGB/spatial values and directional adjacent-edge squared sums. The two directional means are averaged before square root. They agree with the archived targets to the specified comparison precision. This SD pools channels and spatial positions; it is not a pure spatial luminance statistic. Noise and other renderer effects remain part of the target, including nominal zero contrast.

For all 39 decoder fits, feature/physical-target means and population RMS are recomputed from training seen rows only, including the `RMS <= 1e-6 -> 1` rule. Every one of the five physical-calibration candidate losses is recomputed. Independently solving `XᵀX + n alpha I` with Torch gives the same first-minimum calibration-selected alpha and coefficients to numerical precision; no evaluation target selects a penalty. The auxiliary centered raw-decision score shares the selected alpha and uses strict `score > .5`. All retained held-out hard decisions match exactly. All seven views' physical predictions, score predictions and training-mean baselines agree numerically.

All held-out groups are retained and checked: pooled, each location, macro location, every true contrast 0–5, each orientation, and all five current-success bins including empty bins. Counts, target means/variances, train-mean-denominator R², MAE, RMSE and auxiliary fidelity are recomputed. Whole-world bootstrap statistics use archived 256 draws via independently formed world multiplicities, retaining every correlated row in each drawn world. Point estimates and finite intervals agree; paired other-minus-risk MSE contrasts and risk/physical correlations also agree. [The audit source](numerical_audit.py) and [execution log](execution.log) expose the actual checks.

Maximum refit coefficient difference is `7.38e-13` against maximum absolute coefficients `2.93` (ratio `2.51e-13`). Maximum physical-prediction difference is `6.88e-15`, about `1.38e-12` of the smallest corresponding training-target RMS (`.004973`); maximum auxiliary-score difference is `2.00e-12`. Maximum candidate-loss difference is `1.64e-14`; maximum finite metric/interval difference is `2.49e-14`. Means and scales of features agree exactly. Comparison thresholds (`atol 1e-10`, `rtol 1e-8`, tighter image/bound checks) are reviewer arithmetic checks, not scientific acceptance margins. Unseen decoder outputs can extrapolate far outside seen ranges; they remain excluded throughout fitting, selection and scoring.

This is **not a full independent neural reconstruction**. I did not reconstruct the visual encoder, raw sensory adapter, structured/joint inference or their source calibrators from weights. Archived neural tensors are checked for export association, finiteness, downstream arithmetic and intervention invariants; correct neural inference additionally requires the separate production replay. I did not independently regenerate world nuisance masks/phases or rerender pixels from world truth. The pixel check verifies objective statistics and latest-delivery association of stored pixels, not a second renderer implementation. No checkpoint was retrained or recollected.

## Source intervention and limits

Native sensory-row rotation holds archived command history and represented delivery fixed. Risk/current/refresh/logit permutations pass fixed `atol 1e-6`, `rtol 1e-5`; maximum hidden discrepancy is `4.77e-7`, maximum logit discrepancy `9.54e-7`. Local feature and physical-output differences and every saved propagation-bound element are independently checked. Largest physical permutation error is `5.88e-8`, within the actual feature-roundoff linear envelope plus the fixed `1e-12` floor. The envelope is numerical error propagation, not a physical-quality margin.

Inverse sensory restoration, every archived restored structured prediction, and every local restored readout are bitwise exact. Delivery-command patching changes the declared matrix rows and recomposes `after = delivery*refresh + (1-delivery)*current`; all other upstream exported fields and local readouts remain bitwise fixed. Original/patched/inverse delivery and post-command composition match exactly. This is a source-export composition test, not a neural joint-hidden intervention or a physical future observation. Raw sensory rows rotate exactly; unchanged history continues to describe actual past deliveries even when patched memory disagrees. The patch is synthetic and changes address-specific risks, so it is not a pure binding perturbation holding all marginals fixed.
