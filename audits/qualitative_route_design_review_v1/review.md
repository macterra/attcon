## 1. Define the strict endpoint mathematically, not just as a promised separate result

The design formally defines only `P = C AND M AND S`. `M` permits current representational access; `S` permits different status statements across attributes/contents. Thus accurate technical text saying one stored item is present and another unavailable can pass `P` by design. That is a legitimate **broad structural access** measure, but it is broader than my earlier proposal requiring supported represented presentation distinct from a decision claim. The difference is disclosed and should remain disclosed.

The phrase “stricter presentation/appearance result” is not yet a complete decision rule. Freeze at least three distinct outcomes:

- `P_access`: the broad structural conjunction already defined, with access scope explicitly identified.
- `P_presentation_supported`: content-bound **manner of represented presentation or apparent perceptual attribute**, separability as specified, and source support for what the representation depicts. Pure access status, identity uncertainty, task success and physical-property estimation alone fail this outcome.
- `P_experiential_wording`: the corresponding text explicitly claims perceptual appearance/experience. This is a wording/reference match; felt truth remains unauditable. It cannot be labeled source-validated experience merely because ordinary factual clauses pass.

Representational scope is not automatically inferior to first-person scope: a supported description of a depicted perceptual quality can be relevant without claiming private feeling. Conversely, adding appearance grammar to a task-risk estimate cannot satisfy the strict outcome. The source auditor must record which claim is supported, unsupported or **not evaluable as felt truth**, rather than assigning a single global “faithful” flag that obscures this distinction. Do not require instrumentation to verify felt experience; that would recreate an unattainable gate. Do require it to verify the represented-content/presentation meaning it actually claims.

The attention extension also includes access and should receive the same split. A changed content-to-command/access sentence can establish grounded functional reporting while strict presentation and its selective attention-source effect remain absent. Neither `P_access` nor an access-only `A` can achieve the user endpoint.

## 2. Keep reference fidelity and the operational abstraction separate

The publication provenance is faithfully summarized. The PAS example is an author-presented typical report without a trial/participant identifier, not an authenticated raw transcript. Its generalized categories and certainty language do not independently demonstrate clarity–confidence dissociation. My earlier review verified the author-hosted uncorrected proof and matching author-uploaded typeset text; freeze the exact final source/version used rather than silently exchanging versions.

I newly checked [Lutz et al. (2002), Methods, p. 1587](https://portablelaboratory.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/lutzetal02.pdf). The named S4 passage is a training response following an explicit introspective question, and describes image emergence/attention capture. The study used four trained subjects and report-derived categories. This supports a bounded semantic anchor, not spontaneous report emergence, a universal attention multiplier, or an attention-model mechanism.

The references express human appearance/experience; equating their descriptions with technical model access is a **project abstraction**, not a newly verified equivalence in the sources. It is reasonable to annotate that abstraction, but any positive result must name its scope. The revised text generally does this well. Keep authentic feature-negative anchors separate from synthetic boundary cases, and preserve the fact that an authentic consciousness report can lack the proposed composite. Do not claim human prevalence, classifier sensitivity/specificity, or a distributional match from two anchors.

Missing trial bytes no longer block semantic correspondence. More precisely, they remain necessary for the **proposed participant/trial-level psychophysical and native-stimulus transfer analyses**, not for every conceivable quantitative comparison with a published summary. Use that scope in future freezes so the revised exception does not become another universal archive rule.

## 3. Freeze denominators and qualification before reports

“All eligible opportunities” must be operationalized by world/query/status/index before report generation. Keep omissions, unsupported outputs, failed source validity and ambiguous scope visible. Report strict supported matches over all preselected opportunities, plus conditional source-qualified summaries separately; do not quietly make absence of a qualified presentation representation disappear through eligibility filtering. Broad access and strict presentation need separate numerators and denominators in the results template.

Physical source probes have no appearance pass gate. Therefore specify the actual next-step decision: negative or weak decoding is a source-information result; it does not authorize introducing a new adjective mapping until it passes separate source-semantic validation. Positive decoding qualifies only the declared diagnostic estimates and errors. Annotation reliability, source-supportedness minima, ambiguity handling, report budgets and stopping are still unfrozen. This is acceptable for a design, but no Reporter run is yet qualified by it.

Same-information apparatus narration and deterministic separate-table joining are appropriate controls. They may match broad or strict structure. Parity constrains specificity without deleting measured correspondence, and superiority is not automatically a necessary condition. Keep prompts factual, source meanings neutral, and published/synthetic target examples out of Reporter prompts/training. Rater exposure to qualification anchors is appropriate if disclosed; multiple shared-training AI raters do not constitute independent human validation.

## 4. Tighten the physical diagnostic's question

“Information ... beyond its task-success forecast” currently exceeds the defined comparison if read as an information-theoretic or semantic claim. A hidden-state ridge probe outperforming a ridge probe on two calibrated outcome scalars demonstrates **greater recoverability under those probe/input budgets**. It does not rule out a nonlinear transform of forecast coordinates, show conditional independence from risk, or establish a distinct presentation representation.

Either state this restricted question explicitly or preregister an additional development-only residual/nonlinear low-dimensional control. The existing forecast/logit controls, conditional strata and capacity disclosures are useful; an architectural-necessity conclusion is correctly excluded. Do not add a control selected from favorable evaluation results. This refinement is about interpretable source sufficiency, not a new goal requiring decoder superiority.

The auxiliary target is the stored decoder's orientation decision. Agreement measures readout consistency with that decision, not world orientation correctness or accurate perceptual content. The protocol correctly says this; retain the distinction in result tables and any later Reporter source glossary.

## 5. Physical target names and degenerate cases

Standard deviation over all `3×16×16` image values is **pooled image-value variation**, which can include differences between channel means. It is not purely spatial standard deviation. The formula is valid; rename the target to match it, or explicitly choose a per-channel spatial definition before freeze. Do not silently change the mathematical target after execution.

The nearest-neighbor RMS target is specified sufficiently to identify the intended edge statistic. It reflects noise, masks, phase and clipping as well as contrast; it is not nominal contrast, blur or experienced clarity. The protocol retains those limits.

Specify target-scale behavior when training variance is zero, handling of zero-variance evaluation strata, and the exact baseline-relative `R2` formula in code/freeze. A training-mean denominator differs from conventional evaluation-mean `R2`; label that choice clearly. Preserve null/undefined cells rather than inventing perfect scores or silently excluding groups. Exact RNG constants, bin boundaries, numerical tolerances and bootstrap rules still need the registered code review; no code was reviewed here.

## 6. Keep synthetic source patches distinct from natural states

Memory-row readdressing at fixed route history can produce a source whose implied delivery history no longer agrees with the moved memory/observed entries. Its local-network equivariance remains a valid engineering identity, but it is not automatically a naturally realized attention/content state. Declare the patched-input precedence and context consistency, archive the discrepancy, and keep natural-world semantic results separate from synthetic patch results.

Forecast-row corruption leaves risk hidden and physical decoding invariant by construction. That is a valuable export/fidelity check; it cannot supply a selective presentation-source effect. A later qualitative theory-facing assay still needs a declared source prediction that actually affects the strict presentation claim while sensory, risk, answer and language alternatives are controlled. If no such identifiable contrast exists, publish that limit rather than converting architectural invariance into a consciousness result.
