**E1r — completed evaluation call records still disappear on a later-source
failure.** At lines593–595, `update_visibility` and `decoder_visibility` are
created alongside the other completed-source dictionaries, but only
`source_tensors`, `source_checks` and prediction `visibility` are attached to
caller-owned `progress`. At lines612 and821, the active update/decoder lists are
replaced for each source and the completed lists move into those unreferenced
local dictionaries. Thus a failure during ordinary or analytic evaluation can
retain completed A tensors and predictions but lose A's actual update/decoder
call evidence. This defeats the purpose of the new actual-call instrumentation:
its missing calls cannot be established merely from an expected recipe.

Attach both completed dictionaries to `progress` when they are created, before
any source work; keep active list references too. Likewise retain accumulated
shared actual-action and analytic-secondary outputs before later risky work,
so first failure preservation does not depend on reaching the final return.
These are instrumentation retention changes; they require no new world, fit,
forward budget or threshold. A future bounded fixture should demonstrate that
earlier completed source call collections remain reachable from partial state
when a subsequent source fails. This review does not execute such a fixture.
