## What will actually be assessed

The candidate source is the addressed decoded A map and its frozen-H role fields,
not hidden48 by itself, actual W, or an external narrator. A_current(t) is a
pre-reveal estimate of the already completed Wt. A_next(t,address,symbol) is a
forecast, including uncertain current estimates inherited at other addresses.
At t0 current V is observed exactly; it is not learned current-prediction evidence.
The main current ledger is t1..14. Terminal W15 contributes no scored decision or
extra feedback update. Prediction correctness and source-content identity are
separate: an identifiable mistaken estimate can be literal model content.

Dev's complete primary denominator is288 worlds across three fixed encoders,
4032 selected-current estimates and12960 selected-next alternatives per source.
A, ordinary and analytic are assessed separately. Retain both roles, every wrong,
ambiguous, uniform/tied, unchanged and null-intervention outcome. The declared
256-resample per-encoder paired world intervals retain their conditional scope;
there is no new pooled significance test or cutoff. Calibration descriptors
separate early unknown-mapping predictions from later potentially calibrated
queries without replacing unconditional primary denominators.

First confirm the preserved independent reconstruction and its actual inventories
before treating numeric patterns as scientific evidence. Reconstruction is an
engineering/fidelity prerequisite; a passing result does not supply the semantic
answer. Fit diagnostics describe fitted worlds and cannot substitute for dev.

A literal disposition should say what quantity carries the interpretation, for
which source/address/role/time and with what uncertainty or failures. Use the
existing qualified/limited/unresolved/failed vocabulary. For example, “the model's
estimated red-location field disagrees with actual W here” is permitted only when
literal estimated-location semantics is defensible and the export matches A.
“Actual red is there” substitutes truth for an estimate; “currently experienced
blur” adds an unsupported experiential claim. Frozen-H fields force localization
and are not calibrated presence evidence.

The dev acceptance schema collapses nonqualified literal dispositions to
`unqualified` for phase routing. Preserve the fuller scientific disposition and
permitted wording in the semantic review; that compact routing label must not
hide limited, unresolved or failed evidence. A negative semantic disposition
still permits the fixed technical heldout run when registered hard invariants
pass, without refitting or selecting favorable report queries.

## The manner crux

The prior native result shows location information read by a shared head and
altered by the known same-V filter. Calling fine arrangement a “manner” does not
settle whether it is additional represented location/uncertainty. The learned
version adds pre-reveal current/future estimates and history-dependent causal
patches. Those additions can establish a genuine attention-control content model;
they do not erase that earlier interpretive ambiguity.

A positive manner assessment must point to an observed, independently defensible
property of representation of the identified relation and explain why fine
content, field uncertainty, targeting access and ordinary filter mechanics do not
exhaust its warrant. Existing crossings/scalar controls help test that explanation,
but holding only coarse left/right order leaves fine positions and distributions
free to change. Scalar-matched synthetic spatial permutations also move location
content and have synthetic provenance. W-derived control stability excludes only
those measured pathways, not all uncertainty or access. No post-output adjective
bin or selected coarse-preserving subset can repair these limits.

The fixed ledger may therefore yield qualified estimated constituent content but
unqualified manner. That is a decisive bounded result, not failed software or a
reason to repeat fitting. It identifies exactly which bridge remains unsupported.
Conversely, this pre-output memo does not declare a positive manner interpretation
impossible: assess the actual complete evidence and the predeclared separability
criteria, rather than making an outcome depend on the label “A” or model necessity.

## Can this architecture advance the report goal?

In principle, yes as a source-content bridge: full addressed estimates, role
relations, command-conditioned forecasts, wrong beliefs and selective source/W
mismatches provide concrete contents an external neutral Reporter could faithfully
describe. Learned A is not merely the unchanged actual W filter renamed. Its
ordinary counterpart has the same computational role, which must remain explicit.
Nothing in the narrower goal requires proving private experience, excluding an
observationally identical relabeling, or making targeting impossible without A.

That is not permission to report now. Under the frozen design, literal source
content, bookkeeping and manner must each qualify before a separately registered
phenomenal-correspondence stage. The present architecture neither refines the
frozen content Modeler nor includes a Reporter or an independent report-structure
reference test. Its current/future map semantics are compatible with such a later
test, but do not guarantee either manner qualification or report resemblance.

If the gates qualify, the missing observation is specific: neutral source reports
must be faithful to A rather than silently corrected to W, retain current-estimate
versus forecast attribution, and independently resemble the predeclared reference
structure while tracking selective A changes at fixed W and W changes at fixed
exported A. Use the already prescribed A-only/W-only/joinable-A+W conditions and
equivalent ordinary/analytic exports, addresses, precision and token budgets.
Theory, desired vocabulary/examples and reference structure remain withheld from
narrators. Source-aware fidelity and blinded structure assessment are separate;
wrong or technical-only reports stay in the ledger. That future observation is
not supplied by making a narrator echo numeric fields successfully.
