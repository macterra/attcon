## The claim that is worth testing

The memo correctly distinguishes two questions. V1's strict manner claim requires independent warrant beyond extra fine spatial content, uncertainty, access and ordinary filtering. V1 did not supply that warrant. The separately proposed question asks whether independently grounded, addressed relational estimates and their organization supply accurate, independently recognizable report structure. That is an observable and useful restricted hypothesis even while the stronger manner claim remains negative.

It is not necessary to demand a form variable disjoint from every possible fine-content, uncertainty or access description to test this second question. If the complete role/location field is counted as fine content, holding that whole field fixed also fixes its represented spatial organization. An absolute disjointness requirement could exclude the proposed source by definition. Conversely, this conceptual observation does not prove that v1's form is phenomenal or that its manner gate was met. Fine-content and access explanations must remain measured rival accounts and limit the interpretation of a correspondence result.

The actual dev assessment supports a usable source for this narrower test: A and ordinary decoded fields qualify as bounded addressed red/blue location estimates; analytic H(posterior-mean-map) qualifies with mixture provenance; bookkeeping qualifies. These are not arbitrary head outputs simply blessed by narration. Learned attended-W fidelity remains limited, and all manner remains unqualified. Faithfully describing a wrong estimate is a valid fidelity outcome; silently correcting it to physical truth or H(W) is not. An A label, task necessity, unique ordinary failure, first-person phrasing, private-experience proof or mandatory Modeler refinement is not a gate for this narrower hypothesis.

A positive could establish that A supplies the varying modeled contents to an external report process and that faithful reports exhibit an independently specified consciousness-like relational structure under the tested conditions. It would not identify all linguistic structure as originating in A, establish a quale-generating computation, or demonstrate the stronger Modeler-schema/refinement role. The external language model contributes prior structure and expression. Direct-W, ordinary, analytic, generic sensor/state-estimator and template comparisons help measure that contribution rather than demand an impossible uniquely named A advantage. Ordinary parity permits a shared-computation correspondence conclusion; template or sensor parity can leave little theory-specific explanatory gain even if genuine source correspondence is observed.
