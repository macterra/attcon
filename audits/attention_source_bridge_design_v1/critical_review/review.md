# Critical review of attention-source bridge design v1

2026-10-10 local date. Static, hypothesis-informed commissioned AI review by `/root/attention_source_bridge_critical_review`. No experiment, fit, model inference, checkpoint/tensor loading, Reporter call, report rating or primary-paper retrieval was performed. The reviewed proposal is [the bridge draft](../review.md); theory constraints are taken from the supplied [role audit](../../../docs/MSTC_REPORTING_ROLE_AUDIT_V1.md), not independently verified here.

**Verdict: useful new mechanism development; strict native presentation counterpart still unqualified; no discriminating phenomenal-source inference follows from the design as written.** Executing a post-encoder operation on identical saved sensory evidence advances beyond probing physical image statistics. However, reconstructing its output qualifies a model of an internal filter, not automatically a model of presentation. The proposal acknowledges much of this limitation. It should proceed, if chosen, as a bounded source-mechanism qualification with an explicit negative endpoint available, rather than as an already justified theory-facing qualia experiment.

## What changes, and what does not follow

The inspected structured forward path agrees with the draft: local `risk_hidden` depends on sensory rows; `route_hidden` depends on latest-delivery history; post-command correctness is composed from delivery/current/refresh. There is no route-to-local-content transformation in that path. The [prior counterpart review](../../qualitative_source_counterpart_review_v1/review.md) therefore correctly retained a negative strict-counterpart result after successful objective image-property decoding.

The new proposal changes that situation causally. At constant pixels and retained encoder map V, an executed T changes a distinct internal W. A history-conditioned A predicts this change before receiving its result. A successful held-out test would locate information about an internally executed operation that a V-only decoder cannot identify when commands/mappings are randomized. It would rule against a *purely upstream sensory or original-task-confidence explanation of that conditional prediction*. That is real progress, even though W ultimately inherits content from V.

It is not simply a physical-image blur assay: the image need not change. It can nevertheless become a renamed internal blur/statistics assay. A spatial average reduces particular activation components by construction; a predictor trained to reconstruct that average and a narrator supplied its summary can describe this correctly. Neither reconstruction accuracy nor choosing the word “presentation” establishes that those activations constitute how the model represents the identified pattern. Avoid equating independence from pixels with independence from ordinary computation.

The frozen `VisualEncoder` actually has convolution/ReLU/max-pool stages producing a 24×4×4 map before flattening. Its channels and limited spatial resolution do not have a qualified mapping to apparent fine detail. Extracting a spatial tensor is feasible at the source-text level, but attention manipulation of this tensor is new architecture. Whether its remaining resolution supports a meaningful content-form test is an empirical feasibility question; this review did not execute it.

Three separate claims must survive separate gates:

| Claim | Appropriate positive evidence | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Report fidelity | All inspectable assertions accurately describe supplied estimates/events, including mistakes, time and address; matched interventions/restoration behave as registered | Accurate narration can be obtained for any well-described instrument |
| Native represented-form correspondence | A content-specific native representation has independently fixed form semantics; neutral faithful reports satisfy C/M/S and scope criteria independently of their source audit | A successful result establishes model-representation scope, not felt truth |
| Phenomenal-source evidence | A prespecified source hypothesis gains support relative to a named, comparably informed alternative through an observable role/result that differs between them | The current filter/predictor/narrator construction gives no such exclusive prediction against general state estimation |

The [correspondence design](../../../docs/QUALITATIVE_REPORT_CORRESPONDENCE_V1_DESIGN.md) already separates these claims. Its strict endpoint should not be relaxed because the new predictor is native or attention-labelled. Conversely, neither humans nor consciousness proof are prerequisites for the first two gates.

## Minimal corrections before a runnable protocol

1. **Separate current estimates, future predictions and actual W.** The draft describes A as predicting the *next* map but also as the source of the *current* pattern's form. Define timestamps and distinct `A_current`, `A_next(command)` and actual completed W fields, including how a current estimate is obtained without copying its scored target. A predicted alternative is not the presently represented pattern. Independently audit this provenance; freeze what a mistaken estimate can faithfully support.

2. **Qualify literal content-form semantics before target prose.** Freeze at least one concrete addressed content relation that W encodes, and a form property of that relation, with a held-out readout/behavior check independent of A's reconstruction loss. A generic global roughness/energy scalar is insufficient. For example, test whether an addressed represented pattern retains a particular spatial relation and how its native activation arrangement changes, keeping category evidence separate. Use content variation/crossed address controls to show this describes the identified item rather than pooled nuisance. A fixed native content consumer can supply useful independent role evidence; it must not be introduced solely to restate a “clarity” target. Its decision errors do not define presentation. If only activation statistics can be qualified, publish exactly that technical scope and retain the strict negative. No human adjective needs to be assigned.

3. **Specify T and its information boundary completely.** Freeze map extraction stage, kernel/bandwidth, edge handling, output shape/units, conservation claims, current-state versus saved-V application, and reset/restoration. Reversible restoration means restoring saved state; lossy averaging has no mathematical inverse. Define command-to-operation learning and which completed transitions each arm can see. Four prelude transitions cannot be assumed to identify every mapping. Preserve incompletely identified/incorrect mappings. Call T an experimentally defined internal allocation operation; its being inside the machine does not independently establish an endogenous psychological attention process.

4. **Make the decisive controls information-matched.** A-minus-sensory-only loss mainly measures the value of withheld command/history information. Keep that as an ablation, not the principal source-specificity result. An ordinary predictor must receive exactly the same V, current state, commands, transition observations, timestamps, addressing, feedback/update rights and output targets. Match effective capacity/training/search budgets, and publish any residual mismatch. Include a simple command/mapping estimator plus an analytic application of the registered T family when those operator definitions are equally available. This tests whether neural A merely learns a lookup and filter. Good parity is a legitimate result, not an automatic failure of content-form measurement.

5. **Separate A provenance from forced reporting.** An A-only export followed by an A patch necessarily changes the Reporter input. That establishes export dependence. Cross actual W and A estimates, give all narrator arms identical provenance-labelled fields under the same neutral instruction, and score which source each assertion describes. Include a direct-W readout arm and an exactly joinable separated export. Donor states must be reachable under the same declared operation/mapping, with address/time validity retained. Model-estimate/actual-event conflicts must remain visible. This can localize report information; it cannot alone localize phenomenal presentation in A rather than W or the Reporter.

These changes fit a small mechanical development study. They do not require a full MSTC implementation or delay an independently labelled factual pilot. Freeze definitions, tolerances, exclusions, opportunity denominators and stopping before held-out mechanism outputs; freeze report criteria before target text. Development-derived choices must be recorded before reserved evaluation, not retrospectively selected from it.

## Controls and concrete falsifiers

| Control/test | Discrimination | Adverse result to retain |
|---|---|---|
| Same-V randomized T, with sham and saved-state restoration | Internally produced form change versus unchanged sensory evidence | No meaningful content-bound change; map loss dominated by unchanged channels; inadequate held-out prediction |
| Same information ordinary/analytic process predictor | Attention-labelled architecture versus ordinary system identification | Parity explains process prediction without a special attention-schema principle |
| Same-W result produced by a matched non-attentional internal operation; separately, comparable upstream filtering | Operation provenance versus resulting form, and internal estimate versus physical input | Effects follow generic filtering and its observations; no evidence for attention-specific origin. Exact W matching may be infeasible upstream and must not be claimed |
| W-derived task/confidence and access baselines with the same command/history information | Form correspondence versus scalar confidence/access narration | All qualified form claims reduce to access or scalar uncertainty; retained original logit alone fails to control this |
| Crossed A/W mismatch, source removal and restoration, with equal-information direct-W and narrator arms | What estimates/events drive claims and content-use errors | Only supplied text fields change; no independently scored native role effect; provenance collapses or unsupported appearance claims increase |
| Content/address remapping plus form changes at other locations | Content-specific relation versus generic operation/attention language | Claims fail to follow the addressed item, or unaffected items acquire unsupported changes |
| Source-blind C/M/S/scope rating plus separate source-aware audit | Qualitative correspondence versus correct technical narration | Technical/access-only reports; ambiguous correspondence; fluent but unsupported presentation language |

The matched non-attentional control has an interpretive boundary: if two methods create identical W and identical A, an account locating presentation in represented form may predict identical reports. Parity then tests provenance specificity, not refutes representation-based correspondence. The protocol must state its locus prediction in advance instead of treating either parity or difference as a theory win. Similarly, conventional-predictor or narrator parity limits unique attribution but does not erase demonstrated fidelity or form correspondence. Arbitrary numerical/linguistic superiority is not required.

For reconstruction, report changed-region/component errors as well as full-map error, against no-change and conditional-mean baselines. Otherwise predicting unchanged background can look successful while missing the only manipulated content relation. Paired whole-world analysis is appropriate; the three frozen encoders do not provide a trained-model population. Confidence equivalence and meaningful form effects need registered units/tolerances, or remain descriptive. Do not discard zero/low-contrast worlds, wrong decisions, ties, ineffective T transitions or failed reports.

## What could favor the source interpretation

A useful positive pattern would be: naturally executed same-V attention changes alter an independently qualified content form; A anticipates those changes and their content-specific failures at held-out locations/contents; original and additional metacognitive controls fail to explain them; neutral reports faithfully distinguish identification from that form and its attention relation; independent raters recognize the registered structure without seeing source or desired labels. This would favor the **restricted claim that an attention-control representation supplies reportable content form** over upstream-property-only, confidence-only and purely unsupported-language explanations. Reports need not use experiential adjectives or outperform an equally informed narrator. Lack of felt-truth validation does not invalidate this operational model-presentation counterpart; its scope is a positive result distinct from a phenomenal-source attribution.

To favor a source mechanism over *ordinary state estimation/narration as a general explanation*, specify an additional consequence that those accounts do not already predict. A useful candidate role test is whether the same correctly addressed form discrepancy governs both independent content comparison/refinement and the corresponding report effects: intact/blocked/reversed/yoked discrepancies, fixed sensory schedule, matched ordinary predictive-learning feedback, and restoration. Improvement on later independent Modeler reconstructions would test the stronger comparison-medium role in the supplied MSTC audit. Controller command improvement alone does not. A common selective effect across native content use and reporting is stronger than an arbitrary disconnected report wire, but ordinary estimators implementing that same comparison role may still reproduce it.

Thus this optional extension establishes a functional role if successful; it supplies no exclusive phenomenal prediction as presently formulated. No threshold or model name repairs that identifiability limit. If the rival is allowed exactly the same computation, inputs and consumers under a different label, no observable in this proposal distinguishes it. A source-specific claim then requires a genuinely differing prediction, not a forced superiority requirement. The narrow positive pattern is relevant development evidence for the user's goal; it is not credible differential evidence that this predictor generates qualia.

**Disposition:** proceed to a frozen, bounded mechanism/semantic qualification after the minimal corrections, retaining “no strict counterpart” as a terminating outcome. Only a qualified represented-form source licenses the optional strict correspondence pilot, and only within its model-representation scope. Preserve the negative phenomenal-source discrimination verdict even if prediction, control utility and reporting all succeed. No experimental execution or reporting is authorized by this review itself.
