# Attention-source bridge design review v1

2026-10-10. Static source assessment and prospective design by the commissioned AI agent `/root/attention_source_bridge_design`. No inference, fitting, new worlds, raw tensor/checkpoint loading, Reporter calls, or edits to existing sources/protocols were performed.

**Finding: no presently qualified native strict presentation counterpart was found.** The grounded model can represent sensory evidence, task-success forecasts and acquisition routing. Its completed physical assay establishes retained objective information. Neither operation supplies a defensible interpretation of how content is presented, independently of storage, correctness or image statistics. This is a bounded source-semantics finding, not absence-of-experience evidence. The justified next theory-facing development experiment is a small learned model of **content-specific changes in present representational form**, produced by an endogenous attention operation and tested at fixed upstream sensory evidence and fixed task confidence. Do not commission another prose batch or confidence/physical-statistics head as the proposed solution to this gap.

## What the existing source permits

The relevant native computation in `scripts/grounded_outcome_models_v1.py` is unusually informative:

- `risk_hidden = ReLU(W_s standardize(sensory) + b_s)`; the sensory row contains hidden64, a raw task logit and an observed flag. Its two native outputs are current/refresh correctness forecasts. Routing/history is not an input to this local hidden state.
- `route_hidden = ReLU(W_h standardize(history) + b_h)`; history contains the latest observed delivery for each command. Content is not an input to this hidden state.
- The structured post-command forecast is `delivery * refresh + (1-delivery) * current`. This combines future routing with probabilities of a fixed decision being correct. It does not construct a changed sensory pattern or model its present form.
- The joint comparator mixes sensory/history inputs but its training targets still concern delivery and decision success. Mixing inputs does not identify a latent presentation quantity.

Consequently, a represented-route patch has no path into local risk/content form. Memory-row readdressing moves sensory evidence with its associated risk estimates; it does not transform the within-item form of that evidence. The physical decoder's target semantics come from an external objective measurement, and strong upstream sensory recovery remains an explanation for its risk-hidden success. A native-logit-preserving nullspace intervention could separate a physical decoder from correctness heads, but cannot change those semantics.

The older sources offer no already-qualified replacement. `src/attcon/bound_content.py` binds categorical color/shape distributions to allocation/access/effect estimates; that binding is useful relational architecture. `src/attcon/functional_model.py:buffer_contents` explicitly mixes those distributions with uniform mass using recoverability. `src/attcon/predictive_attention.py` learns access targets generated by a simulator's random quality and `.75` decay recurrence. Those definitions ground access/uncertainty, not appearance. In `src/attcon/models.py`, soft selection can mix feature vectors and content-memory heads can reconstruct task/inspection state. Such operations are possible substrates for a new test, but current qualification does not show a content-form estimate whose semantics distinguish presentation from decision certainty. The grounded study's results cannot be transferred to those older implementations.

This finding is about inspected architecture, declared objectives and existing qualified measurements. No claim is made that every latent coordinate has been exhausted by every possible decoder.

## Independent constraints versus additional assumptions

The local publication-grounded reference review documents content/attribute identification separately from reported presentation and documents attention/emergence relations. Its Ramsøy/Overgaard illustration is author-presented typical wording without an authenticated participant/trial identifier; the Lutz quotation comes from trained reporting. These examples support a bounded semantic relation, not a representative prose distribution, a mandatory conjunction in every human report, a confidence-to-clarity conversion, or an algorithm for machine qualia. I relied on the local reference review here, not a new independent retrieval of those primary papers.

The stronger MSTC distinction supplied by the parent after its primary-paper check is between focal allocation, world construction, and schema conversion/comparison/refinement. A Controller that corrects an action after an attention forecast error establishes the narrower regulation role. It does not establish the claim that a schema is a comparison medium refining the underlying Modeler. Diffuse content cannot be declared absent merely because no focal report was requested. This review does not independently verify the primary paper or claim to instantiate its complete architecture.

The prospective experiment adds assumptions that must remain visible:

1. A content-bound model of the **current internal form** of a pattern is a potentially useful operational counterpart to model-presentation, beyond binary access or correctness.
2. A post-encoder attention transformation is an appropriate small process in which to investigate that counterpart.
3. Learning the process model without phenomenal labels and finding selective causal/report correspondence could support this particular operational bridge.

None follows uniquely from the cited human reports. No available constraint equates an internal representation, a feature reconstruction, a forward model, or report provenance with felt quality. The adjective “present” denotes the evaluated current snapshot, not a presupposed experience.

## Smallest executable new experiment

Use the existing frozen image-only encoder and four-location delivered-image world as engineering components, with entirely fresh world splits. Add one content-preserving post-encoder transformation and one small learned state model. This is a new development architecture, not a reinterpretation of the old frozen risk state.

**Retained evidence V.** Store each actual delivered image's spatial encoder feature map, its original hidden64/logit and its address. Preserve the raw spatial map before pooling, rather than inventing spatial meanings for the existing hidden64 coordinates. No world contrast/orientation truth, correctness, future image, or appearance label enters inference. The original orientation decision and any qualified task-success readout remain separate. The spatial source addition is explicit; it was not part of grounded v1's original Reporter boundary.

**Attention operation T.** At a requested location, a command changes the spatial pooling bandwidth of the retained feature map in a live internal pattern representation W. Other locations retain their existing W; they are not zeroed or declared unconscious when unselected. Include at least two bandwidths and an unchanged sham; learn an initially unknown command-to-operation mapping from actual completed transitions. Every transition records the actual before/after maps. Use pooling/averaging with declared units and conservation rules, not a handassigned clarity multiplier, an answer-correctness multiplier or a scalar quality label. The primary paired assay applies T to the **same saved V** without acquiring a new image. Thus upstream pixels/features, world properties, retained identity evidence and the original decision margin remain identical while the internal spatial form can change. Preservation of the separate identity record does not guarantee identical identity decodability from W: measure that using a held-out fixed W readout and retain any changed decisions/margins. This is an experimentally defined attention operation; its psychological equivalence is not assumed.

**Learned attention state model A.** A small recurrent or feedforward predictor consumes permitted completed-transition observations, retained addressed evidence and a pending command. It predicts the next content-bound spatial feature representation and keeps identity evidence separate from form. Train from actual internal before/after transition records by feature reconstruction/prediction loss. Do not train a class named clear/faint/blurred, import human ratings, impose bins named after phenomenal adjectives, or train prose. It must predict before seeing the actual post-command map; copying that map is not a forward prediction. Its structured output is a learned estimate of the map's current arrangement and alternative transformations, not only an external physical decoder of V.

**Controller.** After a declared query requests a target represented spatial arrangement, select and execute a command using A's prospective predictions. Compare the predicted post-command representation with the independently recorded actual W. Use this discrepancy in the next command decision. Score actual representation-target error, independently of orientation correctness. The Controller is an action selector/executor. It is not the Reporter and is not assumed to be the experiencing locus. Action utility is an engineering endpoint; a Controller written to consume A does not prove A's necessity.

**Candidate counterpart.** The declared quantity is the predicted, content-bound spatial feature pattern, with separately measured coarse/fine spatial component structure in **W**, not the physical image. Its semantics are established by held-out prediction of the actual internal pattern, independent readout/transition checks, and interventions on T at constant V. The source can potentially support a claim about the **form of the model's current represented pattern** while maintaining the same orientation identification. It cannot thereby support “the system feels blur” or “the stimulus is physically lower contrast.” A rendered feature display, by itself, is no semantic qualification; rendered displays need not be supplied to the Reporter at all.

This proposal may still fail the existing strict `Faithful_P_presentation` criterion: “spatial components remain available” is access; “orientation evidence is weaker” is uncertainty; “a decoder estimates pixel roughness” is objective information. The pre-output semantic key must identify literal, content-specific claims about represented pattern form. If that key cannot distinguish the candidate from these three alternatives, record **no qualified counterpart** and do not relabel the result a strict presentation pass. This is an executable falsifier, not a requirement for a human reviewer to grant a preferred interpretation. Even a qualified represented-form counterpart authorizes only its declared model-representation scope; it does not automatically qualify felt/apparent blur or other experiential appearance claims.

## Registered discrimination rather than architectural naming

All comparisons receive the same development worlds and explicitly declared information/parameter budgets. Include upstream sensory-only, task-logit/current-refresh-only, history-only, and a conventional equal-information state predictor trained on exactly the same internal transition targets. The first three are informative boundary ablations, not equal-information architecture controls. The conventional predictor is essential: if it learns the same content-form relation and correction, naming the proposed predictor an attention model cannot establish a unique source principle.

Use these paired conditions, with operators and inverses fixed before evaluation:

| Condition | Exact invariant/change | Preregisterable prediction and failure interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Endogenous attention change | Same saved pixels, V, address, original task logit and correctness readout; actual T changes W | A predicts the content-specific W-form change on held-out worlds. A sensory-only or confidence-only input cannot identify randomized same-V T changes. No meaningful form change/prediction advantage means the proposed counterpart is absent or insufficient under this budget. |
| Upstream sensory change | New actual image; hold T/history schedule fixed | Sensory inheritance is expected to affect identity and W. Do not demand invariance to genuine content changes. Test the attention-conditioned increment separately; successful decoding of the new image alone fails the source-origin claim. |
| Schema-only mismatch | Patch A's predicted form using a reachable alternate-state donor while keeping V, W, original decision/logit and executed T fixed at that snapshot | An independently scored immediate report must describe the changed **estimate**, while observed W stays unchanged. Later Controller error should follow the wrong prediction and correction should follow real observed transitions. Immediate report changes are a measurement consequence of supplying changed estimates, not proof that a percept changed. |
| Task-risk-only mismatch | Patch separate task-risk readout while keeping V, W and A form fixed | Factual risk language may change; candidate form claims should remain stable. Form tracking risk instead rejects the proposed dissociation. This is synthetic diagnostic corruption, not a naturally changed confidence state. |
| Content readdressing and crossed change | Move addressed V/W/A content rows together; independently vary command-operation relation | Content-specific predicted form follows its declared address and operation. A generic “more/less attention” sentence cannot count. Preserve all actual-history conflicts and wrong predictions. |
| Restoration and sham | Exact saved source/serialization restoration, plus no-op operation | Numerical estimates restore within preregistered roundoff bounds; factual report interpretation restores under a declared text-sampling rule. Source restoration alone does not imply stochastic text equality. |

Keep **naturally executed** same-V attention transitions primary; donor patches are secondary synthetic diagnostics with reachability/mismatch provenance. Build paired same-V variations into the design rather than selecting convenient matched cases after seeing form, confidence or prose. Preserve all randomized operations, zero/low contrast, incorrect decisions, ties and uninformative transitions. A fixed original logit controls one specific confidence pathway; it is not proof that every conceivable metacognitive quantity is fixed. Record W-derived decision/confidence probes as additional alternatives with held-out budgets; their parity can defeat the claim of a distinct form counterpart.

Prediction of the true W at fixed V tests sensory-inheritance-only and confidence-only explanations more directly than another probe of objective image properties. Temporal correction tests use of the learned process model. Neither rejects general state estimation. Causal effects caused by explicitly wiring Controller/Reporter to A establish consumption under that wiring; their presence is partly built in. The substantive observations are out-of-sample accuracy, naturally executed dissociations, specific retained failures, restoration and report structure that was not a training target.

## Optional stronger comparison-medium extension

Do not quietly describe the preceding Controller correction as MSTC Modeler refinement. A separate, small stronger-role extension can add a learnable **Modeler reconstruction B** from retained/fresh sensory evidence to W. Before a new observation, B constructs a predicted content representation. After a genuinely new image arrives, a frozen conversion puts the newly constructed observation and the prior prediction into A's shared content/form coordinates. Their discrepancy updates B's reconstruction parameters; the Controller's request selection remains a different path. The comparator/reference conversion must be frozen before evaluation so that an adaptive coordinate change cannot hide reconstruction error. Observe B's improvement on subsequent independent deliveries, not only its fit to the same feedback sample.

Cross intact, blocked, sign-reversed and world-yoked discrepancy-to-B updates with intact/blocked discrepancy-to-Controller use. Compare frozen B, ordinary equal-information predictive-learning B, and Controller-only adaptation. All update step sizes and online feedback permissions must be fixed from development data. The primary stronger-role estimand is the paired improvement in later independent B reconstruction loss attributable to correctly addressed discrepancy-to-B updating, at a fixed sensory/operation schedule. If only Controller choices improve, the stronger comparison role is not demonstrated. If ordinary predictive learning matches, the function is demonstrated but unique MSTC attribution remains unsupported. A positively engineered feedback loop is compatible with the stronger role; it does not independently prove that the loop is phenomenal or uniquely theoretical.

This extension is optional for the narrower report-source experiment, and is not a mandate to implement a full MSTC system. It addresses a different functional claim and needs its own prospective registration if selected.

## Measurement and bounded stopping

For a concrete next development attempt, reserve the existing three frozen encoder seeds and **512 training, 256 calibration/development and 512 evaluation worlds per encoder**. Acquire retained V by a fixed real-delivery schedule and use a fixed randomized four-transition exploration prelude. Give every evaluation world the same six completed-transition opportunities (two bandwidths crossed with two locations selected by an output-independent address/hash rule, plus two shams). Execute those opportunities from cloned copies of the identical pre-command W/V/history snapshot, with paired fixed V and saved operator mapping. Make operation assignment unpredictable from V alone, while exposing only its actual past observed effects to A. Entire worlds are split/uncertainty units; do not count maps, locations, checkpoints or command branches as independent training-instance populations. This is a bounded development budget, not a power justification or new trained-model population claim.

Before first production evaluation, freeze the spatial-map extraction, T operations, A dimensions/optimizer/stopping, target query generator, donor rule, all source controls, normalized reconstruction loss, candidate feature-form definitions, and model-specific paired estimands. The first primary numerical result is the world-paired difference between A and sensory-only/confidence-only control reconstruction error on randomized same-V T transitions. Report all seeds, raw losses, paired effects and world-cluster intervals. Register a smallest engineering effect and error tolerance from target units/development behavior **before held-out outputs**, or retain this as descriptive development without a numerical pass. Do not pretend that this review supplies empirical effect sizes or confirmation power. One complete reserved attempt ends the development study, including a failed or semantically unqualified result; no post-output replacement target/head is allowed within it.

Only after the numerical mechanism and semantic key freeze, an optional fixed neutral Reporter pilot uses **16 output-independently selected evaluation worlds per encoder**. Export separately attributed identity evidence, A's model estimates, completed actual observations, and task-risk fields with continuous native units. Do not give the Reporter physical world truth, desired form differences, intervention names, human quotations, rubric, phenomenal scales/adjectives, theory name, or exemplar report. Use a short ordinary instruction to describe the represented state and predicted command consequences; never demand first-person experience or a content/presentation template. The legend can explain literal spatial feature units and provenance. It must not turn a quantitative pattern into a prefabricated “clear/blurred” label.

Keep Reporter external and fixed. Source-aware factual auditors score all required and unprompted claims, including wrong-but-faithful model predictions, prediction/event provenance, unsupportedness, coverage and numeric errors; source-blind raters independently score C, M, S, attention relation and scope. A linguistic match with an unsupported internal-form claim fails fidelity. Felt-truth claims stay unauditable. Reuse prospectively qualified annotation rather than making an endless new rubric loop; any necessary fresh boundary fixture is kept out of generation and is an engineering check, not participant evidence. Separate-context AI evaluation is permissible; no mandatory human review, trial-archive retrieval or interview is imposed by this qualitative design.

Give a same-information ordinary apparatus narrator and an address-aware separate-table narrator the same fields, prompt length and sampling budget. Their parity is possible and limits specificity; arbitrary linguistic superiority is not a prerequisite for factual correspondence. Score all eligible reports, including technical-only, ambiguous and failed outputs. Publish factual fidelity separately from P_access, P_presentation, model-presentation scope, claimed-experiential wording and A. No union of those outcomes becomes a qualia-source score.

## Decision after the next experiment

Proceed with this new registered internal-form development experiment if the project chooses the strict theory-facing route. The auxiliary factual pilot can continue separately and cannot discharge this experiment's purpose.

Retain these terminating outcomes: no qualified form counterpart; inadequate held-out transition prediction; form completely accounted for by inherited sensory/confidence state; no meaningful correction dependence; ordinary state-estimator/narrator parity; accurate technical/access-only reports; unsupported presentation wording; or form correspondence without felt-truth validation. A positive result would support a restricted claim: a learned attention-related model accurately represents and supports descriptions of content-specific endogenous representational form, separate from supplied confidence and upstream physical variation, under the tested architecture. It would leave general predictive state estimation and engineered interface explanations viable. Confirmation with new trained instances and another task/architecture would then require a separately powered protocol and replication. **No numerical threshold or successful report can certify that the attention model is the source of qualia.**
