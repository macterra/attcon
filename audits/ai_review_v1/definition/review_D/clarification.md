# Reviewer D: post-first-pass clarification

Date: 2026-10-10. This clarification follows the archived first pass and preserves it unchanged. Additional exposure consists of a parent message clarifying graded evidential interpretation and `docs/AI_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_V1.md`. I have still not viewed outputs, source states, experimental results, prior completed reviews, or other reviewer files.

The parent correctly identifies a potentially overstrong implication in my proposed correspondence primary. Exceeding the strongest ordinary-description baseline is a criterion for a **stronger discrimination claim**, not a necessary condition for every credible incremental contribution. The stated goal is faithful reporting of the attention-control model with independently justified resemblance to consciousness reports. That goal can receive graded support even when an ordinary-description account remains viable. My first-pass phrase “still does not distinguish” assesses specificity; it must not be read as “no useful evidence is possible until ordinary explanations are excluded.”

The assay should distinguish three levels explicitly:

1. **Operational correspondence:** source-grounded reports express a prospectively defined feature pattern independently justified by a consciousness-report reference. Preregister factual-fidelity acceptance rules and reference correspondence measures separately. Freeze features from independent evidence, show that they can be reliably annotated, and evaluate fresh target outputs without tuning to them. Report the actual strength of matching and its uncertainty. A passing result supplies incremental evidence for the proposed link between attention-model contents and consciousness-like report structure. It does not require every feature, or the entire pattern, to be exclusive to consciousness reports.
2. **Specificity relative to measured alternatives:** compare information-matched ordinary narrators and ordinary apparatus reference descriptions. Superiority on a valid, held-out correspondence endpoint would support a stronger interpretation. Parity means the observed resemblance also has a sufficient ordinary-description explanation within this comparison; it weakens the additional discriminative claim but does not erase demonstrated fidelity or independently justified resemblance. Success against a weak template alone deserves little specificity credit. No finite comparator set establishes uniqueness against every possible account.
3. **Subjective experience:** neither successful operational correspondence nor specificity certifies experience. The reporting experiment measures a predicted consequence, with Reporter distinct from the model/Controller. It can change evidential weight without directly observing qualia.

An actionable revision to the first-pass measurement plan is therefore to use operational correspondence as the theory-facing primary, with ordinary-comparator differences as a separately preregistered specificity test. Reference development need not demand perfect separation of consciousness reports from apparatus descriptions. It must justify why the chosen features or joint pattern represent the former, establish annotation validity, and measure overlap with the latter. Preserve the planned controlled interventions as fidelity/source tests. Do not call attribution alone the validated consciousness endpoint.

Prospectively falsifiable outcomes then remain distinct: inaccurate source tracking fails the fidelity prediction; absent or unstable matching to the independently specified reference fails the report-correspondence prediction; ordinary-comparator parity fails a predeclared superiority prediction while allowing low-specificity operational correspondence; reproducible fidelity plus independent reference matching supports the narrower stated reporting claim. Thresholds for reference matching, factual fidelity, and meaningful comparator differences need separate justification and pilot-based power, not one post hoc success label.

I maintain the first-pass concern that v2 currently has no validated theory-facing feature pattern or frozen endpoint. The structural definitions can advance after the targeted revisions; the reference and comparator work can proceed with AI reviewers and transparent limitations. No human-only prerequisite, task-necessity criterion, or consciousness-proof requirement follows from this review.
