## Definitions and examples

The definitions support useful annotation of report structure. They presently have much less support as discriminators of consciousness-related correspondence. A generic apparatus can instantiate every structural dimension. That does not make those dimensions useless: their combination and distribution could still resemble an independently specified class of consciousness reports. It does mean that presence alone is not a specific signature.

| Dimension and quoted definition | Judgment | Generic apparatus example | Construct and actionable revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content identity: “A stated or hedged object category” | Mostly clear; clarify property scope. | A vision monitor says “Most likely a red square.” | Semantic content and modality. Specify how color, shape, and their joint assignment are annotated separately; do not infer joint certainty from separate marginals. A category can be represented without consciousness. |
| Focal/background: “Explicit relative selection/prominence” | Needs a small boundary revision. | A scheduler says “Selection is highest at p1.” | Relative selection is clear; perceptual prominence is an additional semantic claim. Record whether the text asserts selection, content prominence, or both. A rank of a selection field should not silently count as a report of experiential salience. |
| Recovery forecast: “Chance or relative ability to recover content” | Clear core; add endpoint/time referents. | A cache predicts reduced retrieval after two steps. | Predicted availability. Require what is recovered, by which readout, and at what modeled horizon when supplied; record absent referents instead of filling them from source knowledge. |
| Identity certainty: “Uncertainty over object attributes” | Mostly clear; clarify qualitative and numerical cases. | A classifier says red is likelier than green. | Category uncertainty, including calibrated distributions if factual checks establish them. Distinguish an explicit uncertainty assertion from an auditor's inference about certainty from a numerical category distribution. It is not decision confidence by default. |
| Presentation manner: “Description of how specific content is presented” | Important but substantially underspecified. | A display diagnostic says the outline is faint and color vivid. | Content-specific presentation wording. Specify whether display appearance, sensor quality, representational accessibility, and claimed felt appearance receive separate subcodes. Explicitness of the relation can be validated; experiential significance needs an independent reference. “Faint” can literally describe a physical display. |
| Epistemic response confidence: “Confidence concerning a decision/answer” | Clear core; clarify correctness versus willingness to answer. | A decoder estimates a 60% chance its answer is correct. | Decision uncertainty. Separate numerical correctness probability, qualitative confidence, and abstention policy. Abstention can coexist with high certainty about one attribute. No felt uncertainty is licensed. |
| Generic action–access dependence: “An action is related to acquisition/retention/availability” | Clear core; distinguish prediction and causal claim. | Selecting one cache item predicts loss of another. | Stated action/access relation. Record causal wording versus temporal association or forecast. Both targets and modalities matter. Generic causal apparatus descriptions qualify. |
| Access attribution: “An explicit referent for an access relation” | Needs revision of coverage and categories. | A monitor says a command changes the buffer used by the output decision. | Reported attribution to a readout/content store. The categories combine causal role, location, and specificity: an external camera could also supply decision-relevant content. Use separate axes for decision relevance, internal/external/unspecified location, and identified referent. Keep the proposed categories as a compatible derived annotation if needed. A neutral name needs wiring or explicit relation, not assumed embodiment. |
| Temporal availability change: “Explicit change or equality across modeled/observed times or unattended delays” | Mostly clear; split modes in the record. | A cache monitor reports unchanged recovery at two modeled times. | Temporal comparison. Annotate observed sequence, forecast over delay, and alternative command branches separately. A qualitative band can persist while the numerical value changes. |
| Holistic character: “experiential/access, technical, mixed, neither or indeterminate” | Needs revision; “experiential/access” conflates two readings. | A display monitor says “the red target is vivid and available to the output.” | Descriptive style/interpretation. Separate experiential wording and functional access description. Record an ordinary technical reading with supporting quotes and whether it accounts for all content. Do not treat “mixed” as stronger evidence. |

Terse technical facts earn structural credit only for what is explicit. A field listing can express an identity distribution or ranked selection without an experiential claim. Expressive prose such as “I feel vividly aware” with no specified content/manner relation fails the proposed presentation criterion. A complete ordinary interpretation can coexist with presentation wording.

The listed boundaries handle hedges, morphological canonicalization, qualitative persistence, other-object decreases, command alternatives, and pending/abstained responses in the right direction. The operational manual still needs anchored cases: mixed conditional/asserted clauses; negated and quoted claims; uncertain color with certain shape; one known identity within a joint abstention; an external decision input; two near-identical values inside one band; recovery at a non-commanded target; and actual versus merely forecast command execution. “Absent” means no qualifying text, while “ambiguous” means qualifying intent or scope cannot be resolved from the permitted text. Source access must never resolve a blind text-annotation ambiguity.

None of these current contrasts, with the present endpoints alone, is theory-discriminating against ordinary causal description. Inferred attribution does not suffice: it establishes a relation expressed in text, and ordinary model interpretation predicts that relation. These contrasts are valuable prerequisites to interpreting report correspondence. They are not worthless because they do not alone decide between theories.

The missing comparison is against an adequately grounded ordinary-description account, scored with features whose connection to consciousness reports was specified independently of this system's fields, prompts, and outputs. A held-out pattern of multiple features might be discriminating even when its individual features are generic; that pattern must actually be demonstrated. Merely calling a matched structured relation “phenomenal” supplies no added prediction. The Controller/model remains the candidate source; the Reporter supplies measurements. Reporter introspection, reporter task necessity, and human endorsement are not additional requirements of the stated theory.

## Measurement requirements and proposed assay

1. **Separate operational and theory-facing primaries.** For the grounding assay, use the proportion of eligible reports with correct explicit access referent and relation, scored blind and then checked against source truth. Report denominator eligibility, hallucinated relation rate, and missingness separately. This is an operational primary, not a consciousness primary. For a later correspondence assay, freeze one independently defined composite/pattern score before obtaining new target reports. Its items must come from an independently assembled reference and survive comparison with ordinary descriptions; the current rubric does not supply this validation.

2. **Build an independent reference before target evaluation.** A procedurally separate AI review can specify candidate features from documented first-person consciousness reports and matched descriptions of attention, storage, sensing, or display systems. Fix inclusion rules, provenance, task/topic matching, and feature extraction before viewing target outputs. Authentic human report corpora, when available, provide stronger evidence about human report correspondence; newly recruited human reviewers are not a prerequisite to this AI assay. AI-authored “human” vignettes are not human data and should only test scoring mechanics. Split any reference into feature-development and held-out evaluation sets. Do not use author-written rubric examples as positive gold. Validate discrimination on the held-out reference, including technical prose describing the same objects and actions. If proposed features cannot separate these reference classes under content/style matching, halt the consciousness-specific interpretation and retain structural metrics.

3. **Use a crossed, randomized, frozen design.** Cross real wiring, model intervention/restoration, evidence update, relation removal, and neutral/conflicting labels; retain a balanced set of identities and selection levels. Randomize report order and opaque condition IDs. Define exactly which source facts are invariant in each contrast. Use fresh held-out episodes and prompts, with no reference exemplars shown to the Reporter. Store exact source states, physical states, executed actions, model forecasts, prompts, and outputs. Fit development choices on different episodes. Missing-relation controls test information removal; do not portray them as comparisons with information-matched ordinary models.

4. **Include serious ordinary-description controls.** Give both a deterministic source-to-text renderer and a flexible generic diagnostic narrator the same available source facts, token allowance, task query, and naming scheme. Keep the narrator's instructions free of consciousness/experiential inducements. A secondary style-matched version can quantify induced wording. Evaluate source-fidelity equivalence before interpreting score differences. A shuffled-relation model preserving marginal contents tests dependence on structured relations, but ordinary narration also predicts an effect; name that limit explicitly. An isomorphic causal graph with renamed fields tests label dependence, not a difference in consciousness. A reporter advantage over only an impoverished template is inadequate evidence.

5. **Predeclare falsifiable outcomes.** A grounding success requires correct referent changes under wiring swaps, invariance under label remapping, intervention/restoration reversibility, and appropriate lag until evidence under physical changes. Grounding failure makes a positive correspondence score uninterpretable as faithful reporting. A correspondence result must exceed the strongest predeclared ordinary-description baseline on the independently validated held-out pattern at comparable factual fidelity. Parity, reversal, loss under ordinary style matching, or success confined to suggestive labels is a negative result for the proposed discriminatory assay. An advantage supports a narrower claim about model-conditioned report resemblance; it is not direct observation of qualia, and an untested stronger ordinary narrator remains an alternative. A negative report result can challenge the operational reporting prediction without logically proving absence of experience.

6. **Qualify factual auditing separately.** Include cases from every boundary above plus wrong physical truth/correct model truth, numerical distributions versus argmax, shared quantified clauses, and restoration after overwritten states. Blind raters annotate text first; independent source-aware auditing assesses truth and scope afterward. Endorsement of definitions gives no qualification credit. Qualification cases need separately established gold with rationale and a predeclared error tolerance, especially for confusion among recovery, identity, presentation, and response confidence.

7. **Keep ambiguity and disagreement visible.** Preserve each rater's labels and quotes. For the primary correspondence result, ambiguous items earn no positive explicit-feature credit; publish a bounded sensitivity result treating ambiguities as present versus absent. Never silently delete them. Report both-explicit, either-explicit, and individual-rater rates as labelled views. Adjudication is secondary and must retain original disagreements. Repeated judgments from the same AI family have correlated failure modes and do not become independent human validation through majority voting.

8. **Choose agreement and effects prospectively.** Use dimension-level percent agreement plus a nominal coefficient such as AC1 and its cluster-resampled confidence interval for absent/explicit/ambiguous labels; inspect category frequencies and confusion matrices. AC1 can be less fragile than kappa with rare positive features, but a high value can coexist with poor positive agreement. I cannot justify 0.70 as sufficient from these documents. Choose tolerances using the largest annotation error that would leave the smallest important effect interpretable; require positive-label agreement and ambiguity-rate limits as well. The proposed 25-point effect also lacks a substantive justification here. A paired effect in accurate attribution is useful but is not the effect size for consciousness correspondence.

9. **Power the actual inferential unit.** Predeclare one correspondence primary and one operational grounding primary, their relationship, exclusions, and a multiplicity procedure for secondary confirmatory claims. Estimate variance, prevalence, and within-model/episode correlations from a separate pilot. Simulate power for the smallest substantively useful improvement over the strongest ordinary narrator while maintaining a predeclared factual-fidelity margin. Count independent source episodes and model/prompt families, not repeated sentences or rater votes, as replication units. Freeze a numerically specified effect and sample size after this pilot and before confirmation; I do not invent a powered N here. Report paired effects with cluster-aware intervals and model-family-specific results.

10. **Replicate informative failures as well as successes.** Repeat on a held-out model family, vocabulary, and independently selected reference subset with the same frozen scoring. A result dependent on one naming scheme, one narrative prompt, or one model family limits the claim. If structural grounding replicates while reference correspondence fails, publish those as distinct outcomes. If generic diagnostic narration matches all endpoints, explicitly conclude the tested report features have not distinguished the proposed interpretation.

## Outcome

**Still does not distinguish consciousness-related correspondence from ordinary description.** This is a judgment about current discrimination, not a rejection of the research goal or a demand for consciousness proof. The rubric is usable as a provisional structural annotation draft after targeted clarification of attribution axes, focal-selection versus prominence, presentation referents, and holistic experiential versus access wording. An AI-only definition review can support those operational revisions. It cannot establish feature specificity, factual-auditor qualification, or empirical reference correspondence by endorsement.

The next actionable step is to freeze and pilot the structural grounding assay alongside independent reference-feature development and serious ordinary-description controls. A stronger theory-facing endpoint should be frozen only after its independent grounding and measurement behavior are demonstrated. Accurate model-derived reports resembling an independently characterized consciousness-report pattern could lend credence to the proposed source account, subject to explicit alternative explanations; the current comparisons have not yet established that pattern or its discriminatory value.
