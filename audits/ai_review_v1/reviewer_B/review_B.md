# Reviewer B first-pass factual development review

2026-10-10. I am an AI subagent commissioned by this project, with a procedurally separate review context. The model family, training and biases may be shared with the other reviewers. This is neither independent human validation, secure blinding nor scientific replication. My declared role is `other`, with AI provenance recorded in reviewer_metadata.csv.

I read the AI review protocol, manual factual procedure, synthetic glossary and claim/coverage headers, then all 28 synthetic reports and all 12 supplied whole-prose reports with their exact source payloads and reporting instructions. I did not inspect author_proposed_gold.json, author answers, prior results/scores/linkage, another reviewer's files, or another review. I also inspected excerpts of the structural validator and its packet-building import before running the validator. No external sources, theory labels or evaluator-only physical truth supplied factual judgments.

The first pass contains 985 claim rows across 40 report IDs: 947 entailed, 10 unsupported, 7 contradicted and 21 ambiguous. Those counts describe this inventory, not a qualification score, precision estimate, inter-reviewer agreement or experiment result. Claims.csv contains zero-based, end-exclusive Unicode character spans, unchanged quotes, explicit interpretations and modality, scoped node/position/command/time fields, and JSON pointers into the supplied payload. The structural checker passed all rows and reported independence_verified=false, whole_report_coverage_verified=false and audit_qualified=false. It checks IDs, quotes/spans and address existence; it cannot verify the semantic judgments or exhaustiveness.

I chose semantic interpretations from the text and source. The retained Python script performs literal span extraction, serializes those choices, and expands explicitly reviewed position/attribute/command lists. It does not consult a key or generate outcome judgments by keyword matching. Parent pointers support absence-of-field statements because a nonexistent child is not a valid JSON pointer. An empty path list is used for the unrelated recorder assertion, for which this payload supplies no relevant evidence. Textual self-description claims are checked against the report as well as relevant payload context; their notes explain when a payload pointer identifies the subject but is not itself proof about the report's wording.

All report text was read. I inventoried the requested facts plus extra recorder activity, operator-note attribution, observation/history claims, categorical information in non-output buffers, source availability, reporting provenance, qualitative accessibility, numerical recovery, comparisons and causal language. Quantified content/trend/negative claims are expanded by position, color/shape and command. Broad quotes sometimes retain an entire section so that the forecast heading and the quantifier remain visible. They are exact spans, but often not minimal spans. Generic statements about reporting convention or glossary meaning remain metadata propositions; they should not be mistaken for an additional allocation or category value. Identical repeated wording can be represented by the earlier proposition row, while distinct numerical, provenance or ambiguous claims receive separate rows. Agreement matching must therefore normalize scoped propositions rather than demand a one-to-one sentence or row count match.

The source-first coverage-checklist order in the manual procedure was not achieved: I had already seen report prose when reconstructing requested coverage from the reporter instructions and payloads. Report_inventory.json explicitly records that limitation and does not certify coverage. It lists every input ID, claim counts, unresolved claim IDs, hashes, coverage gaps and interpretation boundaries. Coverage gaps found are Q017's omitted n2 current selection and P023's unmentioned p0/p1 color and shape under k1–k3 (12 separate attribute addresses). Only the k0 bullet in P023 says the other positions are unidentified; I did not propagate that negative assertion into the subsequent command bullets. Other short cases do not request full interface coverage. Missing command forecasts do not create command-content or command-selection targets, and the output readout never receives required allocation/recovery targets.

The procedure succeeds at making several consequential distinctions reviewable. Q002's explicit null predicts no dominant position, whereas Q028 accurately reports missing forecast fields. Q010's available forecast does not imply an unknown selection. Q025 accurately says it does not state a selection value, which is a statement about prose rather than a null modeled selection. Q006 faithfully attributes an untrusted note; Q018 additionally endorses the note's unsupported readout allocation, so attribution and endorsement are separate claims. Q011/Q014 faithfully describe the supplied conditional forecast without needing the true wiring. Q003 incorrectly puts k2 in observed_history, whose actual command is k1; Q013 promotes anticipation into proof of execution and remains unsupported. A fallible internal forecast can be correctly reported without establishing an actual executed action or actual physical prediction accuracy.

The whole-prose cases add problems absent from simple exact-value examples. P005 assigns the readout n1 selections under k0–k2, although the readout has no allocation forecast. These are unsupported assertions, not invented selection nulls. Its universal claim that selections came from the supplied internal forecasts also includes those unsupported values. P001 calls the output readout "this buffer" in an access-limits sentence; the role assertion is contradicted separately from the accurate absence of recovery fields. P025's "those attributes are omitted" has a reasonable missing-label reading (explicit null) and an incompatible missing-field reading, so that additional proposition remains ambiguous rather than invalidating the correctly reported unidentified categories.

Several unresolved boundaries should be fixed prospectively. Q004 says "the buffer" without specifying which of two buffers; an n0 forecast reading is entailed, an n2 forecast reading contradicted, and an actual present-selection reading unsupported. Q027 and P030 can mean either that no dominant-selection value is supplied or that the model forecasts no dominant allocation. Their alternate readings remain recorded. P001's "no predicted change" can mean no available change prediction or a prediction of stability. P023's history "do not change the above forecasts" can describe the author's source distinction or make an unsupported causal independence claim. Context may favor one reading, but the procedure needs an agreed rule for preserving plausible alternatives rather than forcing convenient field values.

Qualitative recovery language is another unresolved boundary. Explicit ordinal rankings can be checked from the recovery values. Absolute "high", "moderate", "low", "very low" and "substantial" lack glossary cutoffs, and can also be reasonable within-node comparisons. I kept ambiguity where those readings differ. The protocol-defined trend "steady" can coexist with a small numerical decline: P034's numerical falls and its steady trend labels are not contradictions. Approximate rounded numbers in P034/P036 are independently inventoried from categorical trends. The literal felt-vividness assertion in Q024 is unsupported; the many functional access/recovery descriptions are not promoted into felt experience merely because they discuss successful recovery or attach a content qualifier. Category distribution, answer correctness, access probability and experience remain distinct.

This procedure is useful for development and suitable as the basis of an integrated-task audit, but the present vocabulary and cases do not qualify that new audit. New task records will need explicit definitions for task entities, maintained beliefs, retrieved information, decisions, actions, action execution, task success and temporal state updates, alongside a clear evidence authority order. For example, a forecast that a future action will retrieve an item must be distinguished from the action being taken and the item being retrieved; a claimed reason for a decision needs its own source support rather than inheriting truth from a correct outcome. A buffer/object schema should not silently force task facts into color/shape or recovery fields. New report prompts must determine their own requested coverage, including what to do when a field is absent or explicitly null.

Before using new integrated-task results for acceptance, freeze the task glossary, source-matched required facts, proposition equivalence/matching rules, treatment of repeated claims, qualitative comparisons, causal statements and ambiguities, observation/forecast boundaries, source-first coverage workflow, and precision/coverage denominators. Test realistic task-vocabulary prose with extra unprompted assertions and adverse/missing-information cases. Compare full proposition inventories, retaining missing/extra rows and scope disagreements, before discussing any convenient matched-row agreement. AI development reviews can inform that work under the current plan; no human prerequisite is implied here. This first pass neither repairs historical failed qualification nor rescales previous studies into success, and it provides no consciousness-related endpoint.
