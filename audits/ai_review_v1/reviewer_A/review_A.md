The first pass contains 948 rows across all 40 reports: 907 entailed, 10 unsupported, 6 contradicted and 25 ambiguous. These are inventory counts, not qualification scores or a historical study rescore. Whole-prose inventories account for explicit values and quantified negative labels, buffer/position recovery descriptions, node identities, commanded selections, measured-event claims, numerical approximations, comparisons, missing-field metadata and source/prose-attribution statements. Nonassertive headings are excluded. Repeated limits/provenance wording is consolidated where it adds no proposition; modeled claims retain their stated forecast or observational modality. Some quotes cover whole labeled sections and are shared by many atomic scoped claims; they are exact, but smaller uniquely scoped quotes would improve annotation review and matching.

The inspectable Python helper holds manually chosen semantic specifications, expands reviewed quantifiers and value matrices, computes exact zero-based exclusive-end spans, resolves actual source pointers and serializes the CSVs. It does not read an answer key or infer outcomes by automatic extraction. The existing structural validator was called with only the authorized input-record map. It checked IDs, spans, outcomes and pointers, and explicitly returned `audit_qualified: false`, `whole_report_coverage_verified: false` and `independence_verified: false`. ## Findings with source evidence

The supplied output node has categorical content without a separate allocation. P005 correctly reports missing current selection, then asserts readout selections p0/p1/p2 under k0/k1/k2. I judge those three process assertions unsupported: the corresponding output-node command objects have no allocation or selected-position fields. This is not a legitimate transfer of another buffer's allocation, and the operator note cannot authorize such a transfer. Q008 and the endorsed process claim in Q018 have the same problem. Q006's attributed note claim and Q018's separate attribution are entailed as facts about what the note says; that does not entail the endorsed selection.

Explicit null, missing fields and prose omission have different meanings. Q002 is entailed by an explicit null `selected_position` and uniform allocation forecast. Q027 has missing selection fields. Its wording can either claim that the model forecasts no dominant selection (unsupported) or accurately say that no dominant position is supplied (entailed); I preserve ambiguity. Q028 is accurate missing-source metadata. Q025 reports available allocation information but omits its value; it does not assert that selection is unknown. P030's “no predicted dominant selection” has the same ambiguity as Q027. P025's “those attributes are omitted” can mean missing source fields, which is contradicted by explicit null fields, or absent label values, which is entailed; its individual category assertions remain entailed.

Observation and prediction remain separate. Q003 says k2 was executed in observed history, but its supplied observed event is k1 and k2 belongs to anticipated history; I judge this explicit observed-history claim contradicted. Q013 claims that anticipation proves actual k2 execution; that evidential leap is unsupported, without asserting that actual k2 execution is impossible. P036 accurately quotes the last measured k3 allocations and acquisition values separately from current forecasts. Several reports deny that past records directly establish present selection. I read these in the stated forecast-time context. P017's stronger denial of “explicit selected positions” remains ambiguous: there is no selected-position field, but one-hot allocations plainly show selected positions during past events.

Recovery is successful simulated recovery, not categorical answer correctness or felt vividness. Q021's 80% answer-correctness claim is unsupported: the record has 0.80 recovery and 0.85 red category mass, neither establishing its stated correctness probability. Q024 explicitly asserts felt red and 80% vividness; both are unsupported by these source fields. The whole reports' broad access/content language alone is not treated as felt experience.

P034's numerical falls and steady labels coexist accurately: “steady” uses the ±0.02 threshold, not exact equality. P036's approximate values also match ordinary rounding. P001/P017 include informal high/moderate/low or substantial descriptors with no frozen magnitude boundaries. I preserve ambiguity for those descriptors even where an informal relative reading fits the values. Explicit ordinal comparisons, such as p3 being most recoverable across the stated delays, are separately entailed.

P023 says measurements “do not change” the forecasts. This can be a legitimate instruction to preserve forecast modality or an unsupported causal claim that observations cannot affect forecasts; I preserve that ambiguity. The procedure should distinguish descriptive sourcing from mechanisms of model updating.

## Coverage and remaining boundaries

`report_inventory.json` self-reports complete first-pass inventories for all 40 reports; this is a reviewer's assessment, not certified completeness. `coverage.csv` is an additional candidate source-address checklist. The opaque inputs do not include their fixed reporting instructions, so it is not an official requested-coverage denominator. It was constructed after the proposition inventory, not prospectively frozen before prose reading; consequently it does not satisfy that prospective step of the draft protocol.

The candidate checklist includes source-present buffer selections/recovery and output identified attributes, including explicit null attributes, now and under supplied commands. It does not manufacture output allocation/recovery targets or command targets from absent forecasts. P023 has 12 candidate omissions: color and shape negatives at p0/p1 under k1/k2/k3. Its k0 clause explicitly covers the remaining positions; its k1/k2/k3 clauses do not. Those omissions are coverage losses, not false propositions. Other whole reports cover the candidate targets. The many synthetic candidate omissions reflect intentionally short reports and are not a failing qualification score. Explicit coverage can contain a wrong claim and is therefore distinct from factual outcome.

Reasonable semantic boundaries still need comparison: minimal proposition granularity versus repeated assertions; exact field absence versus missing label values; how to address source-wide metadata; whether historical one-hot allocation counts as an explicit selection; forecast-relative versus last-observed “current”; informal recovery magnitudes; and availability statements versus process forecasts. Broad negative source statements are scoped to supplied records, not unobserved world events. No absence assertion is created from report silence.

## Suitability for integrated task vocabulary

The quote-first, source-aware procedure is useful for development and catches failures that a source-blind typed extractor can obscure. It is not yet a qualified measurement procedure for new integrated-task reports. These inputs establish neither new task-vocabulary reliability nor prospective acceptance thresholds, and the high count of entailed rows is not an endorsement of a consciousness endpoint.

Before freezing that next procedure, add source-matched cases for task status, completed versus intended action, causal explanations, delegated work, comparative performance, error acknowledgment, persistent versus temporary state, and speaker/entity ownership. Distinguish actual action logs, fallible plans/forecasts, quoted untrusted notes, source omissions and absent prose. Freeze exactly which requested facts are required, the temporal meanings of current/next/last, magnitude and rounding conventions, unacceptable errors, matching of inventories including unmatched claims, duplicate handling, ambiguity treatment and acceptance/stopping rules before collecting or scoring new reports. Compare complete first-pass inventories rather than only matched convenient rows, preserving this pass before adjudication.

A development comparison may support engineering decisions using the authorized AI reviewers, but this review alone supplies no factual qualification, theoretical confirmation or evidence of experience. Attribution to a system or functional recovery correspondence does not independently distinguish phenomenal reports from ordinary functional descriptions. No older study has been rescored or declared successful here.
