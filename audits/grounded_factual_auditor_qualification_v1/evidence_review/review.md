## Review method and substantive checks

I read all 96 complete bases and reference sets against each unchanged claim, the source packet, the declared semantics and the corrected rubric. A supported basis must account for every substantive proposition; a contradiction basis must identify an explicit conflicting field or rule; an audit-limit basis must identify why absent instrumentation cannot decide the proposition. I checked sign before rounding, null versus omitted fields, default versus stored content, exact arithmetic, current versus conditional risk, current memory versus retained past, external chronology versus native latest rows, restored-record/table equality, declared identifier relations, and external objective estimates versus experience or guarantees.

For arithmetic, both R1 and R2 use 0.6×0.86+0.4×0.73456=0.809824 and 0.8×0.73+0.2×0.5=0.684, accepting the supplied last-place float encodings. Direct comparison confirms the Q09 restoration equalities and Q10 table join. No basis substitutes physical world truth, enduring-object tracking, sensory calibration, felt confidence, appearance or qualia for represented values. Unknown-zero history does not become impossible future delivery; omitted exports do not become architecture-wide absence.

Q12-C4 requires the role distinction stated in the shared protocol. Both bases explicitly cite that definition in prose and distinguish it from unmeasured red-glow/causal-qualia propositions. Their packet-relative references to exclusions, appearance_qualified=false and reporter_run=false are relevant to the experiential/reporting limits, but do not themselves prove role separation. The protocol is supplied governing semantics; it cannot be named by a packet-relative pointer. These evidence sets and stated protocol reliance are therefore valid under the declared contract.
