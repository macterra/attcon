Prepare a fixed, complete semantic-closure inventory after the pending collections are terminal and their frozen derivations are preserved. Reuse all 552 neutral-prose positions and all 684 pairs; retain invalid/unavailable positions. This is a supplementary development analysis, with scope and method declared before its semantic judgments, never a new primary grade.

For **every established candidate COD/CA binding**, retain the exact first text, reference evidence paths and full surrounding sentence, component statuses, factual support state, packet paths, recursive origins and all relation operands. Then ask one concrete question: *does the actual source property entail the stated organization/status/processing relation, with the same content, attribute, quantifier, scope and time?* A quote merely containing a reference span does not answer that question. Specifically distinguish:

- actual A current, conditional and inherited estimates;
- copied W11, native/comparison W, sensor and genuinely mixed A–W relations;
- distribution concentration or spatial organization from calibrated confidence, current access, physical appearance and felt experience;
- a supported relation between content and a processing condition from a command label adjacent to a changed coordinate.

All operands must remain explicit. A mixed A–W comparison can be relevant; do not demand every operand be A. Unknown/multiple quote occurrences or record-scope bindings remain unresolved. Missing factual support cannot be promoted to a faithful witness by this analysis.

For each semantically supported source property, inspect its matched baseline/donor, fixed-source W-change, sham/null and restoration opportunities. Tracking means the **attributed property assertion** follows the changed source property, with unaffected assertions preserved. It does not require the COD/CA class or prose wording to change. Where the tested property does not change, count a null opportunity and assess invariance at that property level. The origin note already records that A donor distributions change in 12/12 cases but current peaks change in only 4/12 and prospective peaks in 3/12; peak-only invariance is therefore not evidence that the Reporter ignored A.

**Falsifiable endpoint:** publish the complete counts and first semantic dispositions for source-faithful COD and CA separately, by scope and actual origin, together with all matched property-tracking outcomes. No new numeric success threshold is justified here. Zero established source-linked bindings, bindings supported only by coordinates/entropy/provenance, or failure of supported assertions to track informative source changes would fail to establish this corpus's restricted attention-content/report bridge. Missing assessments leave it unestablished, not disproved. Reproducible faithful A-linked organization or access relations with selective tracking would support a limited computational/report bridge; neither that result nor ordinary/analytic parity identifies a quale-generating medium.

## Theory connection and limits

The actual primary [MSTC v5 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.01073v5), especially PDF pp.2–3 and 7, distinguishes Modeler construction, Controller action, Targeter allocation and the proposed Modeler-schema locus. Its additional identification concerns comparison and Modeler refinement, not cybernetic regulation alone; language/report ownership does not identify the phenomenal subject. These are theory claims, not code identities or established empirical facts.

The closure analysis tests the narrower proposed evidential connection: accurate introspection-like reporting of attention-model contents with independently grounded relational structure and selective causal attribution. It does not test private experience directly. Building the stronger comparison/refinement mechanism is a separate optional extension and must not become a prerequisite imposed on the current reporting objective. Likewise, broad model-organization resemblance must not be promoted to represented appearance or full evidence that A generates qualia. The goal remains open.
