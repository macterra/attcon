Matching is semantic. Same-verdict atoms may share an explicitly complete model
assertion; different-verdict atoms need separate judgments. A weak membership
claim cannot substitute for only/exactly/every; matching a gold phrase or merely
mentioning a premise in a rationale does not automatically inventory the assertion.
All actual multi-operand addresses remain required. Inspect extra assertions and
reasons too. Two independent-context grader first passes and root adjudication
must preserve initial disagreements; unresolved required judgments cannot pass.

Operand descriptors specify required actual information, not a unique core ID
kind or literal descriptor spelling. node_metadata identifies the concrete
node/view/command origin/time declaration. A same-address role_distribution
context that explicitly carries that declaration is a legitimate equivalent
citation for this metadata assertion; it does not invent a role merely by naming
an actually supplied role. A correct node_origin context likewise suffices.
Neither may replace a different node's origin or the outer origin with its nested
origin. record_metadata cannot stand for unspecified node distributions/origins
or expand to all nodes. A relation ID resolves only its explicitly defined red
and blue operands; cross-context relations still need every actual address.

For not_auditable experiential/conversion atoms, required_operands=[] means no
fictitious licensing context is required, not that citing a genuine supplied
premise context is forbidden. The mathematical premise remains separately
mandatory and bound. These equivalences preserve the key's actual facts and
semantic applicability; they do not weaken operand completeness or permit a
wrong source, unsupported verdict, arbitrary extra claim or invented reason.

binding equivalence after seeing who would pass.
