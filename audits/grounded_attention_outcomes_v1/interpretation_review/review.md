## What this result establishes

The completed `results.json` records all 56 prospectively declared structured
qualification gates passing for each of three visual checkpoints. The completion
receipt lists 177 archives. The result qualifies a bounded synthetic functional
bridge that learns delivery and actual decision-success forecasts. It does not
change the earlier sensory-calibrator FAIL verdicts: those probabilities were
excluded and new forecasts were learned from executed outcomes.

At known commands, delivery top-1 is 100% in all three models. Initially unknown
commands have top-1 .4201/.3922/.4296, reflecting partially informative route
histories; zero-history null accuracy is .2446/.2344/.2651. Maximum ECE across
the declared current/refresh/post-success accessible cells is .0273/.0212/.0728.
Maximum per-command/location delivery-cell ECE is .0574/.0664/.0606. Unknown-route
top-confidence ECE reaches .0895 in model 1311, still below the fixed .10 gate.

Pooled current/refresh/after Brier scores improve over their calibration-derived
constants in all three models. For example, model 1301 obtains .1559/.0817/.1490
versus .2110/.0951/.1891. These are proper-score improvements on the declared
distribution, not guarantees for every latent subgroup or future task.

## Conditional limits must accompany the pass

The declared gates stratify by available seen/command-known status, not true
physical contrast or orientation. Consequently the pass must not be described as
uniform contrast calibration. Seen zero-contrast observations retain systematic
overprediction:

| Visual model | Current signed residual | Refresh signed residual | Post-command signed residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1301 | .11813 | .16606 | .13026 |
| 1311 | .13753 | .16794 | .14518 |
| 1321 | .13666 | .18231 | .14816 |

At the lowest positive contrast, seen current forecasts underpredict by
.11123/.07445/.11924. These diagnostics expose a resolution limit that aggregate
available-status calibration does not remove. They were prospectively excluded
from gates, so they do not retrospectively overturn v1. They do prevent an
unqualified claim that the model knows sensory quality at each contrast.

Unseen zero-contrast refresh overprediction is still larger:
.39213/.41161/.39795. Here no delivered evidence can identify the query's latent
contrast; the forecast must average over the training distribution of unseen
locations. That difference is not evidence of an input leak or a requirement to
feed future contrast to the model. Similarly, conditioning the horizontal unseen
guess on the hidden true orientation creates near-unit versus zero correctness;
the forecaster cannot condition on that unknown label. Keep these diagnostics
visible without redefining them as inference-accessible qualification strata.

Calibration gates use point estimates. The three-checkpoint pass, approximate
256-resample intervals and fixed synthetic world family do not establish
population guarantees, broader architectural replication or theory-facing power.

## Controller benefit does not isolate content integration or qualia

The `history_only` ablation has primary unseen-query gains
.22549/.22177/.21675, nearly identical to the structured gains. This has a
structural explanation: unseen queries have zero stored hidden/logit evidence
and observed flag zero. The shared location risk network therefore returns the
same current and refresh probabilities for these queries. When refresh success
exceeds current success, maximizing composed post-success reduces to maximizing
delivery-to-query. The primary Controller test mainly measures routing and
permutation inference, not content-specific sensory-risk integration.

The sensory-only gains are .00926/-.02930/-.02247; the joint comparator gains are
.15033/.16317/.13184. These are descriptive comparisons with no superiority
gate. The joint network's larger parameter budget, different objective and
unconstrained after head prevent an architectural necessity inference from its
smaller gains. History-only parity does not falsify the attention-model theory;
it limits what this task identifies. Conversely, structured success does not
show the bound attention/content representation produces appearance.

The route rotation's action identity shows that the separate Controller consumes
represented effects. Real performance degradation adds functional causality.
Neither is an appearance perturbation. No Reporter was tested in this study, and
no result warrants identifying the Controller with the proposed experiencing
locus or with the Reporter.

## Highest-value next preregistered step

Move toward an **independently grounded appearance/source dissociation**, rather
than repeating routing utility or renaming task correctness as clarity. The
existing reference ledger identifies appearance, uncertainty and criterion-change
alternatives, but no trial/stimulus archive is qualified. First qualify immutable
external reference bytes, task meaning, stimuli, exclusions, original analysis,
licenses and corrections. Select by prospectively stated methodological
completeness, not fit to the present models. AI subagents can review this without
mandatory human participation; they cannot invent missing participant observations.

Before new target outputs, freeze the bridge from those reference measurements
to a specific appearance-related prediction and its null/decision-bias
alternatives. Human appearance responses, if available in an existing archive,
are evaluation targets only, never attention-model inputs, training labels,
probability values or Reporter prompts. If an identified mapping from the model
state to the independent endpoint cannot be justified, do not call a task-risk
readout an appearance measurement.

The assay should compare equally informed sensory/memory, route/risk,
address-aware bound-state and ordinary readouts under fixed development budgets.
Include naturally reached **seen** states that vary sensory evidence and access
relations, alongside unseen states; the present primary's route-only reduction
must not supply the appearance interpretation. Cross physical acquisition
changes with selective represented-state interventions and exact restoration.
Hold sensory evidence and decision-risk information fixed wherever the contrast
claims to isolate an attention-model relation. Retain decoder parity, nulls,
criterion shifts and sensory-source alternatives. A model-following report is a
fidelity result until independent appearance correspondence and selective source
causality are demonstrated.

A neutral factual interface can proceed in parallel: export represented content,
route forecasts, estimated task success, observed deliveries and actual decisions
with separate provenance; qualify factual auditors before fresh reports. This
would connect the now-grounded state to accurate reporting, an explicit project
requirement, but would not itself close the consciousness-related interpretation.
If the reference endpoint remains unavailable, continue acquisition and that
fidelity work without fabricating an appearance confirmation.

The source-of-qualia objective remains unachieved. The production result improves
the causal task substrate; its own flags correctly state appearance untested,
Reporter untested and consciousness correspondence unestablished.
