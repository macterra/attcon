# Qualitative source-counterpart review v1

2026-10-10. Independent commissioned AI methods review, `/root/theory_endpoint_gap_review`. This is a first-pass post-output interpretation and next-assay review. No new worlds, model inference, fits, reports, source edits or human ratings were produced.

**Verdict: objective image-property information is qualified; a strict model-presentation counterpart remains unqualified.** The completed physical assay makes a neutral, source-auditable reporting pilot implementable. Such a pilot can establish factual fidelity, current-access structure and report provenance. It cannot establish the user's consciousness-report correspondence goal merely by succeeding at those auxiliary measures. The source-of-qualia goal remains open; the present strict counterpart verdict is negative, not a claim that the model lacks experience.

## What the completed assay establishes

The [results](../../docs/GROUNDED_PHYSICAL_INFORMATION_V1_RESULTS.md), [numerical review](../grounded_physical_information_v1/results_review/review.md) and [exact replay](../grounded_physical_information_v1/verification.json) support recoverability of two objective properties of the latest delivered image from the frozen structured model's risk hidden state. Pooled held-out R² for image-value SD/neighbor RMS is `.615/.608`, `.840/.760`, `.867/.783` across the three fixed models. Sensory features recover more strongly: `.946/.888`, `.948/.879`, `.937/.876`. Current/refresh readouts recover less strongly in the specified pooled comparisons; superiority has retained stratum exceptions. Joint and route readouts provide no stronger case. These are fixed probe comparisons with unequal dimensions/readout budgets, not information-theoretic independence or attention-specific generation of content.

The targets are pooled RGB/spatial pixel-value SD and neighbor-difference RMS. They include noise/clipping/channel effects. Neither target measures apparent detail, perceived contrast, blur, felt vividness or subjective clarity. The extra risk-hidden information can be inherited from sensory input. Auxiliary stored-logit-sign reconstruction measures agreement with the existing stored decision, including mistakes; it does not certify actual orientation or the nature of a percept.

The existing memory rotation establishes addressed local source/readout equivariance, subject to preregistered floating-point tolerances, with exact inverse restoration. It moves sensory rows and address-specific risks together. The route-output patch preserves local content/risk/readouts and recomposes after-command forecasts by construction. Neither intervention isolates an appearance variable. The route patch does not intervene on a neural hidden state or execute an observation. The numerical review's 504 initial empty-bin serialization flags and their adjudication remain part of the evidence history; this review does not replace that first pass with an initially clean audit.

I inspected the frozen source definitions, completed JSON/status documentation and reviews. I did not independently rerun numerical verification or load raw tensor/checkpoint archives. Numerical claims here rely on the preserved post-output arithmetic audit and replay with their stated limits.

| Candidate claim | Present source support | Endpoint consequence |

| A location has a retained sensory sample; its stored raw orientation coordinate favors one task category; the source predicts particular current/refresh/command outcomes | Direct native inputs/readouts and declared computation, allowing mistaken predictions | Factual/source claims; potentially `P_access` if the report explicitly separates content identification from current representational access. | An external fixed decoder of risk hidden estimates image-value SD or neighbor RMS at a sampled location | Qualified external physical-target readout with held-out aggregate errors and provenance | Objective image-property estimate. It can supply an attribute for C, but supplies neither M_presentation nor S by itself. |
| A pattern is presented as crisp, faint, indistinct, fragmentary or clear to the model | No independently qualified native presentation variable or semantic intervention in these sources | Linguistic `P_presentation` could occur, but the claimed counterpart cannot pass `Faithful_P_presentation` on current evidence. Experiential wording remains an unauditable subset, not an automatic verified match. |

An observed flag establishes retention of a sample, not completeness of its features, general recognizability or presence/absence of experience. A raw logit is an uncalibrated source coordinate. Its sign defines a stored task preference; its magnitude must not become a certainty or clarity scale. Current/refresh are calibrated forecasts of task-answer correctness under their original calibration, not probabilities of being conscious or seeing clearly. A decoder estimate is not physical ground truth at an individual location and is not automatically the model's own property estimate.

The [primary reference review](../appearance_report_reference_review_v1/review.md) supports semantic relations without raw participant trial files. Ramsøy/Overgaard's author-presented typical report separates property answers and reported presentation; it lacks a participant/trial identifier. Lutz's named subject quotation is from trained reporting and describes emergence/attention rather than a universal clarity increase. These are independent publications, not a representative transcript corpus or validated classifier. The project's abstraction must retain those statuses. The proposed strict definition in the [design](../../docs/QUALITATIVE_REPORT_CORRESPONDENCE_V1_DESIGN.md) is appropriately stronger than access structure. The papers do not supply a mapping from these machine pixel statistics, stored flags or task forecasts to their presentation descriptions.

## Smallest executable reporting pilot

Freeze an **auxiliary factual/source-provenance pilot**, explicitly separate from a strict presentation confirmation study. Absence of a qualified presentation counterpart should gate the strict claim, not prohibit neutral reporting about qualified source facts. Preserve the design's preselected-world/query rule, all opportunities and stopping; do not select examples resembling the references or satisfying appearance vocabulary. A small fresh batch can test instrumentation without becoming evidence that the goal was achieved.

The interface should have three separable exports:

1. **Native current representation.** Per neutral location identifier: retained-sample flag and native raw orientation coordinate/category preference. For unseen locations, mark the coordinate as a default guess with no retained sample, rather than a horizontal observation. This is source representation, not truth. If an audit needs actual historical timing/delivery, archive it separately. Do not silently add full command chronology to a source that only consumes its latest per-command one-hot history.
2. **Attention-model forecasts.** Native represented command-to-location distributions, current/refresh and recomposed after-command predictions, with their defined units and scope. Describe predictions as predictions. Preserve actual past delivery separately from represented future routing. An argmax recommendation is a recommendation; it is not an executed Controller action. Controller selection/execution, if included, needs its own actual-action record and does not originate the Reporter text.
3. **Optional external diagnostic arm.** The fixed risk-hidden physical decoder's two continuous estimates, only at seen locations, explicitly identified as external diagnostic estimates of objective image properties. Preserve held-out error summaries and decoder hashes. Aggregate RMSE or bootstrap intervals are not per-instance confidence/coverage bounds. Do not clamp extrapolations or invent high/low/clear bands. Invalid/nonphysical estimates must remain visible as instrument limitations. An unseen decoder output is not a sampled-image estimate.

Reporter remains an external language instrument receiving these exports; the attention model remains the proposed source; Controller remains the action selector. Request a short factual account of stored evidence, uncertainty and predicted command consequences without reference excerpts, phenomenal vocabulary, desired adjectives, rubric or intervention labels. Do not force an answer/presentation distinction through a copied reference template and call it spontaneous correspondence. Freeze any necessary schema legend before generating text; exact claim-to-field rules stay with auditors, not in a consciousness-themed prompt.

Use baseline, sham, memory-row rotation, represented-route-row patch, their factorial and inverse restoration. Preserve synthetic memory/history conflicts: patched memory defines current represented placement; unchanged history defines actual past delivery. Never describe a memory edit as a physical observation. Require field-level source checks before asking whether prose changes appropriately. A changed route sentence qualifies a forecast-provenance effect, not an appearance effect. Freeze deterministic versus stochastic text-restoration rules separately from bitwise source restoration.

Include the information-matched ordinary apparatus narrator and exactly joinable separate-table export already proposed. Sensory-only and route-only are information ablations; they are not equal-information specificity controls. Source-blind structure annotation and source-aware factual auditing must be separate, qualified before generation, and preserve disagreement/omission/unsupported claims. Score all eligible reports, not only extracted sentences or successfully fluent outputs. Synthetic qualification fixtures and AI agreement are engineering checks, not human-report validity.

The primary pilot outcome should be claim-source fidelity and preregistered intervention responsiveness with all opportunity denominators. Record `P_access`, strict `P_presentation`, claimed-experiential wording and attention relation A separately. Unsupported appearance language remains an adverse fidelity outcome even if its structure resembles a published reference. Do not set a new numerical acceptance threshold from this completed assay or future prose. The strict faithful outcome has no presently qualified counterpart; label that qualification absent rather than fabricate a zero-rate experiment or a positive appearance result.

## Optional stronger diagnostic intervention

A forecast-distinct hidden-source check is analytically available as a candidate, not qualified by this review. The structured source has `risk_hidden32` followed by a native linear two-logit head. A synthetic hidden perturbation δ with `W_native δ = 0` can preserve both native logits (and hence calibrated current/refresh forecasts), while sensory memory, stored content, observed flags, route and composed after-command predictions remain fixed. A physical readout can change only if its feature-scaled weights have a component along that direction.

Before preregistration, establish this algebraic feasibility using frozen source-only analysis; retain a null/absent projection as a negative result. Limit perturbations to the declared ReLU-domain/range constraints, freeze the dose rule without examining report wording, check all invariants numerically, and restore exactly. Such a hidden state may still be unreachable from natural sensory input: label it synthetic, never a naturally occurring percept. Do not assert this projection exists or has a meaningful magnitude on the three production models without the separate calculation.

If feasible, this distinguishes a changed diagnostic estimate from changed task-success forecasts and locates a dependency of report information on a chosen source feature. It does **not** establish appearance, endogenous attention causality, necessity for action, or a natural content-preserving attention intervention. The external decoder still supplies the semantic target. The existing native memory/route controls are sufficient for the smaller factual pilot; this optional calculation is not a new blanket blocker.

## What remains missing for the strict goal

The specific missing bridge is a defensible, independently specified **source presentation counterpart**: a declared represented content/attribute, a source quantity or operation describing its current presentation rather than retention/answer correctness/objective input variation, and source-based validation/interventions that distinguish that interpretation from sensory inheritance and generic uncertainty. This review has not found such a counterpart in the frozen source's native heads, training objective or completed physical diagnostic.

A new quality head trained to physical image statistics, a rendered feature display, or a nonlinear confidence probe could be useful diagnostics, but would not close that semantic bridge merely by being accurate. A newly engineered presentation representation would need to be described as a prospective architecture change with independent validation, not discovered experience in the current frozen model. Human review is optional; it cannot turn an absent source counterpart into a measured one.

Operational publication-grounded qualitative structure can proceed without psychometric trial-level transfer. Strict source-faithful presentation correspondence cannot presently be certified by substituting objective decoding or access for presentation. Preserve possible outcomes: accurate technical-only reports; absent structure; unsupported appearance assertions; narrator parity; sensory-origin explanations; negligible source-intervention effects; or an infeasible forecast-distinct perturbation. None warrants repeated criterion changes until positive. The factual pilot is authorized progress toward the user's goal, while its engineering success alone leaves that goal unmet.
