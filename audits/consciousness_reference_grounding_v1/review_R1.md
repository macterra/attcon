# R1: primary-reference grounding review

2026-10-10. Fresh AI review of reference features; no target-output evaluation.

The selected literature supports a narrow candidate correspondence: specified visual content, a separately expressed quality or comparative attribute of its appearance, and separation of that appearance judgment from a discrimination answer. An attention-linked appearance contrast is a defensible additional hypothesis, with a contested causal interpretation. Recovery forecasts, temporal availability, internal-source attribution, and model ownership remain structural/source measures under these references. This is a proposal for prospective measurement, not an empirically validated composite or evidence that the proposed model experiences anything.

## What was verified

**Tagliabue et al. (2016), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572.** Publisher full text was accessible. The EEG procedure required a brightness discrimination followed by a separate PAS response, including when the participant did not see the stimulus. Methods describe four button-selected categories: “no experience of the stimulus,” “a brief glimpse,” “an almost clear experience,” and “a clear experience.” An end-of-session open-ended questionnaire checked category criteria, but I did not retrieve individual questionnaire transcripts. Accuracy increased across PAS categories; this association does not identify the two judgments. The authors distinguish PAS from response-accuracy confidence, but this experiment did not separately collect a confidence scale. Methods and behavioral results support a content/clarity/answer distinction, not calibrated machine recovery-to-clarity conversion. [Tagliabue et al., Methods: EEG Experiment; Behavioral Results; General Discussion](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full).

**Liu, Abrams and Carrasco (2009), DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02300.x.** Author-hosted full PDF was accessible. An RSVP task directed endogenous spatial attention independently of the contrast task. A key response encoded orientation and the location judged higher in contrast; a control reversed the contrast instruction. The authors report higher perceived contrast at the attended location and better orientation performance, interpreting the control as excluding simple cued-location choice bias. This supports testing attention-related, attribute-specific appearance reports, not equating numerical selection allocation with appearance. The measurements are constrained psychophysical responses rather than a free-prose report corpus. [Liu et al., pp. 355–360, Procedure, Results and Discussion](https://npal.psy.msu.edu/wp-content/uploads/Publications/Liu_2009_PsychSci.pdf).

**Anton-Erxleben, Abrams and Carrasco (2010), DOI 10.1167/10.11.6.** Author-lab-hosted journal PDF was accessible. The authors compared comparative and equality judgments using physical contrast increments and exogenous attention. They conclude both procedures detected apparent-contrast changes, while equality judgments had sensitivity and criterion limitations. Their discussion explicitly notes that asymmetric equality criteria can affect estimated subjective equality. This justifies preserving response-rule and criterion alternatives, including for seemingly direct appearance judgments. This study uses exogenous attention; it is not a replication of Liu's endogenous procedure. [Anton-Erxleben et al., pp. 1–3, 12–17](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/wp.nyu.edu/dist/7/32743/files/2025/03/Evaluating-comparative-and-equality-judgments-in-contrast-perception-Attention-alters-appearance.-Journal-of-Vision.pdf).

**Schneider (2011), PMID 22072727, DOI 10.1167/11.13.7.** I retrieved the primary PubMed abstract through the search index; direct page, DOI and an archived full-PDF opening failed. The abstract reports refitting the 2010 data with a response model accommodating low-contrast asymptotic responses, interpreting the effects as subjective-criterion changes. This is an abstract-level verification of the stated alternative, not an independent assessment of the fits. It directly contests the 2010 interpretation and does not itself settle the broader question. [Schneider, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22072727/).

PubMed/PMC direct opens sometimes returned empty pages, CAPTCHA, or internal errors; DOI/publisher openings for the two Journal of Vision papers also failed. Accessible author-hosted copies supplied the 2009 and 2010 text. No claim here relies on a secondary summary. Search results exposed unrelated sources, but these were not used as evidence. These retrieval limits matter most for the 2011 reanalysis.

The PAS labels above are authentic experimental response categories, not participant quotations or machine qualia labels. The brightness, contingent orientation/contrast, and equality tasks likewise provide documented human response formats. Their authenticity does not turn them into unrestricted first-person narratives or establish the proposed machine mapping.

## Proposed feature specification

The following is my synthesis and prospective design proposal. It is not a joint pattern directly measured by all four papers.

Use one annotation record per claim with exact quote, content/location, attribute, time or horizon, assertion/hedge, modality, and scope. Mark each feature `explicit`, `ambiguous`, or `absent`; retain scope ambiguities. Missing text never inherits a feature from an unseen source field. Keep a separate faithful-to-source audit; an accurate report of a mistaken representation is possible.

| Feature | Positive annotation criterion | Grounding status and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Identified content | Specific object/location and attribute; assertions and hedges retained | Content is needed to bind any appearance relation. Color and shape marginals do not supply joint identity certainty. |
| Content-specific clarity/appearance | A specified content has an expressed manner or apparent attribute/comparison; record graded level where stated | PAS supports graded clarity; contrast studies support attribute comparisons. These are different measures. Record display, sensor, represented availability, claimed felt appearance, or unclear scope. No borrowed PAS cutoff. |
| Selection | Named location/content is relatively prioritized | Attention manipulation motivates this functional field. A selection ranking does not establish perceptual prominence. |
| Prominence | Named content is described as standing out relative to named comparison | Retain as a bridge hypothesis. Generic salience is not the same feature as measured apparent contrast or PAS clarity. |
| Attention–appearance relation | Selection/attention and an appearance comparison are explicitly linked for the same content/attribute | Candidate extension motivated by Liu; interpretation contested by the 2010/2011 dispute. Preserve association versus forecast versus causal assertion. |
| Identity certainty | Explicit uncertainty about an attribute or alternatives | Useful separation in the rubric. These selected papers do not validate a separate machine identity-certainty scale. A probability list alone need not express certainty. |
| Answer confidence | Confidence in the correctness of a specified emitted/proposed answer | Keep distinct from clarity, identity, and willingness to answer. Supported as a conceptual boundary, not a separately validated endpoint in the selected experiments. |
| Recovery | Chance/ability to retrieve specified content at a stated horizon and readout | Structural functional measure only; neither contrast appearance nor PAS measures this forecast. |
| Temporal availability | Specified availability comparison across times/delays with observed/forecast status | Structural only under this source set. Experimental presentation timing and neural-component timing do not validate a persistence-report criterion. |
| Action/access/source attribution | Explicit action–availability relation and named referent, recording internal/external and decision role separately | Structural/source measure only. No own-model superiority or source-of-qualia attribution follows from these papers. |

The external bases for the appearance and attention rows are [Tagliabue et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00572/full) and [Liu et al.](https://npal.psy.msu.edu/wp-content/uploads/Publications/Liu_2009_PsychSci.pdf); the criterion caution comes from [Anton-Erxleben et al.](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/wp.nyu.edu/dist/7/32743/files/2025/03/Evaluating-comparative-and-equality-judgments-in-contrast-perception-Attention-alters-appearance.-Journal-of-Vision.pdf) and [Schneider's abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22072727/). Remaining rows are project annotation distinctions or explicit limits of transfer, not new human findings.

## Joint pattern and prospective measurement

Freeze a modest core: **content identity + content-specific appearance/clarity + a separately coded discrimination answer**, with no automatic conversion among their values. Absence of confidence is not failure; report its status separately. The identity of the appearance content and answer content must be linked explicitly, or the joint status is ambiguous. Grades and dissociations matter more than counting adjectives. The external evidence motivates distinguishing these channels, not demanding that every report claim an uncertain answer despite a clear appearance.

Freeze an additional attention extension: **relative selection + attribute-specific appearance comparison + explicit relation between them**, bound to the same object/location. Measure its frequency and fidelity separately from the core. Do not require it in episodes without a relevant comparison, or substitute allocation/recovery differences. Prominence-only wording receives prominence credit and no automatic apparent-contrast or clarity credit.

For accurate reports, source auditing must establish the factual support for each represented-content and functional relation; experiential wording can be recorded but its truth as felt appearance cannot be audited from a recovery field. If the architecture supplies only category, selection and recovery variables, a faithful technical report can score those structural features while lacking the appearance core. Adding an appearance descriptor to a readout would allow provenance auditing of that descriptor, but naming such a variable does not validate phenomenal clarity. Do not induce unsupported appearance prose merely to meet the composite.

Report explicit core/extension matches, partial matches, ambiguity, and source-faithful matches with their denominators. Predeclare eligibility from task design, not observed success; include omissions and unprompted assertions in auditing. Use lower/upper summaries treating ambiguous cases as nonmatches/possible matches. Keep whole episodes, model instances and generation runs as dependence units rather than treating all claims as independent observations.

Qualification should use held-out synthetic cases spanning all boundaries below, quote-first annotation, and a fixed adjudication rule, with disagreements archived. Predeclare reliability, acceptable unsupported-claim rates, meaningful effects, stopping and analysis rules before fresh target evaluation. This review certifies no numerical threshold, power, rater reliability or target pass rate. Synthetic agreement is annotation qualification, not validation against independent human reports.

## Synthetic annotation examples

Every example in this table is newly AI-authored for this review. None is a human transcript, observed target report, validated consciousness datum, or proposed Reporter training/prompt material. Provenance is the R1 annotation proposal; reference-inspired examples instantiate distinctions, not reproduce a study participant's words.

| Synthetic text | Annotation and boundary |
|---|---|
| “The patch at the left looks faint. I answer that it is lighter than the background.” | Content, appearance manner with claimed visual-appearance scope, and answer explicit; confidence absent. Core candidate only if both clauses bind the same patch and a separate factual audit passes. |
| “The patch looks clear, but I am unsure whether it is lighter or darker. I chose lighter and have little confidence that the answer is correct.” | Clarity, attribute uncertainty, emitted answer, answer confidence separately explicit. No inference that such a dissociation was measured in the selected experiments. |
| “The left patch has priority over the right; its stripes also look stronger in contrast than those on the right, and I associate that change with attending left.” | Selection, attribute-specific appearance comparison, and association explicit. Attention extension candidate; causal mechanism unproved. |
| “The left patch receives 70% of the allocation. Its recovery probability at delay 2 is 0.8.” | Selection and timed recovery explicit; appearance, prominence and answer confidence absent. Numerical values are synthetic. |
| “The left patch stands out more, although its contrast looks the same as the right patch.” | Prominence and appearance equality explicit and distinct. Selection absent. Does not pass a predeclared increased-contrast prediction. |
| “The image is clearer now.” | Presentation manner stated, but content, temporal reference and scope unresolved: ambiguous joint appearance/temporal features, not repaired from source state. |
| “Recovery is 0.9, so it feels vivid and my answer must be right.” | Recovery, experiential wording and response claim are textually present; the inference from recovery supports neither felt vividness nor correctness. Audit as unsupported unless separate evidence exists, without deleting the wording annotation. |
| “A camera selector prioritizes the left patch. Its displayed contrast appears stronger than the right. The diagnostic answer is lighter.” | An ordinary apparatus account can contain the same core/extension wording. Scope is display/apparatus unless additional text specifies otherwise. This overlap limits specificity and remains compatible with operational correspondence. |

These examples demonstrate why first-person grammar, consciousness terms, a recovery band, or a selection table cannot supply an appearance feature by themselves. The stronger specificity study should compare information-matched apparatus descriptions and ordinary diagnostic narrators with fixed instructions, covered fields and generation conditions. Measure overlap and any predeclared effect direction; do not presume that apparatus prose must fail.

## Claim levels and implementation decision

Operational correspondence would be graded evidence that fresh, faithful reports express this prospectively defined externally motivated pattern. It does not require beating every ordinary-description comparator. Comparator superiority is a separate stronger specificity outcome; parity reduces specificity without erasing measured correspondence. Neither outcome proves subjective experience or identifies a unique source of qualia.

Maintain three entities throughout: Reporter as instrumentation, Controller as action selector/executor, and the attention-control model as the proposed source. Audit which referent a report actually attributes access to, independent of internal/external location and task relevance. Nothing in these papers establishes that the proposed source must be necessary for task success, or that task necessity is a consciousness criterion.

I recommend adopting the narrow core and separately labelled attention extension for prospective annotation development, retaining recovery/temporal/source features as structural auxiliaries, and withholding any claim that this review has validated a machine consciousness measure.
