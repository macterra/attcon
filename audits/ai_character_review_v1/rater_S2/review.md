# S2 first-pass interpretation

All twelve reports have technical character and admit an ordinary technical interpretation of all their content. Content identity, selection contrast, recovery forecast, access attribution and temporal availability are explicit in every report. Identifications are separate color/shape assertions; joint certainty is not claimed. Recovery is forecast for positioned content over the specified delay, without implying answer correctness or observed decay.

Content prominence, identity certainty, presentation manner, response confidence and experiential wording are absent in all twelve texts. Selection and access ranks do not express salience. High or low accessibility describes functional recovery rather than faintness or vividness. Not-identified labels, null categories and absent fields report identification or record limitations; they do not explicitly compare possible identities or claim uncertainty about an answer. Forecast modality alone earns no certainty rating.

Action–access dependence is explicit in ten reports: current output lacks an identification that becomes available in a command-conditioned forecast. This is a modeled forecast association, not observed acquisition or a proven causal mechanism. C003 and C010 are ambiguous on this dimension. Their no-refresh retention forecasts state an intervention condition, but they offer no command-conditioned access forecast or refresh/no-refresh comparison. A broad reading includes this action-conditioned retention language; a narrower reading of dependence requires an expressly stated access difference attributable to action. Both readings are preserved rather than adjudicated.

Access is attributed to named nodes/buffers and output readouts. Decision-relevance and internal/external location are separately unspecified/ambiguous: a buffer or output-node identifier does not expressly establish a decision relation, and describing forecasts as internal does not clearly locate the accessed referents relative to a decision-maker. Identifiers and modeled forecasts are the explicit attribution cues recorded. Several reports additionally distinguish measured allocation/acquisition history, but those measurements do not establish current access. Presentation referent arrays are empty because presentation manner is absent.

C007 describes numeric decline at n0 p1/p2 while calling those trends steady. C008 gives distinct approximate recovery values at q9 p2 while calling its trend steady. Both still expressly describe temporal recovery forecasts. Qualitative steadiness is not numerical equality. Missing output recovery and selection forecasts remain missing report information.

These are blind wording annotations of development reports, with exact supporting quotations for every explicit or ambiguous dimension. No source condition, factual fidelity, prior verdict or desired outcome was inferred. The original first pass is retained without adjudication. Source-aware auditing cannot repair ambiguity in the blind text.

S2 is a same-project commissioned AI reviewer in a separate context, not an independent human rater. Shared training/model biases can correlate judgments. The protocol discloses twelve presentations of two underlying episodes, so counts cannot be treated as independent system replication. Procedural withholding is not secure blinding. These annotations establish neither consciousness, confirmation nor independent consciousness-reference validation.
