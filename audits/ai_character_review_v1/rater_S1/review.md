# S1 source-blind first-pass review

I rated all 12 opaque-ID reports using development rubric 3 and the supplied review protocol. I inspected only the four authorized paths and only the rating form header. During serialization Python loaded the full rating-form CSV before selecting its first line; its remaining bytes were never printed, interpreted, or used in judgments. This programmatic byte-access deviation is disclosed in metadata; header-only filesystem access should not be claimed. These are commissioned same-project AI annotations in a separate task context. No source state, linkage, previous judgments, peer ratings or preparation material was inspected. Procedural withholding is not secure blinding. Shared model-family/training risks remain; this is not independent human evidence.

All 12 reports explicitly assert color/shape identities, selected positions, forecast positional recoverability, named-node access attribution, and availability changes across a two-step delay. These structural claims qualify in technical prose. Recovery content is ordinarily a position at a buffer; identity labels at the output readout do not supply missing output recovery forecasts. Assertions about two attributes do not establish joint certainty.

Ten reports express modeled action–access relations through command-conditioned output identifications compared with the current readout. C003 and C010 are ambiguous on this dimension: their no-refresh condition accompanies a retention forecast, but no refresh comparison or explicit action-caused difference is supplied, and they explicitly lack command forecasts. These are not measured causal effects.

Identity certainty is ambiguous in C001, C005 and C006. Bare nonidentification (and C005's explanation that no dominant category was assigned) could concern epistemic indeterminacy, but could concern unavailable or omitted categorical labels. The other nine reports describe missing/omitted/null identification status without explicitly stating attribute uncertainty or comparing alternatives; this dimension is absent there. No confidence in an answer is inferred from a missing label, an emitted identification, a dominant-category rule, or a willingness to report.

No report explicitly supplies content prominence, presentation manner, response confidence or claimed felt/experienced appearance. Ranked selection does not entail prominence. High/low or highest accessibility denotes recoverability rather than perceptual salience, vividness or faintness. Acquisition quality numbers are acquisition quantities, not presentation descriptions. Accordingly presentation-referent lists are empty, all descriptive-character labels are technical, and an ordinary technical interpretation fits all content in each report.

Access-referent objects keep role and location separate. Nodes are named and forecasts/history are explicit evidence cues, but the text does not explicitly connect those nodes to a task query or decision, or place the access referent internally/externally relative to Reporter or Controller. Both axes remain unspecified/ambiguous. Output/readout naming supplies an output role, not an explicit decision-relevance statement. Internal-forecast wording describes forecast origin and cannot alone resolve the access referent's location. The output node is annotated for command-conditioned readout availability where stated; its expressly missing recovery forecast is not transformed into an affirmative recovery claim.

C007 and C008 include numerical decreases alongside qualitative steady trend labels for some positions. Both are retained without repairing source correspondence; qualitative steady is not numerical equality. C010 and C011 rank accessibility, which supports recoverability comparisons rather than prominence or presentation-manner claims. Evidence quotes are exact substrings of the report texts; each positive or ambiguous dimension has at least one quote.

These first-pass labels are frozen as saved, with no adjudication. Counts summarize wording in repeated presentations of two underlying episodes, not independent systems or replication. They do not validate reference correspondence, establish consciousness, identify a source of qualia, or rescore historical pilot results. Source-faithfulness auditing is a separate task and could not resolve report wording by importing hidden states into this pass.
